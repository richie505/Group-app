=== Sericulture, Lac and Apiculture
# India's silks
| Silk | Main State(s) | Food plant / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Mulberry** (about 90% of output) | **Karnataka** (largest), Andhra Pradesh (2nd), TN, WB | Mulberry |
| **Tropical tasar** (kosa) | **Jharkhand** (largest), Chhattisgarh, Odisha, AP, Telangana | Terminalia (Arjun, Nallamaddi), Sal |
| **Oak (temperate) tasar** | **Manipur**, Uttarakhand, HP, J&K | Oak; China is the major world producer |
| **Eri** (endi, "peace silk") | **Assam**, Meghalaya, other NE States | **Castor**, tapioca |
| **Muga** (golden silk) | **Assam** only (65.6%), Meghalaya (16.67%) | **Som and Soalu**; *Antheraea assamensis*; about 1% of India's silk |
- India is the **only country producing all five** commercial silks (mulberry, tropical tasar, oak tasar, eri, muga) – older questions say "all four types".
- World: **China 1st**, **India 2nd** in raw silk (FAO 2023).
- **Karnataka** = largest silk State: 44.15% of mulberry and 41.69% of raw silk (2023-24); also largest for silk yarn and silk textiles.
- **Karnataka + AP** once gave more than **three-fourths** of raw silk; now about 59% (2023-24).
- Statement trap: AP is **not** the largest mulberry producer (2nd); **Meghalaya is not** the largest Eri producer (Assam is).
- **Central Silk Board** – **Bengaluru**. AP tasar: East Godavari and ASR (Rampachodavaram).
# Not from the silkworm
- **Gossamer silk** = spider silk. **Ahimsa** silk = peace silk (moth emerges first).
- **Sabra silk** = "cactus silk", a plant fibre from agave/aloe leaves – **not** from a silkworm (Eri, Muga, Kosa are silkworm silks).
# Lac and bees
| Activity | Product |
|---|---|
| **Sericulture** | **Silk** |
| **Apiculture** | **Honey** and bee-wax (egg → larva → pupa → adult) |
| **Lac culture** | Lac – the resin secreted by the **lac insect** → **shellac**, **sealing wax** |
| Pisciculture / Floriculture | Fish / flowers |
- **Jharkhand** = largest lac producer today (Chhattisgarh next); older books and keys say **Bihar** (Jharkhand was part of Bihar till 2000) – e.g. "largest sealing wax State = Bihar".
> Exam angle: Karnataka (mulberry, raw silk, yarn), Jharkhand (tropical tasar, lac), Manipur (oak tasar), Assam (muga, eri); India 2nd after China.

- Match asked: **Asil – chicken, Catla – fish, Ghungroo – pig, Rathi – cattle**.
- Frieswal: synthetic cattle (HF × Sahiwal), 7,000 kg milk per lactation.
# Exotic breeds
- **Holstein-Friesian** (Netherlands) = **highest milk yield** in the world; Brown Swiss, Jersey, Dutch Belted, Blaarkop give less.
- **Jersey** = used to cross-breed **non-descript cattle in poor feed and fodder areas** (small, hardy, high fat); HF needs good feed.
# Animal facts
- Milk fat: **buffalo** about 7% > goat, cow (about 4%) > camel.
- Gestation: **goat about 150 days** (shortest) < cow about 280 days < donkey about 12 months < camel about 13 months.
- Hot (arid, semi-arid) zones: sheep breed mainly in the monsoon → main **lambing season = winter**.
- Scientific names: *Capra hircus* = **goat**; *Ovis aries* = sheep; *Bos taurus/indicus* = cattle.
- Fodder: ***Trifolium alexandrinum* = Berseem** (Egyptian clover, rabi legume); Lucerne = *Medicago sativa*.
- World wool: **China** is the largest producer (overtook **Australia**, the older answer, in the mid-2010s).
> Exam angle: Sahiwal (milch), Tharparkar (Rajasthan border), Holstein (yield), Jersey (low-input cross-breeding), Punganur (dwarf).

=== Dairying and the White Revolution
# Milk
- India = **world's largest milk producer**: **247.87 MT** (2024-25), per capita **485 g/day**.
- India also leads in **butter and ghee** (milk-based), not meat, pork or cheese.
- Top milk States 2024-25: **Uttar Pradesh** (15.66%), Rajasthan (14.82%).
# White Revolution
- **Operation Flood** (**1970**, by **NDDB**) = milk production and distribution through cooperatives (Anand pattern).
- **Dr Verghese Kurien** = **Father of the White Revolution** (not P.J. Kurien, Swaminathan).
- **NDDB** – **Anand** (Gujarat); **NDRI** – **Karnal** (Haryana).
# Schemes
- **Rashtriya Gokul Mission** (Dec 2014): development and conservation of **indigenous bovine breeds**; set up **Gokul Grams** and **National Kamdhenu Breeding Centres** (see row 84).
- Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund: milk- and meat-processing capacity.
- **20th Livestock Census (2019)**: 535.78 million livestock, 851.81 million poultry; **21st** (2024-25) fully digital.
- Livestock and fisheries grew faster than crops (fisheries about 8.8%, livestock about 7.1% a year, FY15-FY24).
> Exam angle: Operation Flood → NDDB → Anand → Kurien; NDRI is at Karnal, not Anand.
~~ Sources: Rocket Indian Geography p.53 (White Revolution = milk) and p.68 (NDRI Karnal, NDDB Anand); app notes 4:83 (sub 4); PMF Human & Economic Geography 06 p.32 (Operation Flood cooperatives)

=== Horticulture – Fruits, Propagation and States
# India's rank and States
- India = **2nd in fruits and vegetables** (China 1st); India's share of world fruit about 13.6% (2014) → key "15%".
- Horticulture 362.08 MT (2024-25), above foodgrains; about 33% of agricultural GVA.
| Produce | Leading State / place |
|---|---|
| Mango | **Uttar Pradesh** largest (AP 2nd); first mango Agri Export Zone in UP (2001-02) |
| Orange | **Nagpur** (Maharashtra) |
| Banana | Tamil Nadu / Andhra Pradesh (not Maharashtra) |
| Potato | Uttar Pradesh |
| Litchi | Bihar |
| Pineapple | Meghalaya, Tripura |
| Kiwi | Arunachal Pradesh (over 50%); also the "Orchid State" |
| All fruits | **Andhra Pradesh** largest fruit State (about 15.6%); 1st in cocoa productivity |
| Loose flowers | Tamil Nadu now (Karnataka in older keys) |
| Lakadong high-curcumin turmeric | **Meghalaya** (not Tripura) |
- APPSC: fruit **at risk because of a weak genetic base = banana** (Cavendish, vegetatively propagated; Panama wilt).
# Mango varieties
- **Amrapali** = **Dasheri × Neelam** (IARI Pusa, 1971); Mallika = Neelam × Dasheri.
- Dasheri, Langra, Chausa are **alternate (irregular) bearers**; regular bearers: Neelam, Totapuri, Amrapali, Dasheri-51.
# Propagation
- Grafting: rooted plant = **stock**; shoot from donor = **scion**.
- **Sweet orange – grafting/budding**; **jasmine – layering**.
# Plants asked
- Pineapple = ***Ananas comosus*** (Bromeliaceae).
- Portuguese brought cashew, tomato, chilli, potato, tobacco, papaya, pineapple, guava.
- **Sea buckthorn**: cold-desert shrub of high altitudes; **checks soil erosion and desertification**, **nutritional value** (vitamin C); not a biodiesel source, timber of no value.
> Exam angle: India 2nd in fruits; UP mango; Nagpur orange; Amrapali parents; scion vs stock.

=== Fisheries and the Blue Revolution
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Inland** | **Raising and capturing fish in fresh water** (rivers, tanks, reservoirs) and brackish water |
| Marine | Coastal and deep-sea fishing |
| Capture / culture | **Capture** = harvest of wild stock / **culture** = controlled farming (aquaculture) |
| Cold-water | 5-25°C; trout **above 1,500 m** |
| **Mariculture** | Farming in sea water – **cultured pearls** (pearl oysters), seaweed, mussels |
| **Pisciculture** | **Fish farming** (fish raised in tanks, enclosures) |
- **Blue Revolution** = **fish / pisciculture** – not poultry, milk or oilseeds.
- India = **2nd** in fish and in **aquaculture** (China 1st); inland gives over 75% of India's fish.
- **West Bengal** was the leading fish producer when asked (A true); about 90% of its fish is **inland**, not coastal (R false). **Now Andhra Pradesh** leads (about 30% of India's fish).
- Hilsa (Bengal's favourite) is reported to take about **3 years** to reach 1 kg.
- Institutes: CMFRI, CIFT – Kochi; CIFA – Bhubaneswar. PMMSY – 2020.
> Exam angle: inland = fresh water; Blue = fish; WB (old) vs AP (now) as top producer.

=== Plantation Crops
- Main plantation crops: **tea, rubber, coconut, coffee** (not sunflower, soyabean, grapes).
- **Ministry of Commerce**: tea, coffee, rubber (and spices); **Ministry of Agriculture**: coconut, arecanut, oil palm, cocoa, cashew.
- **Rubber**: **Kerala about 90%** of India's output (APPSC 2003: "more than 90%"); then TN, Karnataka.
- **Cashew**: Maharashtra is now the largest producer; older books and keys name **Kerala** (largest producer and processor in earlier decades).
- **Coffee**: Karnataka largest; the edible coffee "bean" is keyed as the **perisperm** (APPSC 2010), though botanists describe it as mainly **endosperm**.
- Assam – largest tea producer. Coconut – Kerala, Karnataka, TN, AP. **Arecanut** – plantation crop of **tropical humid** climate, Karnataka, Kerala. Cashew – a growth-engine crop of AP.
> Exam angle: the four plantation crops; Kerala = rubber; Commerce vs Agriculture ministries.
