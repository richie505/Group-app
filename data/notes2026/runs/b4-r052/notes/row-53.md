=== AP's Share and Rank in India
- AP = **"Ratna Garbha"** – the treasure house of minerals.
- **IMYB (2017 state review):** AP is the **sole producer of apatite** in India; leading producer of **barytes**, ball clay, dolomite, garnet, laterite, **limestone**, quartz, silica sand, vermiculite.
- AP's share of India's **resources**: **92% barytes**, 40% calcite, **41% mica**, **31% kyanite** and garnet, 19% titanium minerals, 16% bauxite, 15% dolomite, 13% sillimanite, 12% vermiculite and limestone.
| Mineral | AP's share / rank |
|---|---|
| **Barytes** | **India's top producer**; **~92–93% of India's reserves**; nearly **one-quarter of world output**; a minor mineral since 2015 |
| **Mica** | **First** in India – reserves **40.5% (~41%)**, production **70.2%**; Rajasthan 2nd; Jharkhand, Rajasthan also produce, **Goa does not** |
| **Heavy-mineral beach sands** | ~327 Mt of India's 1,078 Mt = **30.4%**; AP first in **monazite** |
| Limestone | production: **Rajasthan > MP > AP (third)**; largest reserves: Karnataka |
| Diamonds (NMI) | **18,22,955 carats (18.23 lakh)** of India's 319.22 lakh |
| Kyanite | ~**30–31%** of India (NMI 2020); **tin** reserves are in **Chhattisgarh**, not AP |
| Gold (IMYB 2021, 1.4.2020) | **47.17 tonnes** resources; Chigargunta–Bisanattam (Chittoor, Kolar schist belt) reports handed to AP after the **MMDR Amendment 2015** |
| **Asbestos** | AP produces chrysotile (Kadapa) but holds only a few % of reserves – **Rajasthan 59%, Karnataka 36%** of India's asbestos resources |
- Barytes production share: keys give **92%** – strictly the IMYB 92% is AP's share of **resources**; nearly all of India's barytes output comes from Mangampet.
- After beach sands, **bauxite** has the largest share of AP's major-mineral resources.
- **APMDC** (AP Mineral Development Corporation) incorporated **1961**.
- World mica (2022): **China first**, Madagascar second.

=== Rock Systems and Basins of AP
| System | Nature | Region | Minerals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Archaean | oldest; **no fossils**; ~2/3 gneiss | Srikakulam, parts of Eluru | iron, manganese, copper, lead, gold |
| Dharwar | metamorphosed gneiss and schists | schist belts of **Nellore (mica) and Chittoor–Anantapur (gold)**; NextGen also lists Vizianagaram–Visakhapatnam–East Godavari | mica, gold, crude iron |
| **Cuddapah (Kadapa)** | **Proterozoic, ~1.6 billion years**; crescent basin between the arching **Nallamala–Velikonda branches of the Eastern Ghats** | **YSR Kadapa, Nandyal, Kurnool**, Prakasam | barytes, asbestos, steatite, limestone, uranium, diamonds, copper, cobalt, nickel |
| Kurnool | sandstone, shale, limestone | Kurnool region | **glass sand, ornamental stone; no metallic minerals** |
| Gondwana | coal-bearing | Godavari valley | **coal** |
| Eastern Ghats (khondalites, charnockites) | Eastern Ghats Mobile Belt | Visakhapatnam Agency | **bauxite, manganese, graphite** |
| Deccan Trap | Cretaceous lava through **fissures**; **basalt** | **Prakasam, Palnadu, Guntur** | basalt; black cotton soil |
| **Pleistocene / coastal** | recent coastal sediments | **Srikakulam to Nellore** – all coastal districts | **oil and natural gas** (KG basin) as keyed |
- **Kadapa basin** = the **mineral-rich** and **largest** sedimentary basin and the **oldest basin of AP** (APPSC Group-II 2019); Nagarjuna hill and Sanganakallu are sites, not basins – Sanganakallu (Neolithic) is in Ballari, Karnataka.
- **⚠ Dispute:** keys put AP's oil and gas in the "**Pleistocene**" system; strictly the KG-basin hydrocarbons lie in **Cretaceous–Tertiary marine sediments** beneath the Pleistocene coastal cover.
- Dharwar items: keys give **Chittoor and Nellore** (APPSC Group-I).

=== Gold, Uranium and Beach Sands
# Gold
- **Swarnagiri (Jonnagiri)** – **India's first (and largest) private-sector gold mine** after Independence; **Tuggali mandal, Kurnool**; **GeoMysore Services**, **30-year lease**; ~1,000 kg a year; opened 2026.
- **Ramagiri (Anantapur)** – old gold field in the Dharwar greenstone belt; gold-bearing quartz in Anantapur, Chittoor, Kurnool.
- India's **oldest gold field = Kolar** (Karnataka).
# Uranium
- **Tummalapalle (YSR Kadapa)**, **Kadapa basin**: principal AP uranium deposit, **low-grade** ore; **2011**: huge reserves confirmed – among the world's top 20; mine and plant working since **2012**.
- Jaduguda, Narwapahar = Jharkhand; **radium** is extracted from **pitchblende**.
# Beach sands and rare earths
- Ilmenite, rutile, zircon, garnet, **sillimanite**, **monazite → thorium**; along the coast from **Srikakulam to Nellore** (Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Godavari, Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam); **Bhavanapadu (Srikakulam)**; monazite at Bheemunipatnam.
- **Kurnool** is inland – **no beach sands**.
- NGRI found rare earth elements in syenite rocks of **Anantapur** (2023); critical-mineral mapping through **APMDC** (2026).

=== Oil and Gas – Krishna-Godavari Basin
- AP's oil and gas basin = **Krishna-Godavari (KG) basin**, onshore (Konaseema, Godavari delta) and offshore; **Barmer–Sanchore** is in Rajasthan.
| Field / unit | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Tatipaka** | **ONGC mini-refinery** (Konaseema) – an oil refinery of India |
| **Ravva** | shallow offshore; found by **ONGC (1987)**; JV **Cairn India (operator since 1996), ONGC, Videocon Petroleum, Ravva Oil** |
| KG-D6 | **Reliance Industries** (with BP) deepwater gas |
| Deen Dayal West | GSPC block; **ONGC bought 80%** and operatorship (2017) |
| HPCL Visakhapatnam | AP's main refinery |
- **Pasarlapudi blowout** (ONGC well near Amalapuram): began **8 January 1995**, burned about 65 days.
- Underground coal gasification: Reliance near **Recherla and Chintalapudi, Eluru**.
- National: gas share target **15% by 2030**; **JHBDPL = Pradhan Mantri Urja Ganga**.

=== Water, Marine and Forest Resources
- Rivers: Godavari, Krishna, Pennar; north – **Vamsadhara and Nagavali**.
- Coastline **1,053.07 km** (revised; **third** after Gujarat and Tamil Nadu; old figure 974 km, second); continental shelf ~**33,227 sq km**.
- Fishing harbours: new at **Juvvaladinne (Nellore)** and Uppada; salt hub **Naupada (Srikakulam)**; **Gujarat** leads India's salt.
- Forest cover **30,084.96 sq km = 18.47%** of the State (ISFR 2023; AP Survey table rounds to 18.46%); ⚠ Trap: **23.59%** (38,436.58 sq km) = forest cover **+ 8,351.62 sq km scrub**, the "forest area" figure in the AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25; **red sanders** endemic to **Seshachalam–Palakonda–Lankamalla** hills; **GCC** HQ **Visakhapatnam**.
