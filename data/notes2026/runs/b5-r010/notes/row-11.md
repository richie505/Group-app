# S. Chandrasekhar
- **Chandrasekhar limit:** max mass of a stable **white dwarf ≈ 1.44 solar masses**; heavier stars → **neutron star or black hole** – hence exam books' "**principle of the black hole**".

=== Nobel Prize Basics
- **Alfred Nobel** (Swedish chemist) invented **dynamite (1867)** – not cosmic rays (Victor Hess), dynamo, penicillin, typewriter or gramophone.
- **Will 1895**; Nobel Foundation **1900**; **first prizes 1901**; instituted by **Sweden** (Peace Prize given in Norway).
- **Original five:** Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace. **No Nobel for mathematics.**
- **Economics:** set up by **Sveriges Riksbank (Sweden's central bank)** in **1968**, first given **1969** – not in the will.
- **How many each year:** **six** (books and keys); strictly five Nobel Prizes + the Riksbank memorial prize.
- Laureate status cannot be transferred, shared or withdrawn; only the medal can change hands.
- **RDX** (cyclonite, patented **1898**) – **Georg Friedrich Henning**, Germany, not Nobel.

=== Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize and Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar
# Bhatnagar Prize
- **S.S. Bhatnagar:** "Father of Research Laboratories"; **founder-director and first DG of CSIR**.
- **Prize:** by **CSIR since 1958** – India's best-known **science and technology** prize; age limit **45**; **7 disciplines** (biology, chemistry, earth/atmosphere/ocean/planetary, engineering, mathematics, medicine, physics – **not political science**).
- **2010 awardees:** included **Sanjeev Galande**. **Madhav Gadgil** (died Jan 2026) – People's Biodiversity Registers, **Gadgil Commission 2010** (Western Ghats).
# Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar (RVP)
- **Instituted 2023**, **first awarded Aug 2024** (exam key: **2024**); highest S&T honour; **13 domains**; replaced 300-odd ministry awards; medal + citation, no cash.
| Category | Recognises |
|---|---|
| **Vigyan Ratna** | Lifetime – first **G. Padmanabhan (2024)**; J.V. Narlikar (posthumous, 2026 list) |
| Vigyan Shri | Distinguished contribution |
| **Vigyan Yuva-SSB** | Young scientists **up to 45** (Bhatnagar Prize absorbed) |
| Vigyan Team | **3 or more** researchers (first: **Chandrayaan-3 team**) |
- **IYBA (DBT, 2005):** Innovative Young Biotechnologist Award – young researchers in frontier biotechnology.
- **Padma 2025:** 139 – 7 Vibhushan, 19 Bhushan, 113 Shri; **Vinod Dham** ("Father of the Pentium Chip") Padma Bhushan 2025.
