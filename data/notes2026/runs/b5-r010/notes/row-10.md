=== Vikram Sarabhai and the Space Pioneers
# Vikram Sarabhai (1919-1971)
- **Title:** **Father of the Indian Space Programme** (Jharkhand PCS 2021; IAS 2007 match).
- **Institutions:** **PRL Ahmedabad (1947)**; **INCOSPAR (1962)** → **ISRO (1969)**; **TERLS, Thumba (1962)**; **Community Science Centre (1965)**.
- **Posts:** **first ISRO Chairman** till his death (**1971**); **AEC Chairman 1966** (after Bhabha).
- **Awards:** Bhatnagar 1962, Padma Bhushan 1966, **Padma Vibhushan (posthumous) 1972**.
- **Named after him:** **Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC)** = TERLS renamed, **Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala** (asked ×2); Chandrayaan-2 lander **"Vikram"**; **COSPAR Vikram Sarabhai Medal** (1990, COSPAR + ISRO, space scientists of **developing countries**).
- **2026:** **Annapurni Subramaniam** won the COSPAR Sarabhai Medal at the 46th COSPAR Assembly, Florence – **first Indian woman**, fourth Indian (she had the RVP Vigyan Shri 2024).

=== A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
- **Title:** **"Missile Man of India"**; **11th President (2002-07)**.
- **Space:** project director of **SLV-3**, which put **Rohini** in orbit (**1980**).
- **Missiles:** chief of **IGMDP (1983)** – Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Nag, Trishul.
- **Nuclear:** **Pokhran-II (1998)** with Anil Kakodkar. **Bharat Ratna 1997**.
- **Fighter flight:** **Sukhoi-30 MKI**, **8 June 2006**, Pune (co-pilot Wg Cdr Ajay Rathore) – first Indian Head of State in a fighter. **Pratibha Patil** flew a Sukhoi-30 on 25 Nov 2009 – first Indian woman in a fighter.
- **Ideas and books:** **PURA** – Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas (APPSC G2 Mains 2017); **Wings of Fire (1999**, with Arun Tiwari), **Ignited Minds**, **Indomitable Spirit**.

=== Swaminathan and C.N.R. Rao
# M.S. Swaminathan (1925-2023)
- **Title:** **Father of India's Green Revolution** – HYV dwarf **wheat and rice**, with **Norman Borlaug**; later the **"Evergreen Revolution"**.
- **UNEP:** **"Father of Economic Ecology"** (UPPSC 2007).
- **Honours:** **first World Food Prize (1987)**; Ramon Magsaysay 1971; **Bharat Ratna 2024 (posthumous)**.
- **Chaired:** **National Commission on Farmers** (18 Nov **2004**) – "Swaminathan report"; President, **Pugwash Conferences (2002-07)**.
# C.N.R. Rao
- **Field:** foremost **solid-state and materials chemist**; headed the **Scientific Advisory Council to the PM**.
- **Bharat Ratna:** named **Nov 2013** with Sachin Tendulkar, conferred **2014** (asked as "for the year 2013").
