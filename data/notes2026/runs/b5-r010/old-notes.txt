[10:0:0] - **Father of India's space programme**. Founded **PRL Ahmedabad (1947)**, **TERLS** near Thiruvananthapuram (**1962**), **Community Science Centre (1965)**.

## SUB 0 | Nobel Prize Basics
[11:0:0] - **Alfred Nobel** (Swedish) invented **dynamite (1867)**; will **1895**; Nobel Foundation **1900**; not cosmic rays (Victor Hess) ⚠ APPSC key (2010) gave cosmic rays; first prizes **1901**.
[11:0:1] - Original five: Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace.
