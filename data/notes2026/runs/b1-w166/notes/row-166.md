=== Four Anglo-Mysore Wars at a Glance
| War | Years | Mysore | British head | Treaty / result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | 1767-69 | Haidar Ali | **Harry Verelst** (Governor of Bengal) | Haidar before Madras → **Treaty of Madras, 4 April 1769** (mutual restitution, help against third party) |
| **Second** | 1780-84 | Haidar (died **7 Dec 1782**), then Tipu | **Warren Hastings** | Pollilur, Porto Novo; **Treaty of Mangalore (March 1784)** with **Tipu** – mutual restitution |
| **Third** | 1790-92 (PMF 1789-92) | Tipu | **Lord Cornwallis** (led in person) | **Treaty of Seringapatam (1792)** with Tipu – ended Tipu's supremacy in the south |
| **Fourth** | 1799 | Tipu | **Lord Wellesley** | Seringapatam fell; **Tipu killed 4 May 1799** |
- **Correct pairs:** Fourth war – Tipu defeated and killed; First – Haidar **not** defeated; Second – inconclusive; Third – Tipu **lost**.
- **Dates (match):** death of Haidar **1782**; Treaty of Mangalore **1784**; Treaty of Srirangapatnam **1792**; death of Tipu **1799**.
> Madras 1769 = Haidar; Mangalore 1784 and Seringapatam 1792 = Tipu.

=== Tipu Sultan – Capital, Embassies, Jacobin Club
- **Capital: Srirangapatna (Seringapatam)**; born 1750; **"Tiger of Mysore"**; also **Padshah** (1787) and **"Citizen Tipu"** – "Lion of Mysore" is not his title.
- **Embassies on modern lines:** **France** (three ambassadors in Paris, 1788), **Ottoman Empire**, Oman, Persia, **Afghanistan (Zaman Shah)**, Arabia, Mauritius – not Haidar, Mir Qasim or Shah Alam II.
- **Navy:** Board of Admiralty (1796), dockyards at Mangalore, Wajedabad, Molidabad.
- **Jacobin Club** at Seringapatam (**1797**); he became **Citizen Tipu** and **planted the Tree of Liberty**.
- **Reforms:** new calendar, coinage, weights and measures; sericulture; **war rockets** (pioneer – he did **not** discontinue them).
| Monument (Srirangapatna) | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Gumbaz** | Mausoleum of Haidar Ali and Tipu |
| Daria Daulat Bagh | Summer palace with war murals |

=== Second War Battles – Pollilur and Porto Novo
| Battle | Date | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Arcot | 1780 | Captured by Haidar |
| **Pollilur** | **10 Sept 1780** | **Haidar Ali and Tipu** destroyed **Col. William Baillie**'s British force (Spectrum misprints 1781) |
| **Porto Novo** | **1 July 1781** | **Sir Eyre Coote** defeated Haidar – reversed Pollilur, saved Madras |
- Haidar's anti-English alliance with the **Nizam and the Marathas**; British detached them – the Nizam bought off (Guntur), the Marathas by the **Treaty of Salbai (1782)**.
- Coote came from Bengal with men and money sent by Hastings; **Thomas Munro** served under **Hector Munro** and **Eyre Coote**.

=== Haidar Ali (1761-82)
- Rose in the **Wodeyar** army under ministers **Nanjaraj** (sarvadhikari) and **Devaraj** (dalwai), who had made **Chikka Krishnaraja Wodeyar II** a puppet.
- **Faujdar of Dindigul (1755)** under **Nanjaraja**; set up a **modern arsenal at Dindigul with French help**; trained troops on Western lines; de facto ruler **1761**.
- **First war diplomacy:** paid the **Marathas** to stay neutral and **won over the Nizam**; appeared before Madras → humiliating **Treaty of Madras (1769)** – the **first South Indian ruler to defeat British armies**.
- Defeated by Peshwa **Madhav Rao** (1764, 1766, 1771); the English did not help him in 1771 → Second war.
- **Velu Nachiyar** of Sivaganga recovered her kingdom with Haidar's help – the first queen to fight the British.

=== Third War and the Treaty of Seringapatam (1792)
- Cause: Tipu attacked **Travancore** (1789/90), a Company ally; Tipu beat **General Meadows (1790)**; **Cornwallis** took **Bangalore (March 1791)** via Ambur and Vellore.
- **Both the Nizam and the Marathas** joined the British (the grand alliance) – only in the **Third** war.
- **Treaty of Seringapatam** – with **Tipu Sultan** (not Haidar): about **half his territory** ceded, indemnity **Rs 3 crore** (330 lakh), **two sons as hostages**.
| Gainer | Share |
|---|---|
| **British** | **Malabar, Coorg** (protectorate), **Dindigul, Bara Mahal** – Ryotwari began here (**Read and Munro**) |
| **Nizam** | Krishna to beyond the Pennar – **Bellary, Gurramkonda, Gandikota, Kambham, Kadapa** |
| **Marathas** | Around the **Tungabhadra** |

=== Fourth War (1799) and the Settlement of Mysore
- Tipu refused the **Subsidiary Alliance**; **Wellesley** declared war (17 April – 4 May 1799); led by **General George Harris**, General Stuart and **Arthur Wellesley**.
- **Nizam** fought as a subsidiary ally (since 1798); the **Marathas** are listed as allies by NextGen, PMF and Spectrum, but in fact gave **no active help** and refused their share.
- Wodeyars restored: boy ruler **Krishnaraja Wodeyar III** under a subsidiary alliance; Nizam got **Gooty and Gurramkonda**.
- The Nizam's Mysore lands were handed to the Company in **1800** = **Ceded Districts** (**Kadapa, Kurnool, Anantapur, Bellary**).
- Removed the French-backed threat; brought Mysore and Rayalaseema under the Company; Bentinck took over Mysore 1831, Ripon restored it 1881.
