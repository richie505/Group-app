=== Bahmani Kingdom – Founder and Capitals
- **Founded:** **1347** (14th century) by **Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah** – **Hasan Gangu / Zafar Khan** (UPPSC 1995, 2005; UKPSC 2002; State PSC) – not Firuz Shah, Mahmud Gawan, Malik Ambar or Mujahid Shah.
- **First capital:** **Gulbarga**, renamed **Hasanabad** (CGPSC 2014) – not Bidar or Daulatabad.
- **Four regions:** **Gulbarga, Daulatabad, Berar, Bidar** (Golconda was not one).
- **Andhra link:** **Kapaya Nayaka** helped Hasan Gangu found it.
- **Bidar:** **Ahmad Shah I** shifted the capital from Gulbarga to Bidar (APPSC G2 2003).
- **Mahmud Gawan:** minister of **Muhammad III**; kingdom largest under him; **executed in 1481** – the kingdom then broke into five sultanates.
- **⚠ Dates:** **1336** = Vijayanagara; **1347** = Bahmani.
~~ Sources: NextGen qutabshahis p.1; NextGen post kakatiyas p.4; app notes 1:100 (sub 0)

=== Krishnadevaraya and Prataparudra Gajapati
- **Defeated Gajapati:** **Prataparudra Gajapati** (APPSC PYQ) – Kalinga campaign **1513–1519**.
- **Order of campaign:** 1. **Udayagiri** (Nellore) – fell after an **18-month siege**; 2. Kandukuru, Vinukonda, Addanki, **Kondaveedu**; 3. **Kondapalli**, Bejawada; 4. march to **Cuttack**.
- **Victory pillars:** at **Potnuru (Potnur)** and **Simhachalam** (APPSC G2-B 1991) – not Penugonda, Lepakshi or Hampi.
- **Peace (c. 1518–19):** Prataparudra's daughter **Annapurna Devi** (Bhadradevi / Tukkadevi) married Krishnadevaraya; **lands north of the Krishna returned** to the Gajapati.
- **⚠ Not a term:** **Cuttack and the Gajapati heartland were NOT annexed**.
- **Earlier:** **Saluva Narasimha** drove the Gajapatis out of south-eastern Andhra by **c. 1470** and occupied land up to the Godavari.
~~ Sources: NextGen vijayanagara kingdom p.19-20; app notes 1:100 (sub 5, 7)

=== Gajapati Rulers in Andhra
- **Title:** **Gajapati = "Lord of Elephants"** – taken by the Suryavamsi rulers of Orissa (UPPSC 2016); capital **Cuttack**; rule **c. 1434–1541**.
- **Order of Andhra campaigns:** **Kapilendra → Purushottama → Prataparudra**.
| Ruler | Andhra campaign |
|---|---|
| **Kapilendra (Kapileswara)** – founder | Attacked **Tiran Andhra (1448)** (**Penugonda inscription**); annexed **Rajamahendravaram** after 1446, ending the Rajahmundry Reddis; took **Kondaveedu c. 1450** |
| **Hamvira** (Kapileswara's son) | Defeated the Bahmani army attacking Devarakonda; **1471** – Bahmani **puppet** |
| **Purushottama** | Won the throne after a struggle; **refused to help Muhammad III** |
| **Prataparudra** | Lost **Udayagiri, Kondaveedu, Kondapalli** to **Krishnadevaraya** |

=== Gajapati Administration in Andhra
| Officer / unit | Function |
|---|---|
| **Naidu** | **Revenue officers in the southern (Telugu) regions** (APPSC GS 2025 key) |
| Bisoyi (Visayi) | Officer of a vishaya in Orissa proper |
| **Nayakas and samantas** | Collected revenue and kept order |
| **Dandapata** | Large province (whole districts) with a **standing army** |
- **⚠ Disputed:** the Draughtsman paper's printed key gave **Bisayee** for the southern revenue officer; the APPSC GS 2025 key and the app's notes give **Naidu**.
- **Social order:** the **Reddi-era social order was kept**, not abolished.
