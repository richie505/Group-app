=== Fertilisers – Urea, NBS, Neem Coating, Nano and DBT
# Urea and the subsidy regime
- **Urea:** white, highly water-soluble, **46% nitrogen** (closest exam option: 47%); loses N quickly by **volatilisation and leaching**, hence slow-release forms (**urea briquettes**, neem coating).
- **Urea = most subsidised fertiliser**; stays under **statutory price control** (fixed MRP); **not** covered by NBS. Budget 2026-27: urea subsidy Rs 1,16,805 crore vs NBS Rs 54,000 crore.
- **Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS), 2010:** the policy now followed for **P&K fertilisers** – a **fixed per-kg subsidy on N, P, K and S**, announced yearly; covers **22 grades** of decontrolled P&K; **manufacturers set the MRPs**.
- Fertiliser subsidy = **second largest subsidy after food**; Budget 2026-27 **Rs 1.71 lakh crore** (RE 2025-26: Rs 1.86 lakh crore).
- India: **2nd largest consumer, 3rd largest producer** of fertilisers. National Investment Policy for Urea-2026 adds **10 MT** urea capacity.
| Reform | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **NBS** | **2010** | Fixed per-nutrient subsidy on P&K (and S) |
| **Neem-coated urea** | Notified May 2015; all domestic urea from 1 Sep 2015, **imported from 1 Dec 2015** | Neem coating **slows the release/dissolution of urea (nitrogen)** in soil and stops diversion of farm urea to industry |
| **DBT in fertilisers** | **Pilot from October 2016** | Subsidy paid to **fertiliser companies after sale at PoS devices** (Aadhaar-linked), not to farmers |
| **PM-PRANAM** | Approved 28 June 2023 | States keep **50%** of the subsidy saved by cutting chemical fertiliser use |
| **Nano DAP (liquid)** | Launched by **Amit Shah, April 2023** | IFFCO; **8% N, 16% P** |
- **IFFCO Nano Urea:** one **500 ml bottle = one 45 kg bag**; foliar spray.
# Balance and efficiency
- **N:P:K = 10.9 : 4.1 : 1 (2023-24)** vs ideal **4:2:1** – the urea-centred subsidy **distorts the NPK ratio**.
- **Balanced fertiliser use** = nutrients in the right proportion as per soil test → **corrects all deficiencies** (not just "adds NPK").
- **Efficient fertiliser use** = **more crop per kg of fertiliser**; the grain response per kg NPK fell from 13.4 kg (1970) to 3.7 kg (2005).
- **Indiscriminate use of urea** is the main human disturbance of the **nitrogen cycle** (nitrate leaching, N2O emission).
# Fertiliser types and use by State
- Nitrogenous: **ammonium sulphate**, ammonium nitrate, **urea**; phosphatic: **ammonium phosphate (DAP/MAP)**, superphosphate.
- **Largest consumer by quantity: Uttar Pradesh**; in 2009 the top two were **UP and (undivided) Andhra Pradesh**; Punjab is high per hectare.

=== Seeds – Seed Village, SRR, GM Crops and Seed Systems
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Seed Replacement Rate** | % of a crop's area sown with certified/quality seed (not farm-saved seed) |
| Varietal Replacement Rate | % of area under varieties released in the last **3-5 years** |
| **Seed Village** | Farmers given **foundation seed** and **trained in quality seed production** → produce certified seed locally and make it available to others **at the right time and affordable cost** |
- **Seed Village Programme:** ensures timely supply of quality certified seed of notified varieties at farmers' places at affordable prices and quick multiplication of new varieties in the mandal/district; **6.85 lakh seed villages** under the Sub-Mission on Seeds and Planting Materials (ES 2025-26).
- **SRR constraint (UPSC 2014):** a **demand-supply gap in quality seed of low-value, high-volume crops**; India **has** a National Seed Policy (2002) and private firms **do** supply vegetable/horticulture seed.
- Seed accounts for **20-25%** of productivity; problems: high cost of hybrids for small farmers, shortage of quality seed, unresolved GM seed issues.
- **SATHI portal** (Seed Traceability, Authentication and Holistic Inventory): **Phase 1 launched 19 April 2023 by Narendra Singh Tomar** (Union Agriculture Minister).
- **Indian Seed Congress 2017** (Kolkata) theme: **"Seed of Joy"**.
- ICAR **2019-21: 562** new HYVs; **3,236 HYVs since 2014**; Jan 2026: **184 varieties of 25 crops**.
- **Genome-edited rice (May 2025):** **DRR Dhan 100 Kamala** (from **Samba Mahsuri**) + **Pusa DST Rice 1** (from **MTU 1010**); SDN-1 edits **carry no foreign DNA** → outside GMO rules.
| Act / body | Provision |
|---|---|
| Seeds Act 1966 | Quality of notified seeds |
| PPV&FR Act 2001 | Farmers may save, use, exchange and **sell farm-saved seed only unbranded** |
| **GEAC** | Approves GM crops; Rules 1989 under **EPA 1986**; MoEFCC; cleared **GM mustard DMH-11 in 2022** |
- **GM crops with built-in toxins (Bt):** risk of pest resistance and of **encouraging more use of agricultural chemicals**; **Bt cotton** is GM, not a green manure.

=== Soil Health Card, Manures and Organic Farming
- **Soil Health Card Scheme:** launched **19 February 2015, Suratgarh (Rajasthan)**; part of NMSA (Soil Health Management); card shows nutrient status and fertiliser advice.
- **Interval:** the 2015 scheme said a card **once in every 3 years** (AEE 2016 key); in practice cards are now issued **free every two years** (cycles 2015-17, 2017-19).
- Impact: fertiliser use cut **8-10%**; over **25 crore** cards issued.
- **Glyricidia = green leaf manure**; green manure crops: **sunn hemp, dhaincha, green gram**. **Vermicompost:** earthworms turn organic waste into manure.
- **Organic farming = eco-friendly:** compost, green manure, bone meal; crop rotation, companion planting (HYV cultivation, glass houses and shifting cultivation are not).
| Scheme | Year | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| NPOP | 2001 | Commerce Ministry / **APEDA** certification |
| PKVY | 2015 | Cluster of **50 farmers / 50 acres**; certified by **PGS-India or NPOP** |
| National Mission on Natural Farming | **Nov 2024** | Stand-alone mission |
| NMSA | – | Merged into **PM-RKVY** from 2022-23; agroforestry "**Har Medh Par Ped**" |
- **Zero Budget Natural Farming:** **Subhash Palekar**.

=== Mechanisation and Pesticides
- Private investment in agriculture goes mostly to **labour-saving mechanisation** – a response to **rising wages and a tighter labour market** (migration, non-farm jobs).
- Mechanisation level: India **40-45%** (below 50% vs >90% in developed countries).
- **Zero Till Seed-cum-Fertiliser Drill:** developed at **G.B. Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar**.
- **Cage wheel:** fitted to tractors/power tillers for puddling in **wetland (paddy)** fields.
- **SMAM** (Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation): subsidises machines, **custom hiring centres**, farm machinery banks; 25,689 CHCs (2014-15 to 2025-26).
- **Namo Drone Didi:** drones to **women SHGs**; **Rs 1,261 crore** (2023-24 to 2025-26); target **15,000** drones; **80%** central assistance up to Rs 8 lakh.
# Pesticides
- **Chemical pesticides:** advantage = **high efficacy**, quick action; drawbacks = residues, harm to friendly insects, resistance.
- **Biological control** (natural enemies, bio-pesticides such as **neem-based** products): **low cost**, eco-friendly.
- **IPM:** biological, cultural and need-based chemical control.
| Law / event | Provision |
|---|---|
| Insecticides Act 1968 + Rules 1971 | **Central Insecticides Board** and Registration Committee |
| Draft Pesticides Management Bill 2025 | Replaces the 1968 Act |
| Endosulfan | Banned by **Supreme Court**, 2011 |
| Aldrin (banned) | Killed mugger crocodiles in Kota |
- India still uses at least **66** pesticides banned elsewhere.

=== Andhra Pradesh – Inputs and Extension
- **Tenant farmers' cards:** **Loan Eligibility Cards / Certificates of Cultivation (COC)** earlier; now **Crop Cultivator Rights Cards (CCRCs)** under the **AP Crop Cultivators Rights Act 2019** (11-month agreement; **9,13,283** CCRCs by 3 Jan 2025) – give access to bank credit, insurance, input subsidy.
- **D-Krishi:** Aadhaar-enabled **seed** distribution system of AP.
- **ATMA in AP:** all **26 districts**, **60:40** funding; disseminates latest technologies and fills adoption gaps through **bottom-up planning (SREP)**.
- **APCNF:** run by **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS)**; **jeevamrutham, ghanajeevamrutham**; **10.20 lakh farmers** (2025-26); **Gulbenkian Prize for Humanity 2024**.
- **Rythu Seva Kendras** (formerly Rythu Bharosa Kendras): village one-stop centres for inputs, soil testing, e-Panta, CCRCs, insurance; **8,489** functional after rationalisation; buildings under **MGNREGS**.
- Fertiliser consumption **39.39 LMT** (2024-25); 2015-16 actual use **80.78%** of allotment; pesticide use **2,650 MT → 1,716 MT** (2014-15 → 2024-25).
