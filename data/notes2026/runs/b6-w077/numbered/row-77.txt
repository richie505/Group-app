## SUB 0 | Climate, Monsoon and Hazards
[0:0] # El Niño and a Weak Monsoon
[0:1] - First month of the 2026 monsoon: deficit about **40%**; IMD forecast July rainfall **below 94% of normal**.
[0:2] - Reservoirs (**166**, CWC): **47.725 BCM** on 2 July vs 78.077 BCM last year.
[0:3] - Agri exports: CAGR **8.2% (2020-2025)**, about **12%** of core exports; agriculture about **one-fifth of GVA**, **46%** of workforce, **55%** of population linked.
[0:4] - El Niño drought years: **1972, 1982, 2009, 2015**.
[0:5] - **El Niño**: warming of the **central-eastern equatorial Pacific**; Spanish for "**Little Boy / Christ Child**"; weakens **trade winds**, the **Humboldt (Peruvian) Current** and **upwelling**.
[0:6] - ENSO phases: **El Niño, La Niña, Neutral**; a developing El Niño raises the **probability (not certainty)** of a weak monsoon.
[0:7] - **IMD**: est. **1875**; **Ministry of Earth Sciences**; HQ **New Delhi**; **6 Regional Meteorological Centres** (Mumbai, Chennai, New Delhi, Kolkata, Nagpur, Guwahati); one of **six WMO RSMCs**, names North Indian Ocean cyclones.
[0:8] > La Niña generally strengthens the Indian monsoon; El Niño weakens it.
[0:9] # Glacial Lakes and GLOFs
[0:10] - **Mago Chu Basin, Tawang (Arunachal)**: **4 of 5** glacial lakes expanded **2016-2026**, incl. high and very high risk lakes.
[0:11] - ICIMOD lake types: **glacial erosion** (cirque, valley lakes); **moraine-dammed** = **most vulnerable to GLOF** (loose debris), e.g. **Samudra Tapu, Gepang Gath (HP)**.
[0:12] - **Glacial Lake Atlas 2023**: by **NRSC (ISRO)**; **Brahmaputra basin** has the most glacial lakes.
[0:13] - **Elevation-dependent warming**: high altitudes warm faster.
[0:14] - Events: **Kedarnath 2013 (Chorabari lake)**; **South Lhonak Lake, Sikkim, 2023**.
[0:15] - **NDMA GLOF Guidelines 2020**; **CWC** risk indexing; **National GLOF Risk Mitigation Project** (2025): **Arunachal, Himachal, Sikkim, Uttarakhand**.
[0:16] - **Siachen Glacier**: north of the **Nubra Valley**.
[0:17] # Flash Floods and Cloudbursts - Assam
[0:18] - Assam floods first blamed on a **cloudburst in Nagaland**; IMD said it was heavy rainfall, below the cloudburst threshold.
[0:19] - **IMD cloudburst**: more than **100 mm in one hour** over **20-30 sq km**.
[0:20] - **Flash flood**: develops in **less than six hours**; not every flash flood is a cloudburst (dam release, GLOF, saturated soil, poor drainage).
[0:21] - **1°C** warming = about **7%** more atmospheric moisture.
[0:22] - Flash floods most frequent in the **Brahmaputra** basin, then Ganga and Krishna.
[0:23] - **NDMA**: **Disaster Management Act 2005** (**23 Dec 2005**); constituted **27 Sep 2006**; chaired by **PM** (PM + 9 members); **SDMA - CM**; **DDMA - District Collector**; supervises **NIDM**; background: **High Powered Committee 1999**, Gujarat earthquake 2001. **NDRF** is under **MHA**.
[0:24] # Europe's 2026 Heatwave - Omega Block
[0:25] - **Omega Block**: high pressure between two lows, shaped like **Ω**; stalls the **jet stream** (west to east).
[0:26] - **Heat dome**: sinking, compressed air under the high; **suppresses clouds and rain**.
[0:27] - **Europe**: warming at more than **twice** the global rate: the **fastest-warming continent**.
[0:28] - Terms: **climate whiplash** (rapid swing between extremes); albedo loss from Arctic ice; **tropical night**; **urban heat island**.
[0:29] # Climate Talks and Indices
[0:30] - **SB64 Bonn** (UNFCCC mid-year talks): **8-18 Jun 2026**; first **UNFCCC Dialogue on Trade and Climate** (13 Jun) saw developing countries object to climate-linked trade measures; **India** pushed back on new obligations beyond agreed mandates.

## SUB 10 | Biochar, Pesticides and Green Initiatives
[10:0] # Biochar
[10:1] - Made by **pyrolysis**: oxygen-depleted heating at **300-700°C**; by-products **syngas** and **bio-oil**.
[10:2] - **Decomposes very slowly**: stores carbon for **centuries**.
[10:3] - Benefits: water-holding **+10-25%**; yield **+10-30%**; **1 t biochar sequesters 2.5-3 t CO2e**.
[10:4] - Carbon credits: **VM0042** methodology, **2-2.8 t CO2e** credits per tonne; **KISAN kiln** by **IIT Kharagpur**.
[10:5] - India: crop waste **500 MT+/yr**; Punjab-Haryana burn **20 MT+** paddy straw; MSW **62 MT** (50%+ biodegradable).
[10:6] - Potential: syngas **20-30 MT** (8-13 TWh); bio-oil **24-40 MT** (substitutes 12-19 MT diesel/kerosene).
[10:7] - **Biochar concrete**: **2-5%** biochar; heat resistance **+20%**; captures ~**115 kg CO2/m³**.
[10:8] - Trials: **Akola** (maize stalk, black soil); **Kerala** (coconut leaf stalk); abroad: Kenya (rice husk), Thailand, Brazil (**Embrapa**).
[10:9] - PYQ (2020): biochar in **vertical farming** aids **nitrogen-fixing microbes** and **retains water**.
[10:10] # Carbosulfan Ban Proposed
[10:11] - **Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare** proposed a **complete ban** on **carbosulfan** (manufacture, import, transport, distribution, sale, use).
[10:12] - A highly hazardous **carbamate** insecticide; converts to highly toxic **carbofuran**: harms birds, pollinators, aquatic life.
[10:13] - After consulting the **Registration Committee** under the **Insecticides Act, 1968**; **Central Insecticides Board and Registration Committee** are under the Ministry of Agriculture.
