## SUB 3 | Microgravity and the Human Body
[42:3:0] - **Weightlessness** = free fall: craft and astronaut fall with the same **g**, so the **normal force is zero** – books call it "**zero gravity**".
[42:3:1] - No gravity → **weight becomes zero but mass stays the same**; an astronaut's mass never becomes zero.
[42:3:2] - No candle flame in space; astronauts cannot stand erect; an apple released moves along with the ship.
[42:3:3] - Moon weight = **1/6** of Earth's; at **Earth's centre**, true g is zero.
[42:3:4] TABLE head: Condition | Effect
  [42:3:4.0] Bone loss | 1–1.5% bone mass a month
  [42:3:4.1] Muscle atrophy | Postural muscles weaken
  [42:3:4.2] Fluid shift | "Puffy face, bird legs"
  [42:3:4.3] Space Adaptation Syndrome | Space motion sickness
  [42:3:4.4] SANS | Optic disc swelling, vision changes
[42:3:5] - **Charlie Brown effect:** fluid-shift **puffy face and thin legs** in microgravity (not Snoopy or Peanuts effect).
[42:3:6] - Space medicine is also studied through ground **analogues**, not only spaceflight.
