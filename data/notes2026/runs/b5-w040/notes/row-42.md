=== Human Spaceflight Firsts and Records
# World firsts
| First | Who / what | When |
|---|---|---|
| Artificial satellite | **Sputnik-1**, **USSR** (NASA was set up in 1958 in response) | 4 Oct 1957 |
| Human in space | **Yuri Gagarin**, **Vostok 1** (~108 min orbit) – first successful human spaceflight | **12 Apr 1961** |
| American in space | **Alan Shepard** (Mercury, suborbital); John Glenn first American to orbit | 1961 / 1962 |
| Woman in space | **Valentina Tereshkova**, Vostok-6 (USSR) | 16 Jun 1963 |
| Dog in orbit | **Laika** (Sputnik-2) | 1957 |
| Human on the Moon | **Neil Armstrong** (with Buzz Aldrin), **Apollo 11** – **USA**; the USSR never landed a human | 1969 |
| China's first crewed flight | **Yang Liwei**, **Shenzhou-5**, launcher Chang Zheng-2F; project code **921**; China the **3rd** nation (not 5th) | 15 Oct 2003 |
- **Own crewed launch so far:** **USSR/Russia, USA, China** only – India (Gaganyaan) would be the **4th**.
- Distractors: Bachendri Pal and Junko Tabei are Everest climbers; Sally Ride = first American woman in space; Magellan = navigator.

# Axiom-4
- **Axiom-4:** **25 Jun 2025**, **SpaceX Dragon on Falcon 9**; NASA, Axiom Space, ESA; **18 days** on the ISS; returned 15 Jul 2025.
- Crew: **Peggy Whitson** (commander), Shukla (pilot), Uznanski (**Poland**), Kapu (**Hungary**).
- **7 Indian microgravity experiments** (under HSFC): muscle regeneration, algae, **moong and methi**, microbes, **tardigrades**, cognition, cyanobacteria (not lunar soil mining).

=== Gaganyaan – Programme, Spacecraft and Flights
- India's **first human spaceflight programme**; approved **2018**; **crew of 3** to a **~400 km LEO** for **3 days**; splashdown in Indian waters, recovery by the **Indian Navy**.
- Vehicle: **HLVM3** (human-rated LVM3) from **Sriharikota** – not GSLV Mk II; GSLV Mk-III structures redesigned; **Vikas** engine for the L110 stage qualified in **3 tests**.
- Partners: **Russia and France** cooperate; **DRDO** gives human-centric systems (parachutes, life support, crew recovery).
# Spacecraft
| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| Orbital Module | Crew Module + Service Module; put into orbit by HLVM3 |
| Crew Module | Pressurised habitat for 3; survives re-entry; parachutes, sea splashdown |
| Service Module | Propulsion, power on orbit; fires thrusters to de-orbit, then burns up |
| Crew Escape System | Solid motors pull the Crew Module away if the rocket fails |
- Gaganyaan = **two** parts; Russia's **Soyuz** and China's **Shenzhou** = **three** modules (orbital, descent, service).
# Flights and timeline
- Sequence: uncrewed **G1 → G2 → G3 → crewed H1**; programme revised to 8 missions (6 uncrewed, 2 crewed); H1 now targeted **Q1 2027**.
- Original target: **December 2021** (stated in 2018) → repeatedly revised; quote with the statement date.

=== Space Stations – ISS, Tiangong and BAS
| Feature | ISS | Tiangong |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | **NASA, Roscosmos, ESA, JAXA, CSA** (not ISRO); intergovernmental treaties | China alone |
| Modules | **Zarya** (Russian, first, 1998), Unity (US), Zvezda, Destiny (US lab), Kibo (Japan), Columbus | **Tianhe** core (2021) + **Wentian** (Jul 2022) + **Mengtian** (final, Oct 2022) |
| Status | ~400 km; crewed since **Nov 2000**; retire ~2030 | Completed 2022 |
- Strictly, the ISS is no longer the only active modular station in LEO – Tiangong is one too.
- **BAS (Bharatiya Antariksh Station):** approved **Sep 2024**; **five modules**; base module **BAS-1 by 2028** on LVM3; full station **2035**; Indian on the Moon by **2040**.
- Precursors: **SpaDeX** (PSLV-C60, docked 16 Jan 2025; India 4th) – a docking demonstrator; Axiom-4, Gaganyaan.

=== Microgravity and the Human Body
- **Weightlessness** = free fall: craft and astronaut fall with the same **g**, so the **normal force is zero** – books call it "**zero gravity**".
- No gravity → **weight becomes zero but mass stays the same**; an astronaut's mass never becomes zero.
- No candle flame in space; astronauts cannot stand erect; an apple released moves along with the ship.
- Moon weight = **1/6** of Earth's; at **Earth's centre**, true g is zero.
| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bone loss | 1–1.5% bone mass a month |
| Muscle atrophy | Postural muscles weaken |
| Fluid shift | "Puffy face, bird legs" |
| Space Adaptation Syndrome | Space motion sickness |
| SANS | Optic disc swelling, vision changes |
- **Charlie Brown effect:** fluid-shift **puffy face and thin legs** in microgravity (not Snoopy or Peanuts effect).
- Space medicine is also studied through ground **analogues**, not only spaceflight.

# Analogue missions
- **India's first analogue space mission**: **Leh, Ladakh**, **Nov 2024** (with **AAKA**, **IIT Bombay**) – simulates life in an interplanetary habitat; data for selection, **ECLSS**, work-rest schedules.
- **HOPE** (Himalayan Outpost for Planetary Exploration): **Tso Kar**, Ladakh; early-Mars-like – high UV, low pressure, cold, saline permafrost.
- **MITRA**: crew **behavioural** study at Leh with **IAM**; hypoxia, cold, isolation.
