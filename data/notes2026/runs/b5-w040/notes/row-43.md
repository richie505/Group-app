=== RISAT Series – Radar Imaging Satellites
- **RISAT = Radar Imaging Satellite**; carries **Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)** – active microwave sensing.
- Images **day and night** and **through clouds** (all-weather) – used for border surveillance, agriculture, forestry, disaster management.
| Satellite | Year | Launcher | Key fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| RISAT-2 | **20 Apr 2009** | **PSLV-C12** | **X-band**, Israeli-built radar reconnaissance; border monitoring; co-passenger **ANUSAT** |
| RISAT-1 | 2012 | **PSLV-C19** | **C-band (5.35 GHz)** SAR; India's first fully indigenous radar imaging satellite |
| RISAT-2B | May 2019 | PSLV-C46 | X-band; 2019 collision-avoidance manoeuvre against a defunct US satellite using **NETRA** data |
| **RISAT-2BR1** | **11 Dec 2019** | **PSLV-C48** | Earth-observation (spy) satellite; **50th PSLV flight** (not 60th); with **9 foreign** commercial satellites (4 countries); for **agriculture, forestry and disaster management** |
| EOS-01 (RISAT-2BR2) | Nov 2020 | PSLV-C49 | Renamed under the new EOS naming |
| RISAT-1A (EOS-04) | 2022 | PSLV-C52 | Continuity of RISAT-1 |
| EOS-09 (**RISAT-1B**) | 2025 | PSLV-C61 – failed | C-band SAR; lost after a **pressure drop in the third-stage solid motor** |
- RISAT-2 (2009) flew **before** RISAT-1 (2012). PSLV-C18 = Megha-Tropiques, Jugnu, SRMSAT (not RISAT-1).

=== PSLV-C37 Record and Foreign Co-passengers
- **PSLV-C37 (15 Feb 2017, PSLV's 39th flight):** **104 satellites** in one launch – **714 kg Cartosat-2 series** + **2 ISRO nanosats** + **101 foreign** (international customer) nanosats from **6 countries** (103 co-passengers); 506 km SSO.
- World record then; **now SpaceX Transporter-1, 143 satellites (Jan 2021)**; 104 is still ISRO's record.
- **Over 400 foreign satellites** launched on Indian rockets (1 Jan 2026); NSIL: 141 satellites, incl. 138 customer (July 2026).
- **PSLV-C62** (Jan 2026) carried **15 co-passengers** from start-ups and academic institutions.
~~ Sources: app notes 5:43 sub 6

=== EDUSAT (GSAT-3) – Education Satellite
- **GSAT-3 = EDUSAT**: **India's first satellite exclusively for education** (world's first dedicated educational satellite).
- Launched **20 September 2004** on **GSLV-F01** (not PSLV) from **Sriharikota**.
- **Two-way satellite communication** to classrooms – distance education, teacher training, virtual classrooms.
