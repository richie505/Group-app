## SUB 1 | Gaganyaan – Programme, Spacecraft and Flights
[1:0] - India's **first human spaceflight programme**; approved **2018**; **crew of 3** to a **~400 km LEO** for **3 days**; splashdown in Indian waters, recovery by the **Indian Navy**.
[1:1] - Vehicle: **HLVM3** (human-rated LVM3) from **Sriharikota** – not GSLV Mk II; GSLV Mk-III structures redesigned; **Vikas** engine for the L110 stage qualified in **3 tests**.
[1:2] - Partners: **Russia and France** cooperate; **DRDO** gives human-centric systems (parachutes, life support, crew recovery).
[1:3] # Spacecraft
[1:4] TABLE head: Part | Role
  [1:4.0] Orbital Module | Crew Module + Service Module; put into orbit by HLVM3
  [1:4.1] Crew Module | Pressurised habitat for 3; survives re-entry; parachutes, sea splashdown
  [1:4.2] Service Module | Propulsion, power on orbit; fires thrusters to de-orbit, then burns up
  [1:4.3] Crew Escape System | Solid motors pull the Crew Module away if the rocket fails
[1:5] - Gaganyaan = **two** parts; Russia's **Soyuz** and China's **Shenzhou** = **three** modules (orbital, descent, service).
[1:6] # Flights and timeline
[1:7] - Sequence: uncrewed **G1 → G2 → G3 → crewed H1**; programme revised to 8 missions (6 uncrewed, 2 crewed); H1 now targeted **Q1 2027**.
[1:8] - Original target: **December 2021** (stated in 2018) → repeatedly revised; quote with the statement date.

## SUB 4 | Microgravity and the Human Body
[4:0] - **Weightlessness** = free fall: craft and astronaut fall with the same **g**, so the **normal force is zero** – books call it "**zero gravity**".
[4:1] - No gravity → **weight becomes zero but mass stays the same**; an astronaut's mass never becomes zero.
[4:2] - No candle flame in space; astronauts cannot stand erect; an apple released moves along with the ship.
[4:3] - Moon weight = **1/6** of Earth's; at **Earth's centre**, true g is zero.
[4:4] TABLE head: Condition | Effect
  [4:4.0] Bone loss | 1–1.5% bone mass a month
  [4:4.1] Muscle atrophy | Postural muscles weaken
  [4:4.2] Fluid shift | "Puffy face, bird legs"
  [4:4.3] Space Adaptation Syndrome | Space motion sickness
  [4:4.4] SANS | Optic disc swelling, vision changes
[4:5] - **Charlie Brown effect:** fluid-shift **puffy face and thin legs** in microgravity (not Snoopy or Peanuts effect).
[4:6] - Space medicine is also studied through ground **analogues**, not only spaceflight.
