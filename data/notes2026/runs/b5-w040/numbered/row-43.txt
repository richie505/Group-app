## SUB 0 | RISAT Series – Radar Imaging Satellites
[0:0] - **RISAT = Radar Imaging Satellite**; carries **Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)** – active microwave sensing.
[0:1] - Images **day and night** and **through clouds** (all-weather) – used for border surveillance, agriculture, forestry, disaster management.

  [0:2.1] RISAT-1 | 2012 | PSLV-C19 | C-band (5.35 GHz) SAR; India's first fully indigenous radar imaging satellite
  [0:2.2] RISAT-2B | May 2019 | PSLV-C46 | X-band; 2019 collision-avoidance manoeuvre against a defunct US satellite using NETRA data
  [0:2.3] RISAT-2BR1 | 11 Dec 2019 | PSLV-C48 | Earth-observation (spy) satellite; 50th PSLV flight (not 60th); with 9 foreign commercial satellites (4 countries); for agriculture, forestry and disaster management

## SUB 2 | PSLV-C37 Record and Foreign Co-passengers
[2:0] - **PSLV-C37 (15 Feb 2017, PSLV's 39th flight):** **104 satellites** in one launch – **714 kg Cartosat-2 series** + **2 ISRO nanosats** + **101 foreign** (international customer) nanosats from **6 countries** (103 co-passengers); 506 km SSO.
[2:1] - World record then; **now SpaceX Transporter-1, 143 satellites (Jan 2021)**; 104 is still ISRO's record.
[2:2] - **Over 400 foreign satellites** launched on Indian rockets (1 Jan 2026); NSIL: 141 satellites, incl. 138 customer (July 2026).
[2:3] - **PSLV-C62** (Jan 2026) carried **15 co-passengers** from start-ups and academic institutions.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: app notes 5:43 sub 6
## SUB 3 | EDUSAT (GSAT-3) – Education Satellite
[3:0] - **GSAT-3 = EDUSAT**: **India's first satellite exclusively for education** (world's first dedicated educational satellite).
[3:1] - Launched **20 September 2004** on **GSLV-F01** (not PSLV) from **Sriharikota**.
[3:2] - **Two-way satellite communication** to classrooms – distance education, teacher training, virtual classrooms.
[3:3] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 1 p.104; app notes 5:43 sub 4
