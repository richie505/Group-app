## SUB 1 | NSIL and Demand-driven Missions
[1:0] - **Demand-driven model:** NSIL **builds, owns and operates** satellites against **assured customer demand** (Antrix was supply-driven).
[1:1] TABLE head: Mission | Fact
  [1:1.0] GSAT-24 (June 2022) | First demand-driven satellite after the reforms; 24 Ku-band satellite built by ISRO; launched by Ariane-5 from Kourou, French Guiana (not Balasore); entire capacity leased to Tata Play (DTH); inaugurated at Tata Play's broadcast centre, New Delhi (Aug 2023)
  [1:1.1] GSAT-N2 (GSAT-20) | First demand-driven satellite fully funded and owned by NSIL; Ka-band broadband, in-flight connectivity; SpaceX Falcon 9, 19 Nov 2024 (not Q2 2025) – Indian rockets lacked capacity for it
  [1:1.2] LVM3 (2022) | First dedicated commercial mission – 36 OneWeb satellites
[1:2] - 10 in-orbit satellites transferred to NSIL in 2022; NSIL launches: **141 satellites (138 customer)** as of July 2026.

## SUB 2 | Indian Space Policy 2023 and Space Legislation
[2:0] - **Indian Space Policy 2023:** **approved by the Union Cabinet 6 April 2023**, **released by DoS 20 April 2023**; boosts the role of the Department of Space and private sector.
[2:1] TABLE head: Entity | Role under the policy
  [2:1.0] ISRO | Advanced R&D, deep space, human spaceflight; moves out of operational and commercial production
  [2:1.1] IN-SPACe | Independent regulator – promotes, authorises, supervises NGEs
  [2:1.2] NSIL | Commercialises mature ISRO technology
  [2:1.3] DoS | Policy, space diplomacy, treaty compliance
  [2:1.4] NGEs | End-to-end space activity
[2:2] - Sept 2026: ISRO "**will not be privatised or reduced**" to a regulatory body.
[2:3] TABLE head: Instrument | Year | Status
  [2:3.0] Draft Space Activities Bill | 2017 | Licensing of private space activity
  [2:3.1] Space Activities Bill | Finalised May 2025 | Private participation; statutory powers to IN-SPACe
  [2:3.2] Liability Convention | 1972 | Liability on the launching State
[2:4] - India still has **no Space Activities Act**; no statutory liability framework yet.
