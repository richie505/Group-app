=== Inclusive Growth in the Five Year Plans – 11th and 12th
| Plan | Period | Theme / objective |
|---|---|---|
| 9th | 1997–2002 | Growth with social justice and equality |
| 10th | 2002–07 | Faster growth with development |
| **11th** | **1 Apr 2007 – 31 Mar 2012** | **"Towards Faster and More Inclusive Growth"** – the approach paper's theme; **first** Plan to make **inclusive growth** its core objective |
| **12th** | **2012–17** | **"Faster, Sustainable and More Inclusive Growth"** – adds the **sustainable** focus; the **last** Five Year Plan |
- Questions that say "inclusive growth" alone → **11th Plan**; "sustainable growth" or the three-word theme → **12th Plan**. "Growth with social justice" is the 9th Plan, not the 11th.
- Short forms used in options all mean the 12th Plan theme: "faster sustainable inclusive growth", "faster, sustainable and more inclusive growth"; a "theme = faster growth + more inclusive growth + sustainable growth" question → **all of these**.
- **11th Plan inclusive-growth components** (NextGen): **agricultural growth; employment generation and poverty reduction; reduction of regional and other disparities; equitable growth** – plus 27 monitorable national targets (income and poverty, education, health, women and children, infrastructure, environment). **Strengthening the capital market** and "exclusive dependence on urban services" are **not** components.
- **12th Plan aims:** cut poverty, improve regional equality, better living conditions of SC/ST/OBC/minorities; it **emphasised inclusive growth** (not corporate, competitive or cooperative growth).
- The basic, constant goal of all Indian Plans: **growth** (with self-reliance, employment, equity) – not curbing private enterprise or communes.
- **Plan era ended 31 March 2017** (Planning Commission replaced by **NITI Aayog**, 1 Jan 2015); a 2012 question "which Plan are we in?" = **12th Plan** – now there is no Five Year Plan.
> Repeat angle: 20 PYQs (APPSC, UPSC 2010/2014, NDA 2016, UPPSC, UKPSC, RAS) ask only "which theme belongs to which Plan".

=== Inclusive Growth – Meaning, Requirements and Inclusive Governance
- **Inclusive growth** = **broad-based, shared and pro-poor** growth: growth that advances **equitable opportunities** for all economic participants, with benefits reaching **every section** of society; it raises people's **participation** in the growth process.
- It looks at both the **pace and the pattern** of growth (who benefits), plus gender and regional balance.
- **Three pillars** of India's model: **Market Economics, Empowerment, Pragmatism** (APPSC-GS 2025) – not "Antyodaya, Empowerment, Poverty and Unemployment".
| Requirement | Role (Rocket #70) |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure development | Expands access and economic opportunities |
| **Agricultural revival** | Benefits the rural population |
| Education, vocational training | Improves capabilities – the answer for "underutilised and unskilled manpower" |
| Health, social services | Improves human capital |
| Employment, **credit to farmers** | Enables participation and productive activity |
| **SHGs** | Financial / economic participation (bottom-up) |
| **MSMEs** | Employment and entrepreneurship |
| **Right to Education** | Brings disadvantaged children into the mainstream |
- Inclusive growth "would necessitate" infrastructure, agricultural revival and social services → **all of the above**; SHGs, MSMEs and the RTE Act all further it → **1, 2 and 3** (UPSC 2011).
- Inclusive growth is **NOT** expected to come from a **high national-income growth rate by itself**; it comes from rural development, agriculture and adequate farm credit. Focusing solely on urban areas, raising import tariffs or cutting social spending do not help.
- Target segments: marginal farmers, landless labourers, SC/ST – **not** semi-urban persons alone (UPPSC 2016).
- **Inclusive governance** (UPSC 2012): **District Planning Committees** in all districts, **more public-health spending**, **stronger Mid-day Meal** → **2, 3 and 4**; permitting NBFCs to do banking is **not** part of it.
- **OECD (with the G20):** "Promoting Strong, Sustainable and Inclusive Growth" – the OECD's publication / theme for G20 policy coordination.
- Instruments in numbers: **DAY-NRLM** 10.05 crore women in **90.09 lakh** SHGs; **MSMEs** 31.1% of GDP, 35.4% of manufacturing output, **48.58%** of exports; **VB-G RAM G** replaced MGNREGA, **100 → 125 days**; **Aspirational Districts** (Jan 2018, 49 KPIs, 5 themes), **Aspirational Blocks** (Jan 2023, 500 blocks).
- **AP:** Navaratnalu (2019–24: Amma Vodi, YSR Rythu Bharosa) → **Super Six**: **Talliki Vandanam** ₹15,000 per school child; **Annadata Sukhibhava** ₹20,000 per farmer; **Deepam-2** 3 free LPG cylinders; **Stree Shakti** free bus travel for women (15 Aug 2025); AP Rural Inclusive Growth Project (five components).

=== Distributive Justice – Constitution and Socialism
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| 38 | Social order with social, economic and political justice; **38(2)** (**44th Amendment, 1978**): minimise inequalities in income, status, facilities and opportunities |
| **39(b)** | Ownership and control of the **material resources** of the community distributed to **subserve the common good** |
| **39(c)** | Operation of the economic system must not result in **concentration of wealth and means of production** to the common detriment |
| 31C | **25th Amendment, 1971**: protects laws giving effect to 39(b) and 39(c) |
- **Socialism** aims at **distributive justice** – fair distribution of income, wealth and resources; both statements of 39(b) and 39(c) are **present** in the Constitution (DPSP, Part IV).
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth, Indian Polity 8th ed. ch. Directive Principles (Art. 38, 39, 31C); app notes 3:15 sub 1

=== Approaches to Poverty and the Bhagwati–Sen Debate
| Approach | Illustration |
|---|---|
| **Trickle-down** | Part of the income of the **asset-owning class filters down** to the assetless; early Plans' growth-first strategy (criticised: widens inequality) |
| Direct attack | 5th Plan "Garibi Hatao"; wage-employment and livelihood missions |
| Welfare | Nordic states |
| Bottom-up | SHG movement |
- **Bhagwati–Sen debate** – growth first vs social infrastructure first:

| | Jagdish Bhagwati (with Arvind Panagariya) | Amartya Sen (with Jean Drèze) |
|---|---|---|
| Priority | **Economic growth first** – rapid market-led growth creates jobs and revenue for welfare | **Investment in social infrastructure first** – health, education, nutrition |
| Book (2013) | *Why Growth Matters* | *An Uncertain Glory* |
- The debate (APPSC G1 2017) is **growth first vs social infrastructure first** – not industry vs agriculture, rural vs urban or private vs public investment.
- Sen: backward countries should give top priority to **primary education and health** (APPSC-GS 2007).

=== Measuring Inclusion – WEF Inclusive Development Index and Poverty Data
- **WEF Inclusive Development Index (IDI)** – pillars: **growth and development; inclusion; intergenerational equity and sustainability**.
| Edition | Top (developing / emerging) | India |
|---|---|---|
| **IDI 2017** (16 Jan 2017, Davos) | **Lithuania** (then Azerbaijan, Hungary) | **60th of 79** developing economies |
| IDI 2018 (Jan 2018) | Lithuania | 62nd of 74 emerging economies |
- Inclusion is measured by **HDI**, poverty rates, literacy, access to basic services and gender equality – not by GDP, industrial output or employment alone.
- **World Bank:** extreme poverty ($3/day) **27.1% (2011-12) → 5.3% (2022-23)**; at $4.20 a day 57.7% → **23.9%**. World Inequality Database: income inequality **rising**.
