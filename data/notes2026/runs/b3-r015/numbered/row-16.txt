## SUB 0 | Human Development and the HDR – Origin, UNDP, Concept and Pillars
[0:0] - **UNDP** was the first to focus on the **human aspect of development**: first **Human Development Report (HDR), 1990**, which contained the first **HDI**; HDRs released most years since.
[0:1] - The HDR **compares countries on educational levels, health status and per capita income** ("Human Development Report" – not a population, education or quality report).
[0:2] - **Mahbub ul Haq** introduced the concept and **created the HDI (1990)**; **Amartya Sen** worked with him on the first HDRs (team incl. Inge Kaul, Paul Streeten, Frances Stewart). Sen = **capability approach**; **Martha Nussbaum** extended it with a list of central capabilities.
[0:3] - Human development = enlarging people's **choices, freedoms and opportunities**; people, not growth alone, are the criteria; income is a **means**, not the end.
[0:4] - **Four pillars of human development:** **Equity, Sustainability, Productivity, Empowerment** (CDS 2020) – not "inclusion" or "gender".
[0:5] TABLE head: Approach to human development | Idea
  [0:5.0] Income | Higher income = higher human development (oldest)
  [0:5.1] Welfare | People as passive beneficiaries of welfare spending
  [0:5.2] Minimum / basic needs (ILO) | Health, education, food, water, sanitation, housing – India's Minimum Needs Programme (5th Plan) = investment in human (resources) approach
  [0:5.3] Capabilities | Amartya Sen
[0:6] - **HDR 2016** ("Human Development for Everyone"): **universalism** is the key to human development.
[0:7] - UNDP identifies two categories of poverty: **income poverty** and **human poverty** – both.
[0:8] - **Same GNI, different HDI** shows the role of **national policy choices** (Malaysia richer than Chile, but Chile ~7 years more life expectancy and 3 more years of expected schooling).
[0:9] TABLE head: Basis | GDP / per capita income | Human development
  [0:9.0] Nature | Primarily economic measure | Broader, people-centred
  [0:9.1] Dimensions | One-dimensional | Multidimensional
[0:10] - **Countries covered:** HDR 2018 update **189**; HDR 2021/22 191; HDR 2023-24 and 2025 **193**.

## SUB 1 | HDI Dimensions, Indicators and Methodology
[1:0] TABLE head: Dimension | Indicator | Min | Max
  [1:0.0] Long and healthy life | Life expectancy at birth | 20 | 85
  [1:0.1] Knowledge | Expected years of schooling | 0 | 18
  [1:0.2] Knowledge | Mean years of schooling (adults 25+) | 0 | 15
  [1:0.3] Decent standard of living | GNI per capita (PPP $), log | $100 | $75,000
[1:1] - HDI = composite of **life expectancy, educational attainment and per capita income**; base = **health, education, standard of living**. Before 2010 the income indicator was per capita **GDP**.
[1:2] - **NOT in the HDI:** **ownership of basic assets**, **access to banking**, **employment**, poverty, nutritional status, quality of life, environment, urbanisation, human security, gender, inequality (that is the **IHDI**).
[1:3] - Method: dimension index = (actual − min)/(max − min); education index = **arithmetic mean** of its two sub-indices; income uses the **logarithm** (diminishing returns); **HDI = geometric mean** of the three indices (**since 2010**), equal weights.
[1:4] - **PPP dollars** allow fair comparison across price levels.
[1:5] TABLE head: Category | HDI value
  [1:5.0] Very high | 0.800 and above
  [1:5.1] High | 0.700–0.799
  [1:5.2] Medium | 0.550–0.699
  [1:5.3] Low | below 0.550
[1:6] - India **0.685** (HDR 2025, for 2023) = **Medium**, just below 0.700.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC Exams 15 p.6-9 (indicators, goalposts, groupings), 16 p.1 (geometric mean since 2010); NextGen Rocket Sheets Indian Economy #52 p.77; UNDP HDR 2025 Technical notes; app notes 3:16 sub 1-3

## SUB 4 | Human Development in Indian States – NHDR and State HDRs
[4:0] TABLE head: Report | Year | Fact
  [4:0.0] First State HDR | 1995 | Madhya Pradesh; released by Amartya Sen in Delhi; HDI for the State and all districts
  [4:0.1] First NHDR | April 2001 | Planning Commission; Kerala top, Punjab 2nd, UP 13th, Bihar 15th (descending: Kerala, Punjab, UP, Bihar)
  [4:0.2] Second NHDR (India Human Development Report) | 2011 | Planning Commission / IAMR; Kerala 1st
  [4:0.3] First AP HDR | 2007 (released 2008) | CESS, Hyderabad; sponsored by Planning Commission + UNDP
[4:1] - The Indian Human Development Report gives, for each sample village, **education, health and infrastructure-and-amenities** indices – **no unemployment index**.
[4:2] - **Kerala** consistently tops ("Kerala Model"); Bihar, UP, MP at the bottom (Bihar 0.650 vs India 0.685, 2023).
[4:3] - **HDI and SDG ranking** of States are **positively related** because SDG targets rest largely on **education and health** (UPPSC 2019: A and R true, R explains A).
