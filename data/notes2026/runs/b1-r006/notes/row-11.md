| Sculpture | Site | Material |
|---|---|---|
| **Dancing Girl** (~10.5 cm / 4 inches; found 1926; National Museum, New Delhi) | Mohenjodaro | **Bronze** (lost-wax) – not clay, gold or carnelian (UPSC 2025) |
| **Priest-King** (bearded man in trefoil shawl, half-closed eyes) | Mohenjodaro | **Steatite** |
| Male torso | Harappa | **Red sandstone / jasper** |

- The IVC gives the **first cities**, the **first script** (right to left) and the **earliest stone sculpture** (three-dimensional) in the subcontinent – but **not iron** sculpture; iron was unknown (CDS 2017).
- Lifelike bronze and stone figures → **advanced metallurgy** and **craft specialisation**.

=== Pottery
- **Wheel-made**, sturdy, glossy; typical **red pottery** (red slip) **with black painted designs**; **plain red** ware the most common → colour = **red** (BPSC 1995).
- Motifs: **geometric, floral, pipal leaf, fish-scale**, animals.
- **Perforated jars**: large hole at the base, small holes in the wall – probably for **straining liquor**.
- **Kalibangan** = six types of pottery; **Lothal** = painted jar (cunning fox story); big storage jars reached Oman.
- Only evidence of painting is on pottery (no wall paintings).

=== Lost-wax (Cire Perdue) and Metal Crafts
- 1. Wax model coated with clay. 2. Heated: wax melts out. 3. Molten metal poured into the hollow mould. 4. Clay removed.
- Lost-wax objects: **Dancing Girl**, **copper dog and bird of Lothal**, **bronze bull of Kalibangan**, buffalo and goat figures; continued in Late Harappan and Chalcolithic cultures (Daimabad bronzes) and later in **Chola** bronzes such as the **Nataraja**.
| Object | Site | Metal |
|---|---|---|
| Dog chasing a deer | Harappa | Bronze |
| Dog and bird | Lothal | Copper |
| Bull / ox | Kalibangan | Bronze |
| Chariot group, buffalo, rhinoceros, elephant | Daimabad | Bronze / copper |
| Gold foundry, active furnace | Rakhigarhi | Gold |
| Gold and silver necklaces | Allahdino | Gold, silver |
- Tools: chisels, needles, fishhooks, razors, mirrors, axes; vessels of copper, bronze, **silver**.

=== Beads, Faience and Shell
| Centre | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Chanhudaro | Bead-making factory, furnace, shell-working, bangles (APPSC G1 2022); lipsticks, ink pot; only city without a citadel |
| Lothal | Bead workshops; carnelian beads for export |
| Rakhigarhi | Gemstone factory – 3,000+ semi-precious stones |
- Materials: **carnelian** (from **Saurashtra**/Bharuch), agate, jasper, **steatite**, lapis lazuli, **faience**, **gold**; beads exported to **Mesopotamia** and the **Persian Gulf**.
- **Faience** (ground sand/silica, glazed blue-green): beads, bangles, tiny vessels – called the **world's first glazed ware**; tie with **mosaic glass** = early clue to glass-making.
- Shell work: Chanhudaro shell-ornament factory; Nageshwar, Balakot, Lothal.
- Seal carving (steatite; unicorn commonest motif) and carnelian working = new techniques of the period.
