## SUB 1 | Pottery
[1:0] - **Wheel-made**, sturdy, glossy; typical **red pottery** (red slip) **with black painted designs**; **plain red** ware the most common → colour = **red** (BPSC 1995).
[1:1] - Motifs: **geometric, floral, pipal leaf, fish-scale**, animals.
[1:2] - **Perforated jars**: large hole at the base, small holes in the wall – probably for **straining liquor**.
[1:3] - **Kalibangan** = six types of pottery; **Lothal** = painted jar (cunning fox story); big storage jars reached Oman.
[1:4] - Only evidence of painting is on pottery (no wall paintings).
[1:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.99; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.8, p.12; old app notes row 11
## SUB 2 | Lost-wax (Cire Perdue) and Metal Crafts
[2:0] - 1. Wax model coated with clay. 2. Heated: wax melts out. 3. Molten metal poured into the hollow mould. 4. Clay removed.
[2:1] - Lost-wax objects: **Dancing Girl**, **copper dog and bird of Lothal**, **bronze bull of Kalibangan**, buffalo and goat figures; continued in Late Harappan and Chalcolithic cultures (Daimabad bronzes) and later in **Chola** bronzes such as the **Nataraja**.
[2:2] TABLE head: Object | Site | Metal
  [2:2.0] Dog chasing a deer | Harappa | Bronze
  [2:2.1] Dog and bird | Lothal | Copper
  [2:2.2] Bull / ox | Kalibangan | Bronze
  [2:2.3] Chariot group, buffalo, rhinoceros, elephant | Daimabad | Bronze / copper
  [2:2.4] Gold foundry, active furnace | Rakhigarhi | Gold
  [2:2.5] Gold and silver necklaces | Allahdino | Gold, silver
[2:3] - Tools: chisels, needles, fishhooks, razors, mirrors, axes; vessels of copper, bronze, **silver**.

## SUB 4 | Beads, Faience and Shell
[4:0] TABLE head: Centre | Evidence
  [4:0.0] Chanhudaro | Bead-making factory, furnace, shell-working, bangles (APPSC G1 2022); lipsticks, ink pot; only city without a citadel
  [4:0.1] Lothal | Bead workshops; carnelian beads for export
  [4:0.2] Rakhigarhi | Gemstone factory – 3,000+ semi-precious stones
[4:1] - Materials: **carnelian** (from **Saurashtra**/Bharuch), agate, jasper, **steatite**, lapis lazuli, **faience**, **gold**; beads exported to **Mesopotamia** and the **Persian Gulf**.
[4:2] - **Faience** (ground sand/silica, glazed blue-green): beads, bangles, tiny vessels – called the **world's first glazed ware**; tie with **mosaic glass** = early clue to glass-making.
[4:3] - Shell work: Chanhudaro shell-ornament factory; Nageshwar, Balakot, Lothal.
[4:4] - Seal carving (steatite; unicorn commonest motif) and carnelian working = new techniques of the period.
[4:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.88, p.100; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.7-8, p.13; old app notes row 11
