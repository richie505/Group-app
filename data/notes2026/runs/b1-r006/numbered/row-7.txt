## SUB 0 | Lothal – Port Town, Dockyard and the Maritime Heritage Complex
[0:0] - **Lothal** (Ahmedabad, Gujarat) on the **Bhogava** (tributary of the Sabarmati), near the **Gulf of Khambhat** = **the port city** of the IVC (UPPCS 1999, BPSC 2011, SI 2019) – not Harappa, Kalibangan, Mohenjodaro or Ropar.
[0:1] - **Dockyard:** brick-lined basin **218 m × 37 m** with sluice gates – the world's earliest tidal dock; warehouse on a raised platform with seals and sealings.
[0:2] - **Discovered 1954, excavated 1955–60 by S.R. Rao** (not Wheeler, not Daya Ram Sahni).
[0:3] - **Other finds:** rice husk, fire altars, bead-making factory, Persian Gulf (button) seal, ship on a seal, terracotta ship, copper dog, chess-like game set, three double burials, painted jar (cunning fox story), ivory scale.
[0:4] - Entrance of houses on the **main street** (other cities: side lanes). Called "Mini Harappa".
[0:5] - **Other ports:** Rangpur, Somnath (Prabhas Patan), Balakot, Kuntasi, Khirasara, Sutkagendor.
[0:6] # National Maritime Heritage Complex (NMHC)
[0:7] - **NMHC** is being built at **Lothal**, Gujarat (not Kozhikode, Visakhapatnam, Sindhudurg, Kanyakumari, Puri or Panjim) – Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways; approved by the **Union Cabinet, October 2024**.
[0:8] - **As of Sep 2026:** Phase 1A about **82%** complete, target **31 Oct 2026**; Phase 1B to add eight galleries and a lighthouse museum.

## SUB 4 | Largest Sites, UNESCO and Rakhigarhi
[4:0] - **Five largest** cities: **Mohenjodaro, Harappa, Ganweriwala, Rakhigarhi, Dholavira**.
[4:1] - **Rakhigarhi** (Hisar, Haryana) = **largest Harappan site** (about 350–550 ha; 11 mounds per Shinde); **Bhirrana** = oldest.
[4:2] TABLE head: Site | UNESCO status
  [4:2.0] Dholavira | WHS 2021 (India's 40th) – only inscribed Harappan site in India
  [4:2.1] Mohenjodaro (Pakistan) | Inscribed 1980
  [4:2.2] Rakhigarhi | Tentative List
[4:3] - **Rakhigarhi DNA:** the ~4,600-year-old female skeleton **lacked the "Steppe Pastoralist" gene** → read as **indigenous ancestry**, challenging the Aryan-invasion view.
[4:4] - **Mound 7** cemetery: **56 skeletons**; dig of **23 Jun 2026** found **five complete skeletons** (aged 30–40) and **three fragmentary burials**.
[4:5] - First site excavated: Harappa (1921); first after Independence: Ropar.
