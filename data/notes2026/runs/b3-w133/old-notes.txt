[133:0:0] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [133:0:0.0] 3 | Formation of Telangana – 10 districts incl. Hyderabad, Medak, Karimnagar, Khammam (minus Polavaram villages); amended by the 2014 Polavaram Ordinance/Act to move 7 Khammam mandals to AP

  [133:0:0.3] 6 | Central expert committee on AP's new capital – report within six months of enactment
  [133:0:0.4] 7 | Governor of existing AP to be common Governor for a period determined by the President
  [133:0:0.5] 8 | Governor's special responsibility for security of life, liberty and property of residents of the common capital (law and order, internal security, vital installations, Government buildings)

[133:0:2] - **Sec 8(4):** assisted by **two advisors** appointed by the **Central Government**.

[133:0:4] - **Hyderabad's status:** it lay in Telangana's territory (not common territory); it was AP's **de jure** capital till 2 June 2024 while AP worked from Amaravati; tax revenue of Hyderabad is **not shared** with AP.

## SUB 5 | Part IV – High Court
[133:5:0] - **Sec 30:** the **High Court of Judicature at Hyderabad** = **common High Court** for both States till AP's High Court is constituted (named "High Court of Judicature at Hyderabad for the State of Telangana and the State of Andhra Pradesh"); judges' salaries shared by population ratio.
[133:5:1] - **Sec 31:** a **separate High Court for AP**; the Hyderabad court becomes Telangana's; **31(2): principal seat at such place as the President notifies** – the Act names **no place**.
[133:5:2] - **Presidential order of 26 December 2018** (Art 214) → **High Court of Andhra Pradesh at Amaravati** functioning from **1 January 2019**.

## SUB 7 | Capital Question After 2014
[133:7:0] - **Sivaramakrishnan Committee** (Sec 6) on the new capital; AP chose **Amaravati** (2015).
[133:7:1] - Common capital ended on **2 June 2024**; Hyderabad is now Telangana's capital only.
[133:7:2] - AP Assembly resolution of **28 March 2026** asked to insert "**at Amaravati**" in Sec 5(2); the **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026** named **Amaravati** the sole capital with effect from **2 June 2024** – the first law in which Parliament named a State's capital. === NOTES 3:135 | Division of Assets, Liabilities, Employees and Institutions; Ninth and Tenth Schedule Institutions
## SUB 0 | Principles of Division – Item → Basis → Section
[135:0:0] # The core rule
[135:0:1] - **Act:** AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 – President's assent **1 March 2014**; appointed day **2 June 2014**.
[135:0:2] - **Core rule:** **assets by location**, **liabilities (debts) by population ratio**.
[135:0:3] - **Population ratio** (Census 2011): **AP : Telangana = 58.32 : 41.68** (≈ **58 : 42**).
[135:0:4] - **Result:** AP got about **46% of revenue** but inherited about **58% of debt** – major assets built in the united State stand in **Hyderabad**, which went to Telangana; so the location rule is unfair to AP.
[135:0:5] - **Financial division:** **Part VI**, "Apportionment of Assets and Liabilities", **Sections 47-67** (the money items are **Sections 47-64**, Sec 64 = residuary; 76-83 = services); disputes settled by mutual agreement, failing which by **Central order on the advice of the CAG** (Sec 47(4)).
[135:0:6] # Item → basis → section
[135:0:7] TABLE head: Item | Basis | Section
  [135:0:7.0] Award of the 13th Finance Commission | Population ratio and other parameters (Centre apportions) | 46(1)
  [135:0:7.1] Land, stores, goods inside a State | Location | 48
  [135:0:7.2] Stores, goods, property outside the existing State (e.g. AP Bhavan) | Population ratio | 48
  [135:0:7.3] Treasury and bank cash balances | Population ratio, by adjusting credit balances in the books of the RBI – no transfer of cash between treasuries | 49
  [135:0:7.4] Arrears of property tax / land revenue | State where the property lies | 50
  [135:0:7.5] Arrears of any other tax | State of the place of assessment | 50
  [135:0:7.6] Loans and advances to a body or person inside the State (incl. deferred taxes of industrial units) | State where the area / unit is located | 51(1)
  [135:0:7.7] Loans and advances to a person or institution outside the State | Right to recover belongs to Andhra Pradesh; sums recovered shared by population ratio | 51(2)

[136:0:4] - **14th Finance Commission (2015-20):** post-devolution **Revenue Deficit Grant of ₹22,113 crore** to AP over five years; States' share raised **32% → 42%**; removed the general/special category distinction.

=== NOTES 3:139 | Compensation for Loss of Revenue and Development of Backward Districts (rayalaseema and North Coastal)
## SUB 0 | Backward Districts Package – Sections 46 and 94
[139:0:0] - **Section 46(2)**: Centre may make **appropriate grants** and give a **special development package** to **backward areas** of AP; **46(3)**: adequate incentives **in particular for Rayalaseema and north coastal regions**.
[139:0:1] - **Section 94(2)**: Centre to support backward-area programmes (physical and social infrastructure).
[139:0:2] TABLE head: Feature | Detail
  [139:0:2.0] Districts | 7 = 4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Coastal
  [139:0:2.1] Rayalaseema | Anantapur, Kurnool, YSR Kadapa, Chittoor
  [139:0:2.2] North Coastal | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam
  [139:0:2.3] Amount | ₹50 crore per district per year = ₹350 crore a year (2016-17)
  [139:0:2.4] Model | KBK (Odisha) and Bundelkhand packages
  [139:0:2.5] Status | First allocations made; disbursements stalled
[139:0:3] > Section options 43-46: the package is **Section 46** (46(2)-(3)); not 45, 47 or 49.

## SUB 1 | BRGF and the Aspirational Districts Programme
[139:1:0] - **BRGF** (Ministry of Panchayati Raj, 2006-07): objectives – **bridge critical gaps in local infrastructure** and development, **strengthen panchayat and municipality governance**, give **professional support** to local bodies for planning, improve their performance; all three are objectives.
[139:1:1] - BRGF covered **250** districts; **13** in undivided AP (9 Telangana + Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa, Vizianagaram; not Srikakulam or Hyderabad); **delinked from central support in 2015-16**, folded into the Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA).
[139:1:2] - **Aspirational Districts Programme**: **NITI Aayog**, launched by PM Modi in **January 2018**; **112** districts; built on **Convergence, Collaboration, Competition** (not compensation).
[139:1:3] - **Convergence** = effective use of funds and services of different departments.
[139:1:4] - Visakhapatnam Economic Region (**10 districts**, 13 Nov 2025) is not part of ADP.
[139:1:5] ~~ Sources: web page tnrd.tn.gov.in/schemes/cen_brgf_13.html (BRGF aims); app notes 3:139 (old-notes.txt)
## SUB 2 | Tax Incentives and Revenue Compensation
[139:2:0] - **Section 94(1)**: fiscal measures incl. tax incentives (notified 2015): **35% additional depreciation** + **15% investment allowance** for units set up **1 Apr 2015 – 31 Mar 2020** in backward districts of **both AP and Telangana**.
[139:2:1] - SCS (refused) would have given a **90% central share in CSS** and tax exemptions.
[139:2:2] - **101st Amendment, 2016**: GST compensation for revenue loss.
[139:2:3] - 2014-15 resource-gap grant: **₹3,979.50 crore**; **16th FC** ended RD grants for **all States** (AP loss up to ₹38,000 crore); AP cites the PM's **Rajya Sabha** assurances.
[139:2:4] - **Polavaram**: Centre limits liability to **old price levels**.
[139:2:5] ~~ Sources: app notes 3:139 (old-notes.txt)
## SUB 3 | Rayalaseema
[139:3:0] - Now **8 districts**: Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai, YSR, Annamayya, Tirupati, Chittoor.
[139:3:1] - **Low population density** due to recurring **droughts and famines**.
[139:3:2] - **43%** of horticulture area, **71%** of micro-irrigation; six AP districts in India's top 10 for micro-irrigation = five Rayalaseema + **Prakasam**.
[139:3:3] - **Mega Seed Park**: **Kurnool** district (Tangadancha).
[139:3:4] - **Sri Bagh Pact (16 Nov 1937)**: irrigation priority to Rayalaseema for 10 years.

## SUB 4 | North Coastal Andhra (Uttarandhra)
[139:4:0] - "**Kalinga Andhra**", 6 districts: Alluri Sitharama Raju, Anakapalli, Parvathipuram Manyam, Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam.
