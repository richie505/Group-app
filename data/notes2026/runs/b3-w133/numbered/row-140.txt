## SUB 1 | Divisions, Waltair Split and the K-K Line
[1:0] - SCoR has **4 divisions**: **Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, Guntakal**; Vijayawada, Guntur and Guntakal came from **South Central Railway**, which shrinks to **three divisions**.
[1:1] - **South Central Railway** itself began functioning on **2 October 1966**.
[1:2] TABLE head: Part | Goes to
  [1:2.0] Andhra part of Waltair | Visakhapatnam division of SCoR
  [1:2.1] Rest of Waltair | New Rayagada division of East Coast Railway (HQ Bhubaneswar)
[1:3] - **Kottavalasa-Kirandul (K-K) line** = "revenue lifeline" of Waltair; Odisha claimed it; AP argued Waltair earned more than **Vijayawada, Guntur and Guntakal combined**; AP offered only Odisha stretches.
[1:4] - A dedicated zone helps **VCIC** by cutting freight costs and improving port connectivity.
