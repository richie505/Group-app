## SUB 1 | World Bank Projects in AP
[1:0] - **AP Economic Restructuring Project (1998)**: AP became the **first Indian State** to negotiate an **independent World Bank loan**; it included reform of district primary education in **14** educationally disadvantaged districts.
[1:1] - **APRIGP**: approved **19 December 2014**; **$75 million** of $107 million; **five components** (first: **value chain development**); covers the **150 most backward mandals**; implemented by **SERP**.
[1:2] - **SERP** also runs the **Indradhanussu** total-immunisation drive and a **retail chain** concept to market SHG products and FMCG goods.
[1:3] - **APIIATP**: climate resilience in **565 minor-tank villages**, **22 districts**; concluded **31 October 2025**.
[1:4] - AP Road Sector Project: **850 km** of MDRs and State highways; **AP Disaster Recovery Project** after **Cyclone Hudhud (2014)**.

## SUB 3 | EAP Repayment under Special Assistance
[3:0] - **Special Assistance Measure** (Union Cabinet, **15 March 2017**), in lieu of SCS: Centre repays loans and interest of EAPs signed **2015-16 to 2019-20**, bearing **90%** of the cost.
