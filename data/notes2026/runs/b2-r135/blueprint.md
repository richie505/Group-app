## 3. Repeats and traps
- **Kaka Kalelkar = first BC Commission** is the single most repeated fact (7 PYQs); **Mandal** chairman/government/year/report 6 more.
- **86th Amendment = Art. 21A** (4 PYQs); **Gram Sabha initiates FRA claims** (UPSC 2013 asked twice, same wording).
- **RTE vs ECCE (Art. 45)** odd-one-out repeated in APPSC HWO papers; RMSA "6-16" wrong statement asked twice.
- **Bill vs Act numbers** (124th Bill → 103rd Act; 123rd Bill → 102nd Act) are a favourite APPSC G1 trap.
- Keys to watch: the old app key "Bharia first habitat rights" was wrong (Baiga, MP, 2015-16); Mandal "1978" vs Laxmikanth "1979" now carries a ⚠ dispute line.
