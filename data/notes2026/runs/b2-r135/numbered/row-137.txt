## SUB 0 | Universalisation of Education – NPE, SSA, RMSA, Samagra Shiksha
[0:0] # National Policy on Education
[0:1] - First NPE: **1968** (Kothari Commission); second NPE: **1986** (revised 1992).
[0:2] - **NPE 1986 vs 1968:** to universalisation of elementary education it **added the goal of minimum levels of learning (MLL)** – quality, not just enrolment.
[0:3] - NPE priority areas: **universal elementary education, adult literacy, education for women's equality** – "**compulsory nutrition education to all**" is **NOT** a priority area (APPSC G2, 2018).
[0:4] # Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan (2001) → Samagra Shiksha (2018)
[0:5] - **Samagra Shiksha (2018)** merged **SSA, RMSA and Teacher Education**; funding **60:40**, **90:10** for North-Eastern and hill States; KGBVs up to **Class 12**.
[0:6] - **Component of SSA:** **NPEGEL** – National Programme for Education of Girls at Elementary Level; also **KGBV**.
[0:7] - **Wrong statements on SSA after RTE:** "GoI bears the **whole expense** of SSA" (it is shared); "it is **mandatory** on GoI to bring **early childhood care and education** under SSA" (ECCE below 6 is Art. 45, a DPSP, outside RTE).
[0:8] - **Interventions for universal elementary education:** mid-day meals, free books and uniforms, SSA, KGBV – **not free laptops** (code a, b, d, e).
[0:9] - **Tamil Nadu** pioneered the Mid-Day Meal; ICDS 1975.
[0:10] # RMSA (2009) and RUSA (2013)
[0:11] - **RMSA** = universal access to **secondary** education (Classes 9-10); **SEMIS** (Secondary Education Management Information System) = a **database system mapping schools** at national, State, district, block and village level.
[0:12] - **Not associated with RMSA:** "universal education for all children aged **6-16**" (6-14 is RTE); "access to quality secondary education is a **constitutional commitment**" (only 6-14 is, under Art. 21A).
[0:13] - **RUSA** (Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan) = funding of State **higher** education institutions; the wrong statement: "it is recommended by the National Education Commission".
[0:14] - Teacher education is regulated by the **NCTE** (National Council for Teacher Education).

## SUB 1 | RTE Act, 2009 – Provisions
[1:0] - Full name: **Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009**; gives effect to **Art. 21A**; free and compulsory **elementary** education for **6–14** years (Classes 1–8); in force **1 April 2010** – the Art. 51A(k) duty became a statutory right then.
[1:1] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [1:1.0] 3 | Age 6–14; neighbourhood school; children with disabilities: free education 6 to 18 years
  [1:1.1] 12(1)(c) | Unaided private schools (excluding minority) reserve 25% entry-level seats for weaker and disadvantaged groups; State reimburses
  [1:1.2] 21 | School Management Committees; three-fourths members parents/guardians
  [1:1.3] 29(2)(f) | Medium of instruction, as far as practicable, the child's mother tongue
  [1:1.4] 38 | Rules by the appropriate Government (e.g. AP RTE Rules, 2010, framed under the Act)
[1:2] - **Pupil-teacher ratio**: **30:1** primary; **35:1** upper primary. **Teacher qualifications**: the **NCTE** (not a State council).
[1:3] - No **screening**, no **capitation fee**; out-of-school child admitted to an **age-appropriate class**.
[1:4] - Monitoring and grievance redressal: **NCPCR and SCPCRs**.
[1:5] - **No-detention:** original Act – none till Class 8; **RTE (Amendment) Act 2019** – regular exams in Classes 5 and 8, a child failing the re-exam can be held back.
[1:6] - **Not features of RTE:** early childhood care below 6 (Art. 45); pre-school and secondary education; **Centre bearing all funds** (shared); minority schools under the 25% quota.
[1:7] - **Education is in the Concurrent List** (42nd Amendment) → a State law **repugnant** to the RTE Act is **void to the extent of repugnancy** (Art. 254).
[1:8] TABLE head: Case | Issue decided
  [1:8.0] Unni Krishnan v. State of AP (1993) | Children below 14 have a fundamental right to free education under Art. 21; arose from Andhra Pradesh
  [1:8.1] Society for Unaided Private Schools of Rajasthan (2012) | Upheld the 25% quota; not for unaided minority schools
  [1:8.2] Pramati Educational Trust (2014) | RTE Act not applicable to minority schools
  [1:8.3] Bachpan Bachao Andolan v. UoI (2011) | Child trafficking, children in circuses
[1:9] - **January 2026**: SC reaffirmed Sec. 12(1)(c); States to frame admission rules under **Sec. 38**. **4 Sep 2026**: SC called quality elementary teacher education vital to **Art. 21A**.

## SUB 4 | POCSO, Juvenile Justice and Adoption
[4:0] - **POCSO Act, 2012**: in force **14 November 2012**; child = **below 18**; **gender-neutral**. **Criminal Law (Amendment) Ordinance, April 2018**: death penalty possible for rape of a girl **below 12**. **POCSO (Amendment) Act 2019**: death penalty for aggravated penetrative sexual assault.
[4:1] - **JJ Act, 2015** (first JJ law **1986**): **16–18** accused of heinous offences → **Juvenile Justice Board** makes a preliminary assessment; **Child Welfare Committees** handle children in need of care and protection; **2021 amendment**: **District Magistrate** issues adoption orders.
[4:2] TABLE head: Law / body | Fact
  [4:2.0] CARA | Statutory under the JJ Act 2015
  [4:2.1] Adoption Regulations 2022 | Surrendered child: 2-month reconsideration period
  [4:2.2] HAMA 1956 | Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs (not Muslims)
  [4:2.3] JJ Act route | Open to all religions
[4:3] - **DPDP Act 2023**: child = below 18; **verifiable parental consent**.
