## SUB 2 | Nobel Prize Basics
[2:0] - **Alfred Nobel** (Swedish chemist) invented **dynamite (1867)** – not cosmic rays (Victor Hess), dynamo, penicillin, typewriter or gramophone.
[2:1] - **Will 1895**; Nobel Foundation **1900**; **first prizes 1901**; instituted by **Sweden** (Peace Prize given in Norway).
[2:2] - **Original five:** Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace. **No Nobel for mathematics.**
[2:3] - **Economics:** set up by **Sveriges Riksbank (Sweden's central bank)** in **1968**, first given **1969** – not in the will.
[2:4] - **How many each year:** **six** (books and keys); strictly five Nobel Prizes + the Riksbank memorial prize.
[2:5] - Laureate status cannot be transferred, shared or withdrawn; only the medal can change hands.
[2:6] - **RDX** (cyclonite, patented **1898**) – **Georg Friedrich Henning**, Germany, not Nobel.

[6:1] - **S.S. Bhatnagar:** "Father of Research Laboratories"; **founder-director and first DG of CSIR**.
[6:2] - **Prize:** by **CSIR since 1958** – India's best-known **science and technology** prize; age limit **45**; **7 disciplines** (biology, chemistry, earth/atmosphere/ocean/planetary, engineering, mathematics, medicine, physics – **not political science**).
[6:3] - **2010 awardees:** included **Sanjeev Galande**. **Madhav Gadgil** (died Jan 2026) – People's Biodiversity Registers, **Gadgil Commission 2010** (Western Ghats).
