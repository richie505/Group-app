=== NOTES 5:113 | Nanotechnology – Nature, Scope, Applications, Nano Mission, Health and Environmental Concerns
## SUB 0 | Nanoscale – Meaning and Properties
[113:0:0] - **1 nm = 10^-9 m** (one billionth of a metre); **0.1-1 nm** = atomic scale.
[113:0:1] - **Nanoparticle size: 1-100 nm** (APPSC 2018) – at least one dimension **less than 10^-7 m**.
[113:0:2] - New properties come from **quantum effects** and a very high **surface-area-to-volume ratio** → more reactive, different optical/electrical behaviour.
[113:0:3] - **Nanogold:** gold nanoparticles look **red, purple or blue**, not golden (surface plasmon effect); used in diagnostics and drug delivery.
[113:0:4] - Most important property of nanomaterials (RAS 2013 key): **friction**.

## SUB 2 | Carbon Allotropes and Nanomaterials
[113:2:0] TABLE head: Allotrope | Bonding / structure | Key property
  [113:2:0.0] Diamond | sp³; tetrahedral 3D network, each C bonded to four | Hardest natural substance (Mohs 10); electrical insulator; excellent thermal conductor; transparent, high refractive index
  [113:2:0.1] Graphite | sp² layers held by weak van der Waals forces | Soft, slippery (layers slide); opaque, black; good conductor; lubricant for heavy machines
  [113:2:0.2] Fullerene C60 | sp² hollow cage; 12 pentagons, 20 hexagons, 60 C atoms | "Buckyball"; semiconductor
  [113:2:0.3] Graphene | Single-atom-thick sp² layer; 2D hexagonal honeycomb | Strongest material; excellent conductor, flexible, transparent
  [113:2:0.4] Carbon nanotubes | Rolled graphene cylinders (1D tubes) | Very strong; carry drugs and antigens
  [113:2:0.5] Amorphous carbon | Coal, charcoal, coke, lampblack | Activated charcoal – high surface area
[113:2:1] - Diamond: least compressible and best heat conductor among natural materials; insulator (no free electrons) – two separate facts.
[113:2:2] - **Wurtzite boron nitride** is believed harder than diamond.
[113:2:3] - **Graphene** = pure **carbon** (not silicon), extracted from graphite.
[113:2:4] - **Fullerene** = carbon allotrope (**not a fluorine compound**).
[113:2:5] # Quantum Structures and Other Nanomaterials
[113:2:6] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [113:2:6.0] Quantum dot | Semiconductor nanostructure, about 1.5-10 nm; size-set colour; displays, solar cells
  [113:2:6.1] Wells / wires / dots | Confinement in 1 / 2 / 3 dimensions (2D / 1D / 0D)
  [113:2:6.2] Nanoporous materials | Separation, catalysis
  [113:2:6.3] Nanofibres | Filtration, smart clothing, scaffolds
  [113:2:6.4] Nanocrystals | LEDs, imaging
  [113:2:6.5] Fullerenes, nanotubes | Lubricants, catalysts
## SUB 3 | Tools and Synthesis
[113:3:0] TABLE head: Tool / route | Fact
  [113:3:0.0] STM | Images atoms on conducting surfaces only
  [113:3:0.1] AFM (1986) | Works on non-conductors too
  [113:3:0.2] Top-down | Cut bulk down – lithography, milling, etching
  [113:3:0.3] Bottom-up | Build from atoms – self-assembly, CVD, sol-gel
## SUB 4 | Generations of Nanoproducts (m.c. Roco)
[113:4:0] TABLE head: Generation | Nature | Examples
  [113:4:0.0] I | Passive nanostructures | Aerosols, colloids, polymers, ceramics, coatings, nanocomposites
  [113:4:0.1] II | Active nanostructures | Targeted drugs, sensors, 3-D transistors, amplifiers
  [113:4:0.2] III | Systems of nanosystems | Robotics, 3-D networking, nanoelectronics, nanomedicine
  [113:4:0.3] IV | Molecular nanosystems | Molecular manufacturing
[113:4:1] Order: Aerosol → Targeted drugs → 3D networking → Molecular manufacturing.
[113:4:2] - **Four generations of nanoproducts:** this classification (passive, active, systems of nanosystems, molecular nanosystems) was given by **M. C. Roco**.
## SUB 5 | Nano Devices – Nanogenerators and Nanosensors
[113:5:0] - **NCL Pune nanogenerator**: electricity when pressure is applied → **piezoelectric effect** (APPSC 2018, 2019).
[113:5:1] - **Quartz crystal in watches** = piezoelectric effect (charge from mechanical stress).
[113:5:2] - **Gold-nanoparticle** lateral-flow kits (Transasia, Mylab): detect **malaria, dengue, typhoid** in 15 minutes.
[113:5:3] - **Graphene** bendable **wearable sensors** (IISc + C-DAC).
[113:5:4] The NCL nanogenerator is piezoelectric – NOT photoelectric or thermoelectric.

## SUB 10 | Concerns and Regulation
[113:10:0] - **Nanotoxicity**: particles enter cells and organs → **oxidative stress, inflammation**, long-term toxicity, **persistence and bioaccumulation**; unclear food-chain entry.
[113:10:1] TABLE head: Type | Examples
  [113:10:1.0] Natural | Volcanic ash, sea spray, smoke
  [113:10:1.1] Engineered | Nano-silver, CNTs, TiO2
[113:10:2] - **No dedicated nano law**; frameworks: **DGFASLI** guidelines (occupational), **FSSAI 2022** guidelines, **SCOMET** list (dual-use).
[113:10:3] - **Guidelines for Evaluation of Nano-based Agri-input and Food Products** (2020): by **DBT**, Agriculture Ministry and **FSSAI**.
[113:10:4] - Concern of a "**nano-divide**".
