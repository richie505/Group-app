## 3. Angles
- **Founding year / first winner / first Indian** (Right Livelihood 1980, Bhanu Athaiya 1983, Nobel 1901, Jnanpith 1961/1965): the most frequent PYQ angle.
- **Winner ↔ country / category** (Abu Zeid – Egypt, Kutch Express – social values, Duplantis – Sportsman 2025).
- **Statements on one laureate** (Buchner I/II/III): pick the true pair.
- **Book ↔ prize ↔ author** (The Vegetarian – International Booker; Voldemort – Rowling).
- App MCQs add NOT-correct pairs, assertion-reason, chronology (Miss Universe, Oscar milestones) and number questions (Padma 131 = 5 + 13 + 113).
