# Rules and Background
- **Article 18(1)**: no titles except **military and academic distinctions**.
- **Balaji Raghavan v. Union of India (1996)**: national awards are **not titles**; they cannot be used as a **prefix or suffix**.
- Padma Awards instituted **1954**; **suspended 1977–80 and 1992–95**.
- **Bharat Ratna**: **maximum three per year**; last given **2024** (**5 awardees**, incl. **P.V. Narasimha Rao**).
