# Medicine 2026 - Optogenetics
- Protein used: **Channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR-2)** from the green alga **Chlamydomonas** (its eyespot) - a "**molecular light switch**" that opens in about **0.2 ms**.
- Alga species: **Chlamydomonas reinhardtii**, a **unicellular green alga**; field - **neuroscience**.
- **Hegemann** studied Chlamydomonas (early 1990s); **Nagel** expressed ChR-1/ChR-2 in **frog egg cells**; their joint paper - **2003**.
- **Deisseroth** put ChR-2 into **rat neurons (2005)**, coined the name "**optogenetics**" (**2006**), and controlled **mouse whiskers (2007)**.
- **Francis Crick** (1970s) first proposed using light to control neurons.
# Physics and Chemistry 2026
- Physics: **Francis Halzen (Belgium)** - IceCube Neutrino Observatory.
- Chemistry: **Kagan (France)** and **Soai (Japan)** - asymmetric synthesis, autocatalysis.
- Prize money 2026: **12 million Swedish kronor** (about **US$1.2 million**); ceremony on **10 December** (Alfred Nobel's death anniversary).
# Literature 2026 - Anne Carson
- **Anne Carson (Canada)** - writer and poet.
- 2025 winner: **László Krasznahorkai (Hungary)**.
- **19 women** have won the Literature prize.
- First Literature recipient: **Sully Prudhomme (France, 1901)**; first woman: **Selma Lagerlöf (1909)**; first Asian: **Rabindranath Tagore (1913, *Gitanjali*)**.
# Peace 2026 - Navi Pillay
- Announced **9 October 2026** in **Oslo** by Norwegian Nobel Committee chair **Jørgen Watne Frydnes**.
- Ceremony: **Oslo, 10 December 2026**; prize **12 million SEK (~US$1.2 mn)**.
- 2025 winner: **María Corina Machado (Venezuela)** - democracy movement; she gave her medal to **Donald Trump** in **January 2026**.
- The **five-member Norwegian Nobel Committee** is appointed by the **Storting** (Norway's Parliament).
