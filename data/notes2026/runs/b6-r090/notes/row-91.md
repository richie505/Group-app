# Background and Previous Editions
- NFA established **1954** (first awards for films of 1953); presented by the **President**; implemented by the **Ministry of I&B**; the **Directorate of Film Festivals** (est. **1973**) organises the NFA and **IFFI**.
- Acting awards from **1968** (for films of **1967**): first winners **Nargis Dutt (Urvashi)** and **Uttam Kumar (Bharat)**.
- **71st NFA** (for 2023; held Sep 2025): Best Feature **12th Fail**; **Shah Rukh Khan** and **Vikrant Massey** shared Best Actor; **Rani Mukerji** Best Actress; **Mohanlal** got the **Phalke Award**.
- **70th NFA** (2022): **Kutch Express** (Gujarati, dir. Viral Shah) - **Best Feature Film Promoting National, Social and Environmental Values**; it also won Best Actress (Manasi Parekh) and Costume Design. Not *Hellaro* or *Chhello Show*.
