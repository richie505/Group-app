[0:18] # Rules and Background
[0:19] - **Article 18(1)**: no titles except **military and academic distinctions**.
[0:20] - **Balaji Raghavan v. Union of India (1996)**: national awards are **not titles**; they cannot be used as a **prefix or suffix**.
[0:21] - Padma Awards instituted **1954**; **suspended 1977–80 and 1992–95**.
[0:22] - **Bharat Ratna**: **maximum three per year**; last given **2024** (**5 awardees**, incl. **P.V. Narasimha Rao**).
