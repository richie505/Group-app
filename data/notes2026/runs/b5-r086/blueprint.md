## Trends
- **Recall of "the main cause" dominates the causes questions.** The answer is always habitat destruction, worded in different ways, and NOT-a-cause items test what restores balance instead.
- **IUCN status questions are where keys go stale or are disputed.** UPSC 2012's "endangered group" now holds a CR (GIB) and an NT (wild ass) species. UPSC 2013's gharial/leatherback/swamp-deer question is keyed "all three" by the official/most keys, but PMF keys it "none" (strict IUCN reading).
- APPSC repeats **Red List terminology** (CR, the name "Red List", IUCN full form) and **AP species** (Pondicherry shark at Kakinada, red sanders, Gooty tarantula).
- **Invasives:** water hyacinth and Prosopis repeat; UPSC 2024 added the native-vs-introduced angle.
