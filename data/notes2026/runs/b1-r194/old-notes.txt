## SUB 0 | Jyotiba Phule and the Satyashodhak Samaj
[194:0:0] # Jyotiba Phule (1827-1890)
[194:0:1] - Born **1827**, a gardener's son – **Mali community**, Maharashtra.
[194:0:2] - Title **"Mahatma"** given by **Vithalrao Krishnaji Vandekar (1888)**; called **"Father of Indian Social Revolution"**.
[194:0:3] - **1848**: girls' school, **Pune**; taught by **Savitribai Phule** – **India's first woman teacher**. The Indian women's movement was inspired largely by Phule.
[194:0:4] - **Savitribai** headed the Samaj's women's section; worked for lower-caste girls.
[194:0:5] # Satyashodhak Samaj ("Society of Truth Seekers")
[194:0:6] - Founded **24 September 1873, Pune**; Phule its **first president**; anti-caste movement in Maharashtra.
[194:0:7] - Worked for **Shudras and Ati-Shudras** against **Arya-Brahmin** exploitation (not for Arya-Brahmins).
[194:0:8] - Objectives: end caste and untouchability; break **priestly monopoly** over ritual; education for women, Shudras, Ati-Shudras; social service.
[194:0:9] - **Satyashodhak marriages**: **without priests**; allowed **inter-caste** marriage.
[194:0:10] - Symbol: **King Bali** (the oppressed) against the Brahmanical symbol of **Rama**.
[194:0:11] - **Din Bandhu** (Marathi weekly, **Pune, 1877**) – **Krishnarao Bhalekar**; spread the movement to remote Maharashtra; later edited from Bombay by **N.M. Lokhande**.
[194:0:12] # Works of Phule

  [194:0:13.0] Gulamgiri (1873) | "Slavery"; compared caste oppression with slavery

  [194:0:13.2] Sarvajanik Satyadharma Pustak | Published posthumously, 1891

[194:0:14] Phule's symbol = **King Bali**; Justice Party symbol = **Scales**.

## SUB 3 | Temple Entry and Rights Satyagrahas
[194:3:0] # Vaikom Satyagraha (1924-25)
[194:3:1] - Place: **Vaikom (Travancore, Kerala)**.
[194:3:2] - Issue: right of lower castes (Ezhavas and others) to use the **roads around the Vaikom temple** – part of the **Temple Entry Movement** (APPSC-G2 2023).
[194:3:3] - Leaders: **T.K. Madhavan, K.P. Kesava Menon, K. Kelappan**; backed by Narayana Guru.
[194:3:4] - **Periyar** jailed → title **"Vaikom Veerar"**.
[194:3:5] - **Gandhi**: visited in **1925**, negotiated with Travancore → compromise opened some roads; **not directly a participant** (Gandhi led Rajkot, Kheda, Non-cooperation, not Vaikom).
[194:3:6] - Outcome → **Travancore Temple Entry Proclamation, 1936**.

[194:4:4] - Poona Pact: separate electorates of the **Communal Award** replaced by **reserved seats in joint electorates** – **148 seats** vs **71**; accepted unwillingly **to save Gandhi's life**.
[194:4:5] - **Neo-Buddhism**: mass conversion at **Deeksha Bhoomi, Nagpur, 14 Oct 1956**, with **22 vows**.
[194:4:6] - Sought annihilation of caste, legal rights, separate electorates; **Gandhi** saw varna as division of labour and untouchability as a corruption.

## SUB 5 | Justice Party (south Indian Liberal Federation)
[194:5:0] # Background, Aim and Social Base
[194:5:1] - Brahmins about **3%** of Madras Presidency population but over **90%** of government jobs.
[194:5:2] - Aim: **protect interests of non-Brahmins** (APPSC 2008); end Brahmin dominance in jobs, education, politics, religion.
[194:5:3] - Social base: non-Brahmin landed gentry, **intermediate landed castes** (Andhra: **Reddys, Kammas, Kapus**). Dalits built a parallel "Adi" movement.
[194:5:4] - British backed it partly to weaken **Home Rule**; used against Home Rule and Gandhi's movements.
[194:5:5] - **Andhra Mahasabha (Vijayawada, 1917)** sought reserved seats for non-Brahmins – **Ponaka Pattabhirama Reddy** and **Tripuraneni Ramaswamy Chowdary**.

## SUB 7 | Adi-andhra and Adi-hindu Movements
[194:7:0] # Terms
[194:7:1] - **"Adi"** = original inhabitants; rejected "Panchama". **Adi-Andhra** = Dalits of the Andhra (Madras) region; **Adi-Hindu** = Dalits of Hyderabad State.
[194:7:2] - Early root: **Adi-Andhra Sabha (1890)**, first called the Pariya Mahasabha.
[194:7:3] # Bhagya Reddy Varma (born 1888, Hyderabad)
[194:7:4] - Original name **Madari Bhagaiah**; **"Father of the Dalit movement"** in Hyderabad/Andhra; coined **Adi-Andhra and Adi-Hindu**.
[194:7:5] - **Jagan Mitra Mandali (1906)** → Manya Sangham (1911) → **Adi-Hindu Social Service League**; journal **Adi Hindu**.
[194:7:6] - **Sahapankti bhojanams** and **Buddhism** for Dalit consciousness; against child marriage, liquor, Devadasi system.
[194:7:7] - Presided over the **All India Adi Hindu Conference, Lucknow, 1930** – chose **Ambedkar** for the Round Table Conferences.
