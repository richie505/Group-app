## 3. Repeats and traps
- **Satyashodhak founder** asked 9 times; "anti-caste movement in Maharashtra" (UPSC 2016) printed 3 times.
- **Justice Party first CM** = A. Subbarayalu Reddiar (×3); Thyagaraya Chetty, the party president, declined office – the tempting wrong option.
- **Bobbili Raja (1932)** opposed a separate Andhra state (×2); **Panagal Raja** = Cooum boundary remark.
- **SNDP demands (CSE 1998):** the printed key B, C, D was wrong – Spectrum lists schools, government jobs, roads/temple entry and political representation → A, B, D.
- **Depressed-class bodies:** League = Jagjivan Ram; Association = M.C. Rajah; Federation = Ambedkar; Anti-Untouchability League = Gandhi; BHS = Ambedkar, not Phule.
- **1917 Vijayawada Panchama sabha:** sources split on who presided (South First: Sundru Venkaiah; Samvit Kendra: Bhagya Reddy Varma) – disputed.
- **Stem errors:** "Jagan Mitra Mandali in 1960" (= 1906); "Kudi Arasu, May 1924" (usually May 1925); "one nation, one God, one religion" (= Narayana Guru's "one caste, one religion, one God").
