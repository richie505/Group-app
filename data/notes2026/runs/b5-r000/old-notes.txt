## SUB 1 | Characteristics of Science
[0:1:0] - Science: **empirical, testable (falsifiable), reproducible, systematic (orderly), self-correcting**; needs honesty and commitment to practical ideals.
[0:1:1] - **Pseudoscience**: claims scientific status but **cannot be tested**.
[0:1:2] - **J.V. Narlikar** (astrophysicist): **astrology** has not been considered a science (UPPSC 2018).
[0:1:3] - Considered sciences: **astronomy** (celestial bodies), **cosmology** (origin and structure of the universe), **nanotechnology** (1-100 nm).
## SUB 2 | Scientific Method
[0:2:0] - **Observation, question, hypothesis, experiment, analysis, conclusion**, then replication / peer review.
[0:2:1] - **Theory** = well-tested explanation of **why**; **Law** = what always happens, often mathematical (Newton's laws).
[0:2:2] A theory does **not** "become" a law.
