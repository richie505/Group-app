  [161:0:1.0] NSG | 1975 | 48 | Nuclear materials and technology
  [161:0:1.1] MTCR | 1987 | 35 | Missiles and drones – 500 kg payload over 300 km
  [161:0:1.2] Wassenaar Arrangement | 1996, Vienna | 42 | Conventional arms and dual-use goods
  [161:0:1.3] Australia Group | 1985 | – | Chemical and biological weapon precursors
  [161:0:1.4] Hague Code of Conduct | 2002 | – | Transparency on ballistic-missile proliferation
[161:0:2] MTCR = 500 kg / 300 km; NSG = 48; Wassenaar = 42, Vienna
[161:0:3] # Outsiders
[161:0:4] - **China and Pakistan** are outside both **Wassenaar and MTCR**; **North Korea** is outside MTCR.
[161:0:5] - North Korea admitted exporting missiles to **Pakistan and Iran** and asked the US for **compensation** to stop their further spread.
## SUB 1 | India's Membership Status

  [161:1:0.0] MTCR | Member – joined June 2016
  [161:1:0.1] Wassenaar | Member – 42nd, December 2017

  [161:1:0.3] Hague Code of Conduct | Joined
  [161:1:0.4] NSG | Not a member – only the 2008 India-specific waiver
[161:1:1] - India = **"de facto responsible nuclear state"**: **No First Use** doctrine, export controls, entry into **3 of the 4** major regimes.
[161:1:2] India is in MTCR, Wassenaar and Australia Group, but **not in the NSG**.
## SUB 2 | Nuclear Suppliers Group
[161:2:0] - Formed **1975**, known as the **"London Club"**, in reply to India's **1974 Pokhran-I** test.
[161:2:1] - **Voluntary, consensus-based**; **48 members**; **no permanent secretariat**.
[161:2:2] - **2008 India-specific waiver** (India-US Civil Nuclear Deal): allows civil nuclear trade, import of uranium and reactors.
[161:2:3] - The waiver does **not** make India an NPT member.
[161:2:4] - India's entry is blocked by the **consensus rule**; **Australia backs** India's NSG bid. **Rafael Grossi** chaired the NSG in 2016.
## SUB 3 | Npt and Ctbt
[161:3:0] # NPT
[161:3:1] - **Signed 1968**, **in force 1970**, **extended indefinitely in 1995**.
[161:3:2] - Recognises **five Nuclear Weapon States** – those that **developed and tested before 1 January 1967**: **US, Russia, UK, France, China** – the same as the **UNSC P5**.
[161:3:3] - Of China, France, India, Israel, Pakistan: **only China and France** are NPT-recognised NWS.
[161:3:4] - **North Korea left the NPT in 2003**.
[161:3:5] - **Review Conferences** every **5 years**; the consensus rule lets single states block outcomes.
[161:3:6] - India rejects the NPT as discriminatory – **"nuclear apartheid"**; wants a **time-bound disarmament plan** before signing.
[161:3:7] - Criticism: NPT lets recognised powers modernise without disarmament deadlines.
[161:3:8] # CTBT
[161:3:9] - Adopted **1996**; bans **all nuclear explosions**; **not in force**.
[161:3:10] - India has **not signed**: no time-bound disarmament promise; it still allows **subcritical tests**.
## SUB 4 | Arms-control Treaties and Nuclear Deals
[161:4:0] TABLE head: Treaty | Parties | Status
  [161:4:0.0] JCPOA (2015, not 2010) | Iran with P5+1 (US, UK, France, Russia, China, Germany) and EU | US left 2018; Iran dropped all limits 5 Jan 2020
  [161:4:0.1] New START (signed April 2010) | USA and Russia (not China) | Russia paused it 2023; its expiry (2026) removed all binding limits
  [161:4:0.2] INF Treaty (1987) | USA and USSR | US left 2019
  [161:4:0.3] CCW (1980) | UN convention on inhumane conventional weapons | India is party to all five protocols
[161:4:1] - **START** = **Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty**. New START is a bilateral treaty limiting **deployed strategic nuclear warheads**; signed by **Barack Obama**; the last nuclear arms-control pact left between the US and Russia.
[161:4:2] - **US + Russia** hold about **90%** of global arsenals.
[161:4:3] - **Iran:** US and Israel struck its nuclear sites **June 2025**; Iran's Parliament weighed **NPT exit** (Mar 2026).
## SUB 5 | Iaea and India's Safeguards
[161:5:0] - **IAEA:** HQ **Vienna**, established **1957**; India a **founding member (1957)**; verifies that civil nuclear material is not diverted to weapons.
[161:5:1] - DG **Rafael Grossi** voiced worry over strikes on Iran's nuclear sites (Apr 2026).

## SUB 7 | Fatf
[161:7:0] - Set up **1989 by G7**; secretariat at **Paris (OECD)**.
[161:7:1] - **Grey list** = jurisdictions under **increased monitoring** (not by itself a call for action).
[161:7:2] - **Black list** = **high-risk jurisdictions subject to a call for action** – Iran, North Korea, Myanmar (unchanged at the Oct 2026 plenary).
[161:7:3] - Oct 2026: grey list fell from 22 to **18** – DRC, Monaco, Bulgaria, Côte d'Ivoire removed.
[161:7:4] - **Pakistan:** grey list **June 2018 – October 2022** for failing its **27-point action plan** on terror financing.
[161:7:5] - **India:** member since **2010**; **mutual evaluation adopted June 2024** → **"regular follow-up"**; also in the **APG** (Asia/Pacific Group on Money Laundering, a FATF-style regional body).
## SUB 8 | Tpnw, Fmct and Nuclear-weapon-free Zones
[161:8:0] - **TPNW:** first binding ban on nuclear weapons; **in force 2021**; no nuclear-armed or NATO support. **India did not sign** – says it adds nothing to customary international law.
[161:8:1] - **FMCT:** **India supports** negotiation at the **Conference on Disarmament** – universal, verifiable, non-discriminatory, not capping existing stocks.
[161:8:2] "India does not support FMCT negotiations" is **false**.
