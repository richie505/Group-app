## SUB 1 | UN Peacekeeping
[1:0] - **Three principles:** **consent** of the parties, **impartiality**, **no use of force except in self-defence and defence of the mandate**.
[1:1] - **UNSC** creates and ends missions by resolution; **Department of Peace Operations** manages them; head = **Under-Secretary-General Jean-Pierre Lacroix** (since 2017, succeeded Hervé Ladsous; still in post 2026).
[1:2] - **CUNPK, New Delhi** (Indian Army) trains Indian and foreign peacekeepers.

  [1:3.0] UNMIL | Liberia | 2003-2018
  [1:3.1] MINURCAT | CAR and Chad (not Haiti) | 2007-2010

  [1:3.3] UNMISET | Timor-Leste | 2002-2005
  [1:3.4] UNMISS | South Sudan | 2011-
  [1:3.5] MONUSCO | DR Congo | 2010-
  [1:3.6] UNIFIL | Lebanon | 1978-
[1:4] - First contribution: **Korea, 1950**; largest contingent: **ONUC Congo (1960-64)**.
[1:5] - India: **largest cumulative contributor** (2,90,000+ personnel since 1950); in monthly rankings India is now **fourth-largest** (2026). Around **2011** the top contributors were **Pakistan and Bangladesh**, with India third.
[1:6] - **UNMISS:** India largest contributor (**1,775**). **All-women Formed Police Unit:** **Liberia, 2007**. **IPKF** (Sri Lanka) was not a UN force.

## SUB 4 | Diaspora Data and Mobility
[4:0] - India has the **largest diaspora** (~35 million); major hosts **USA (~5.4 million)**, **UAE (~3.5 million – largest NRI population)**, Malaysia.
[4:1] - India is the **top remittance recipient** – about **$129-135 billion (2024-25)**.
[4:2] - Indians lead Fortune 500 firms – **Satya Nadella (Microsoft)**, Sundar Pichai (Google); India is the **largest supplier of foreign medical graduates** to the US and UK.
[4:3] - Indian-born now Australia's largest immigrant community (Jul 2026).
[4:4] - **Hong Kong** ended visa-free entry for Indians (pre-arrival registration from **23 January 2017**), citing rising **asylum claims / refugee problem**.
[4:5] - **APNRTS:** set up by the AP Government in **May 2016**, chaired by the CM.
[4:6] > Exam angle: largest host / remittances; visa curbs and their stated reasons.
