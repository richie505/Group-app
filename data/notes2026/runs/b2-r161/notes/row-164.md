=== Border Management, CAPFs and Coastal Security
- **Department of Border Management** (2004) – **Ministry of Home Affairs**; MHA controls all **CAPFs** ("One Border, One Force").
- **Ministry of Defence** controls the Army (LoC, LAC) and the **Coast Guard**.
| Force | Border / role | Raised |
|---|---|---|
| **BSF** | **Pakistan and Bangladesh** | **1 Dec 1965** |
| ITBP | China (LAC), 3,488 km, Karakoram Pass to Jachep La | 1962 |
| SSB | Nepal and Bhutan; newest border force | 1963 |
| **Assam Rifles** | **Myanmar**; oldest paramilitary; HQ Shillong | 1835 |
| **CRPF** | Internal security, LWE; under **MHA**; began 27 July 1939 as Crown Representative's Police | 1939 |
| **CISF** | Critical infrastructure, airports; under **MHA**; **Infosys** = first private company with CISF cover (**July 2009**) | 1969 |
| **Coast Guard** | Territorial waters; under **MoD** (not Navy, not MHA) | 1977 |
- **Paramilitary forces** (old list): **Assam Rifles, Indian Coast Guard**, SFF – not the Directorate General of Resettlement.
- **Indian Coast Guard** = central coordinating agency for **marine oil-spill** response.
- **Home Guards:** raised under **State/UT Home Guards Acts** (not a central Act); **auxiliary to the police** for internal security; **Border Wing Home Guards battalions** in border States (UPSC 2023: only two correct).
- **Police and Public Order** = State List, **Seventh Schedule**; BSF jurisdiction in Punjab and West Bengal 15 km → **50 km**.
- **Post-26/11 tiers:** Navy – overall; Coast Guard – territorial waters; coastal police – shallow waters. **Sagar Kavach** biannual exercise.
- **CIBMS** smart fencing (Pakistan, Bangladesh); **Vibrant Villages** I – China border; **VVP-II (2024-25 to 2028-29)** – other land borders; **PM-DevINE** for the North East.
- AP: 6 marine police stations sanctioned in Phase I (2005) of the Coastal Security Scheme.

=== Defence Set-up – CDS, Commands, Institutions, Agnipath
- **CDS:** post created **Dec 2019**; first **Gen Bipin Rawat (1 Jan 2020)**; now **Gen Anil Chauhan**; heads the **Department of Military Affairs**; **Permanent Chairman, Chiefs of Staff Committee**; member of **Defence Planning Committee**; **principal military adviser to the Defence Minister** on tri-service matters; **maximum age 65**.
- The **Defence Minister**, not the CDS, chairs the **Defence Acquisition Council**.
- Before the CDS, the **senior-most Service Chief** chaired the Chiefs of Staff Committee.
- **Agnipath (2022):** **four-year** recruitment of Agniveers.
- Army commands: Northern, Western, **South Western**, Southern, Eastern, **Central**, **ARTRAC (Army Training Command)** – there is **no "South-Eastern Command"**. Navy: Western (Mumbai), Eastern (Vizag), **Southern (Kochi)**.

=== Left-wing Extremism, Greyhounds and the North-East
- **People's War Group:** **Andhra Pradesh**; Kondapalli Seetharamaiah, 22 April 1980; merged into CPI (Maoist) 2004.
- **Greyhounds:** AP's anti-Naxal force, raised **1989**; operational and training base **Anandapuram (Visakhapatnam)**; cut Naxal activity in Alluri Sitharama Raju and Parvathipuram Manyam; AOB.
- **CoBRA** (Commando Battalion for Resolute Action) = **CRPF**'s anti-Naxal force; OCTOPUS = AP's counter-terror force; Salwa Judum = Chhattisgarh militia.
- **Legacy Thrust Territories** (MHA): develop areas freed of LWE so it does not return.
- **Manipur:** **Meitei vs Kuki**, since **May 2023**. NE accords – Bodo, Bru-Reang, NLFT.
- Drug routes: **Golden Crescent** – Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan; **Golden Triangle** – Myanmar, Laos, **Thailand**. Terror financing: hawala, **FICN** (fake Indian currency notes), narco-terror.
> Exam angle: Greyhounds base = Visakhapatnam; CoBRA = CRPF.
