## SUB 2 | NALSA v. Union of India (2014) and Related Judgements
[2:0] - Delivered **15 April 2014** by Justices **K.S. Radhakrishnan** and **A.K. Sikri**; recognised transgender persons as the **"third gender"**.
[2:1] - **Gender identity is self-determined** – not by biological examination.
[2:2] - Directions: treat as **SEBC** for reservation (**vertical, OBC-type** quota); separate **public toilets**, health facilities, welfare schemes.
[2:3] TABLE head: Case | Year | Issue decided
  [2:3.0] K.S. Puttaswamy | 2017 | Nine-judge bench: privacy a fundamental right (Art. 21)
  [2:3.1] Navtej Singh Johar | 2018 | Decriminalised same-sex relations (Section 377 IPC)
  [2:3.2] Supriyo v. Union of India | 17 Oct 2023 | 5-judge bench, 3:2: no legal recognition of same-sex marriage; left to Parliament

## SUB 3 | Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019 and 2026 Amendment
[3:0] TABLE head: Year | Event
  [3:0.0] 2014 | Rights of Transgender Persons Bill – private member's Bill by Tiruchi Siva (DMK)
  [3:0.1] April 2015 | Rajya Sabha passed it – first private member's Bill passed there in over four decades; not passed by Lok Sabha
  [3:0.2] 2019 | Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act; in force 10 January 2020
  [3:0.3] 13 Mar 2026 | Amendment Bill introduced by Virendra Kumar in Lok Sabha
[3:1] - **Definition:** includes trans men/women, **intersex variations**, genderqueer and socio-cultural identities (kinner, hijra, aravani, jogta); administered by **MoSJE**.
[3:2] - **Certificate of identity:** **District Magistrate**; revised certificate **after surgery**; application through the **National Portal for Transgender Persons** (Rules 2020).
[3:3] - Offences (forced labour, abuse etc.) = **6 months to 2 years + fine**; criticism: lighter penalties, DM gatekeeping, **no reservation**.
[3:4] - **2026 Amendment:** self-perceived identity **excluded** (Sec. 4(2) omitted); certificate by DM on a medical board's advice – criticised for **reversing NALSA's self-identification**; challenged by **Laxmi Narayan Tripathi** and **Zainab Javid Patel**.
[3:5] - **National Council for Transgender Persons:** statutory (Sec. 16, 2019 Act), constituted **21 August 2020**; chair = **Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment**; non-official term **3 years**.
[3:6] - **NHRC Advisory 2.0** (May 2026): separate **intersex, trans men, trans women** categories in Census 2027.
[3:7] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:143 sub 2-4
## SUB 4 | SMILE, Garima Greh and Data
[4:0] - **SMILE** (Support for Marginalized Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise): 2022, **MoSJE**; outlay **Rs 365 crore**; goal **"Bhiksha Vritti Mukt Bharat"**; skilling via **PM-DAKSH**.
[4:1] - **Garima Greh** = **shelter homes** for transgender persons – **23 in 17** States/UTs (ES 2025-26).
[4:2] - **Census 2011:** about **4.88 lakh** transgender persons; highest **Uttar Pradesh**.
[4:3] - **NTR Bharosa Pension (AP):** **Rs 4,000/month** for elderly (60+), widows and transgender persons.
