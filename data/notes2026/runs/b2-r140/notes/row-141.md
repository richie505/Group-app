=== NSAP and Old-age Pensions
# National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)
- Launched **15 August 1995** (first national social assistance programme); ministry **Rural Development (MoRD)**, not Social Justice; rests on **Articles 41 and 42**.
- Cabinet approved continuation **FY 2026-27 to 2030-31** (outlay ₹53,428 crore); renamed **Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana** (sub-schemes e.g. PM Vriddhavastha Pension Yojana, PM Divyangjan Pension Yojana) (2026).
- Five components:
  - 1. **IGNOAPS** – old-age pension (BPL, 60+): Rs 200/month (60-79), Rs 500 (80+); Centre's share frozen at Rs 200 since 2007; about 2.2 crore beneficiaries (June 2026).
  - 2. **IGNWPS** – widow pension (BPL widows 40-79).
  - 3. **IGNDPS** – disability pension (80% or more disability).
  - 4. **NFBS** – lump sum on death of the primary breadwinner (Rs 20,000).
  - 5. **Annapurna** – **10 kg free food grains** a month to seniors eligible for but not getting IGNOAPS.

=== Central Schemes for the Elderly
| Scheme | Ministry / agency | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana** | MoSJE; ALIMCO | Free physical aids (walking sticks, wheelchairs, hearing aids, dentures) to BPL seniors (60+, or income up to Rs 15,000/month); funded from the **Senior Citizens' Welfare Fund** (unclaimed deposits); at least **30% women** per district; launched **1 April 2017 at Nellore, AP** |
| AVYAY (Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana) | MoSJE | April 2021, renamed from National Action Plan for Senior Citizens; umbrella of **4**: Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens, State Action Plan, RVY, Elder Line; Rs 287.81 crore utilised (ES 2025-26) |
| **PMVVY (PM Vaya Vandana Yojana)** | **Ministry of Finance; LIC** | 2017; social security in old age with **assured pension 8% for 10 years**; max purchase Rs 7.5 lakh at launch (raised to **Rs 15 lakh** in 2018); closed for new subscription after 31 March 2023 |
| **Varishtha Pension Bima Yojana** | LIC (Budget 2003-04) | Launched 14 July 2003 for **senior citizens** (55+ in 2003; 60+ in the 2014 version); single premium, assured 9% return |
| Senior Citizen Savings Scheme | India Post / banks | Safe fixed-interest savings for seniors |
| Ayushman Vay Vandana card | MoHFW (AB-PMJAY) | Health cover of **Rs 5 lakh** for **all aged 70+, irrespective of income** (2024) |
- Matching set (2023): **PMFBY** = crop insurance; **PMVVY** = old-age pension at 8%; **PMJDY** = basic bank account for every unbanked household.
- **IPOP / Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC):** up to **90%** of project cost to **NGOs** for old-age homes, day-care centres, mobile medicare units.
- **SAGE** (Seniorcare Ageing Growth Engine): up to **Rs 1 crore** equity for elder-care start-ups (Govt stake below 49%) (Aug 2026).
- **SACRED portal**: re-employment of senior citizens.
> Trap: PMVVY = pension (LIC, Finance); Vay Vandana = health (70+); Vayoshri = aids (ALIMCO); Varishtha = senior citizens, not the disabled.

=== Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007
- Enacted in **2007**; nodal ministry **MoSJE**; brought into force **state by state** from **29 December 2007**.
- Creates a **legal obligation** on adult **children (biological, adopted, step)** and **legal heirs** to maintain parents and senior citizens who cannot maintain themselves.
- The Act **punishes children for neglecting old parents**: **abandonment** of a senior citizen – up to **3 months** jail and/or **Rs 5,000** fine.
- **Old age homes:** at least **one per district**.
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| Section 20 | Medical care of senior citizens; basis of the NPHCE |
| Section 23 | Property transfer made on condition of care can be declared void by the Tribunal if the transferee neglects the parent |
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Maintenance Tribunal | Sub-division level; in AP headed by RDO / Sub-Collector |
| Appellate Tribunal | District level; in AP headed by the District Collector |
| Maximum maintenance | Rs 10,000 per month |
| Time limit | Case decided within 90 days |
- **2019 Amendment Bill:** proposed **removing the Rs 10,000 cap** and covering **in-laws and grandchildren**; it **lapsed** (not passed).
- **First State law:** **Himachal Pradesh** Maintenance of Parents and Dependents Act, **2001**.

=== Elder Line, Ageing Data and Elder Abuse
- **Elder Line 14567** – National Helpline for Senior Citizens; **MoSJE**; component of **AVYAY**; technical support from **Tata Trusts** and **NSE Foundation**.
- **LASI** (Longitudinal Ageing Study in India), Wave 1 **2017-18**, by **IIPS, Mumbai**, under MoHFW; about **72,250** persons aged **45+** and their spouses.
- **India Ageing Report 2023** – by **UNFPA** with **IIPS** (not HelpAge India): elderly to **exceed children (0-14) by 2046**; feminisation and ruralisation of ageing.
- **AP elderly share:** about **12.4%** of AP's population aged **60+** in **2021** (*Elderly in India 2021*, NSO); India **10.1%**; Kerala 16.5%.
- **HelpAge India:** elderly-care services; releases **elder-abuse surveys**.
- **Shyam Saran Negi** (Kalpa, Kinnaur, HP) – independent India's **first voter** (Oct 1951).
