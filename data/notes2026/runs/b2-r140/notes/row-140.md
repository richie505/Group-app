=== Schemes, Portals and Initiatives for Persons with Disabilities
# Campaigns and portals (DEPwD, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment)
| Scheme / portal | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Accessible India Campaign = Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan** | For **persons with disabilities**; launched **3 December 2015**; DEPwD flagship; universal accessibility in built environment, transport and ICT |
| Sugamya Bharat App | Crowdsourced reporting of accessibility gaps in buildings, transport, infrastructure |
| UDID (Swavlamban portal) | Single national disability ID and digital certificate; **centralised national database** |
| ADIP | Free aids and appliances (plus corrective surgery) below income ₹22,500/month; subsidised up to ₹30,000 |
| DDRS | Grants-in-aid to NGOs for rehabilitation |
| SIPDA | Grant-in-aid to implement the RPwD Act |
| PM-DAKSH-DEPwD portal (2023) | UDID-based skilling + Divyangjan Rozgar Setu (jobs); MoSJE, not Minority Affairs |
| IGNDPS (NSAP) | Pension to severely disabled BPL persons (MoRD) |
| Scholarships | 6 components: pre-matric, post-matric, Top Class, National Fellowship, Overseas, Free Coaching |
| **PRASHAST App** | **NCERT / Dept. of School Education & Literacy** (September 2022), not DEPwD; teachers screen children for **disability conditions** (the 21 RPwD disabilities) |
| Sugamya Pustakalaya | Online library of accessible books – DAISY, audio, Braille |
- The three verticals of the Accessible India Campaign: **built environment, transport, ICT**.
- **Accessible elections:** accessible booths, **Braille-enabled EVMs**.
- 3 December = International Day of Persons with Disabilities **and** launch of the Accessible India Campaign (2015).
# Inclusion and entrepreneurship
- **Inclusive India initiative** (MoSJE / **National Trust**): brings persons with **intellectual and developmental disabilities** into the mainstream (inclusive education, employment, community life) – not women, PVTGs or the third gender.
- **EKAM Fest** (**E**ntrepreneurship, **K**nowledge, **A**wareness, **M**arketing): organised by **NHFDC** (now NDFDC), first held 2-9 March 2020 in New Delhi; promotes **Divyangjan entrepreneurship** and awareness of the potential of PwDs.
- **Divya Kala Shakti** (DEPwD): cultural showcase of the artistic talent of PwDs.
- **Indian Sign Language Dictionary** (ISLRTC, DEPwD): 1st edition March 2018 (~3,000 terms); **2nd edition 27 February 2019** (~6,000 terms – the edition asked as "released in March 2019"); 3rd edition February 2021 (10,000 terms).
- **"Access to libraries for persons with disabilities – Checklist"**: developed by **IFLA** (International Federation of Library Associations), Professional Report No. 89, 2005.
# Inclusive education
- **Special education** targets **children with disabilities (CwSN)**, not gifted children.
- **Samagra Shiksha** inclusive component: assessment, aids, assistive technology, therapy for all RPwD disabilities – exclusive education of gifted children is **not** part of it.
> Trap: PRASHAST = Education ministry/NCERT, not DEPwD; ADIP = aids to individuals; DDRS = grants to NGOs; EKAM = entrepreneurship.

=== RPwD Act 2016 and the Disability Laws
# Laws and milestones
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1981 | UN International Year of Disabled Persons |
| **1992** | **Rehabilitation Council of India Act** – constitution of the RCI for **regulating the training of rehabilitation professionals** (statutory body from 1993) |
| 1995 | **PwD (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act** – first comprehensive, welfare-based law; **7** disabilities; created the Chief Commissioner |
| 1999 | **National Trust Act** – autism, cerebral palsy, mental retardation, multiple disabilities |
| 2006 | First National Policy for PwDs |
| 13 Dec 2006 | UNGA adopted the UNCRPD |
| Oct 2007 | India ratified the UNCRPD |
| 2010 | **Sudha Kaul Committee** (MoSJE) drafted the replacement Bill |
| **2016** | **Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act** enacted (Bill passed by Parliament December 2016) |
| 19 Apr 2017 | RPwD Act in force |
- Order: UNCRPD adopted → India ratified → RPwD enacted → in force.
# RPwD Act 2016 – key provisions
- **First rights-based** disability law; **replaced the 1995 Act**; meets UNCRPD obligations; bars discrimination in government employment.
- The 2016 law is named **"The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act"** – the 1995 law was "Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act".
- **Sec. 2(s)**: PwD = long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairment which, with barriers, blocks full participation.
| Provision | Value |
|---|---|
| Specified disabilities | **21 (from 7)**, e.g. autism, acid attack victims, sickle cell disease; Centre may add |
| Benchmark disability | At least 40% |
| Government jobs | 4% |
| Higher education | 5% seats |
| Free education | Benchmark-disability children 6–18 |
| Monitoring | Chief Commissioner for PwDs, Sec. 75(1) |
- Survivors of **forcible acid ingestion** recognised as acid attack victims (SC, 5 May 2026).
> Trap: passed **2016** (not 2014/2018), in force 19 April 2017; 4% jobs vs 5% higher education; 7 → **21** (not 17 or 15).

=== Types of Disability – Learning Disability and Blindness
- **Learning disability** = difficulty in a specific academic skill despite normal intelligence: **dyslexia** (reading), dysgraphia (writing), dyscalculia (maths).
- **Dyslexia** is the example specific to learning disability; **autism spectrum disorder**, **ADHD** and **mental retardation (intellectual disability)** are separate developmental conditions, not specific learning disabilities.
- RPwD Act 2016 lists **specific learning disabilities** among its 21 disabilities, separately from autism and intellectual disability.
- **New definition of blindness (April 2017, MoHFW, WHO criteria):** a person is blind if unable to **count fingers from 3 metres** (the 1976 definition used **6 metres**); programme renamed National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment.
> Trap: dyslexia = learning disability; ADHD and autism are not "specific learning disabilities"; blindness now 3 m, not 6 m.

=== Cases on Disability Rights
| Case | Issue |
|---|---|
| **Jeeja Ghosh v. UoI (12 May 2016)** | Woman with cerebral palsy deplaned (Kolkata–Goa, 2012); right to live with dignity; **SpiceJet** ordered to pay **₹10 lakh** |
| Vikash Kumar v. UPSC (2021) | Scribe not limited to benchmark disability; reasonable accommodation part of equality |
| Rajive Raturi v. UoI (8 Nov 2024) | Mandatory accessibility standards; Rule 15(1) of RPwD Rules ultra vires; time-bound directions for transport and buildings |
| SC, March 2026 (Vikram Nath, Sandeep Mehta) | 40% is a floor, not a ceiling; suitability by functional ability |
| T.M.A. Pai (2002) | Minority status unit = State (distractor) |
- SC: **safe footpaths** part of Art. 21, minimum width **1.8 m**; **digital access** part of Art. 21, citing the **UNCRPD**.
- **Prisoners with disabilities (SC, Dec 2025):** prisons to be made accessible and **prison manuals revised** in line with the **RPwD Act, 2016**.

=== Data and Andhra Pradesh – SADAREM
- **Census 2011**: **2.68 crore** PwDs = **2.21%** (about **2.2%**) of the population (2001: 2.13%); largest: **movement 20.3%**, hearing 18.9%, seeing 18.8%. WHO estimate ~16% → under-reporting.
- **Census 2027**: adds acid attack, neurological and blood disorders; autism and dwarfism left out.
- **SADAREM** (Software for Assessment of Disability for Access, Rehabilitation and Empowerment), Andhra Pradesh: **centralised database** that assesses **disabled persons** and issues digitally signed permanent disability certificates; 3,14,211 certificates in 2024-25.
- **AP**: **11.04 lakh** PwDs (Census 2011); largest type orthopaedic.
- **Divyang Shakti** (AP): **100% free APSRTC travel** from **18 March 2026** for 40%+ disability; escort 50%; SADAREM certification.
- AP pension **₹6,000** a month; 52,263 assistive devices (11 Jun 2026).
- **AP Differently Abled and Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation**: aids, appliances, economic rehabilitation.
- AP Samagra Shiksha: **Best State** in implementing RPwD Act (2024-25); ₹3,500 per CwSN; 679 Bhavitha Centres; 9 DDRCs, 70 NGOs under DDRS.
> Trap: SADAREM = AP disability database (not minorities/women/children); UDID = the national one.
