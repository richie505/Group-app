## 3. Repeats, traps and keys changed
- **RK Mission founder/year** asked about 12 times (1897; "1879" as the wrong statement). **Chicago 1893** asked 5 times; MPPSC 2013 printed 1863/1892/1881/1894 and the old key was 1892 → option added "1893".
- **Theosophical Society founders and New York, 1875** vs Besant: Besant did not found it; "Both (2) and (3)" added for the Sachivalayam Besant statements (she co-founded BHU and believed in transmigration of the soul).
- **Dar-ul-Ulum (UPPCS 2014)** appears twice with different keys (Shibli Numani / Hussain Ahmad): Spectrum gives Shibli's Darul Uloom at Lucknow → both keyed Shibli, with a dispute line (Deoband's Darul Uloom = Nanautavi and Gangohi).
- **Unkeyed (-1) PYQs now keyed:** Police 2016 match → a-iii, b-ii, c-iv, d-i; IAS 1994 shuddhi → Arya Samaj; MAO principal who turned Sir Syed against the Congress → Theodore Beck; Khalsa College Amritsar → 1892.
- **Sociology "movement arising in extreme cultural change" (UPSC 1996/1999):** the two copies are keyed transformational / millenarian; both now keyed millenarian with a dispute line, and moved to book 2.
- **App MCQ, Kuka year:** the old key 1845 has no source; Spectrum says 1840 → option added "1840" with a dispute (some date it to 1857).
- **Other traps:** MAO school 1875 vs college 1877; Faraizi founder Haji Shariatullah vs leader Dudu Miyan; Taiyuni = Karamat Ali, not Gangohi; Akali = Sikhs, not Tamils; Rast Goftar edited by Dadabhai Naoroji, not Malabari; BHU stone laid by Hardinge, not Malaviya.
