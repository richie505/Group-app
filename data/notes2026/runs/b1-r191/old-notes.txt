## SUB 4 | Ramakrishna Mission, 1897
[191:4:0] - Founded **1 May 1897** by **Swami Vivekananda**, at **Balaram Bose's house, Baghbazar, Calcutta**, soon after his return from the West; **named after his guru**.
[191:4:1] - HQ: **Belur Math**, on the **Hooghly**, **Howrah** (est. **1898**) – permanent HQ of the Ramakrishna Order; architecture blends Hindu, Islamic and Christian motifs (unity of religions).
[191:4:2] - Motto: **"Atmano Mokshartham Jagad Hitaya Cha"** – "for one's own liberation and the welfare of the world".
[191:4:3] # Two Wings and Work
[191:4:4] - 1. **Monastic order** – renunciation + practical spirituality.
[191:4:5] - 2. **Lay disciples** – preaching, philanthropy, humanitarian work.
[191:4:6] - Work: **schools, colleges, hospitals, orphanages; famine and disaster relief**.
[191:4:7] - Nature: **social and spiritual – non-political, non-sectarian, humanitarian**.
[191:4:8] "Vivekananda founded the Ramakrishna Mission in **1879**" = WRONG; the year is **1897**.
[191:4:9] - **1 Feb 2026**: RKM's **Vivekananda Human Excellence Centre, Vijayawada** (₹8.5 crore) inaugurated by RKM President **Swami Gautamananda**; **M. Venkaiah Naidu** called the Mission a "**university of values**".

## SUB 7 | Arya Samaj and Shuddhi
[191:7:0] - Founder: **Dayanand Saraswati** (born **Mool Shankar**); **"Martin Luther of India"**.
[191:7:1] - Founded **April 1875, Bombay (Mumbai)** – not Calcutta; HQ at **Lahore in 1877**.
[191:7:2] - Text: **Satyarth Prakash**. Slogans: **"Go Back to the Vedas"**, **"India for the Indians"**.
[191:7:3] - Firsts: first to use **'Swaraj'**; declared **Hindi** the national language; first to urge **Swadeshi** and boycott of foreign goods.
[191:7:4] - Principles: one God; **idol worship useless**; **all may read the Vedas**; against caste by birth and child marriage; for widow remarriage and women's education.
[191:7:5] - Gave Hindus **self-confidence and self-reliance**; aimed to restore Vedic religion in purer form. **Pandit Gurudutt** also carried on his work.
[191:7:6] - **Shuddhi Movement**: launched by the **Arya Samaj** (APPSC G2 2025) – reconversion of those who had turned to **Islam or Christianity** + admission of outsiders.
[191:7:7] # Split of 1893
[191:7:8] TABLE head: Faction | Leaders | Institution
  [191:7:8.0] College / DAV party | Mahatma Hansraj, Lala Lajpat Rai | First DAV School, Lahore, 1886
  [191:7:8.1] Gurukul party | Swami Shraddhanand (Munshi Ram), Pandit Lekh Ram | Gurukul Kangri, Haridwar (1902) – Vedic curriculum
[191:7:9] - Cause: **Western vs Vedic education and diet**, after Dayanand's death.
[191:7:10] - **Split of 1893:** In **1893**, after Dayanand's death, the Arya Samaj split into the **College (DAV) party** and the **Gurukul party**.

## SUB 2 | Aligarh Movement
[192:2:0] - Background: after 1857 Muslims lagged because they rejected Western education.
[192:2:1] - **Initial aim**: spread **Western scientific education** among Indian Muslims; social uplift with cooperation with the British.
[192:2:2] - **MAO College**: founded **1875** as **Madarsatul Uloom Musalmanan-i-Hind**; renamed **Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College** in **1877**; modelled on **Oxford and Cambridge**.
[192:2:3] - **1920**: MAO College became **Aligarh Muslim University (AMU)**.
[192:2:4] - Scientific Society translated Western science, history and philosophy into **Urdu**.
[192:2:5] - **Principals**: first principal **H.G.I. Siddons**; then **Theodore Beck (1883-99)**, who backed Muslim separatism; then **Theodore Morison**.
[192:2:6] - **Later leaders**: **Chiragh Ali, Altaf Husain Hali, Nazir Ahmad, Mohsin-ul-Mulk**.
[192:2:7] - Significance: built the **modern educated Muslim middle class**.

## SUB 1 | Kuka (namdhari) Movement
[193:1:0] # Origin and Practices
[193:1:1] - Region: **Punjab**; centre under Ram Singh at **Bhaini (Ludhiana)**, not Nankana Sahib.
[193:1:2] - Wore **white clothes**, ate **no meat**, carried a **lathi**.
[193:1:3] - Rejected idol worship, worship of tombs, trees and snakes, and **Brahmin-led rituals**; revived **Guru Gobind Singh's Khalsa** ceremonies.
[193:1:4] # Boycott Methods
[193:1:5] - Boycotted **British goods, English education, law courts and the postal system**.
[193:1:6] - **Satguru Ram Singh** = **first to use non-cooperation and boycott** as political weapons (APPSC 2025).
[193:1:7] - Significance: anticipated the **Swadeshi** and **non-cooperation** methods.
[193:1:8] # Suppression
[193:1:9] - After the **1872 Malerkotla** clash, dozens of Kukas were **blown from cannons**.
[193:1:10] - Ram Singh **deported to Rangoon (Burma)**.
[193:1:11] Kuka and Wahabi both began as **religious revolts** and became **political movements** against British rule.
## SUB 2 | Nirankari Movement
[193:2:0] - Founder **Baba Dayal Das** (1855).
[193:2:1] - Teaching: worship of God as **Nirankar (formless)**; no idol worship.
[193:2:2] - Issued **hukamnamas** on correct practice; own worship places and priests.
[193:2:3] - **Cooperated with the British**; became a **separate group within Sikhism**.
