[43:3:0] # Universal Health Care and PMJAY
[43:3:1] - **Bhore Committee (1946)**: defined **UHC** – care to all **irrespective of ability to pay**.
[43:3:2] - **PMJAY (2018, Ayushman Bharat)**: **₹5 lakh per household per year**, in-patient care.
[43:3:3] - 2023–24: PMJAY covered **58.8 crore** people, budget about **₹12,000 crore**; SHIPs about ₹16,000 crore; total about ₹28,000 crore.
[43:3:4] - **Private hospitals** get **two-thirds** of PMJAY spending.
[43:3:5] - Utilisation: about **80%** nominal coverage but only **35%** of insured patients used insurance (2022–23).

[43:3:7] - Public health spending: **1.3% of GDP** (2022, World Bank) vs world average **6.1%**.
[43:3:8] - **UHC models**: **Canada, Thailand**. **National Health Policy 2017**: public health spending target **2.5% of GDP**.
