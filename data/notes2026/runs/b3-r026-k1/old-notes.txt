=== NOTES 3:26 | Demography of India – Census 2011 Findings, Population Growth, Density, Sex Ratio, Literacy
## SUB 0 | Census History
[26:0:0] - **First Census attempted: 1872** (not 1871); **first synchronous / regular Census: 1881**; held every **10 years** since.
[26:0:1] - **Census 2011**: **15th** national Census; motto **"Our Census, Our Future"**; the last Census published.
[26:0:2] - **Census 2027**: includes **caste enumeration**; population enumeration from **February 2027**; mascots **Pragati and Vikas**.
[26:0:3] - **Delimitation Bill 2026** is reported to use **2011 Census** data.
[26:0:4] - **Census Town** definition criticised as it may **undercount urbanisation** (MoHUA, Sep 2026).
