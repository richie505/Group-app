## 5. Settled conflicts
- **Yogasara:** the printed key (Kundakundacharya) is wrong; NextGen previous papers, NextGen AP History Part 6 and Rocket AP History give **Nagarjuna**.
- **Magadha's first capital** with no Rajgriha option: the printed key "Pataliputra" was wrong → option added.
- **Mahavira's dates:** NextGen/PMF give 540–468 BCE and the tradition gives 599–527 BCE; both are in the notes.
- **Kausali under Vajramuni (APPSC G1 2004):** kept at the official key; no Drive or web source confirms it.
