=== NOTES 1:25 | Rise of Magadha – Haryankas, Shishunagas, Nandas and Causes of Supremacy
## SUB 0 | Magadha – Overview and Early History
[25:0:0] - Magadha was the **most powerful** and **richest Mahajanapada** (6th–4th c. BCE); it became the base of the **Mauryan Empire**.
[25:0:1] - Long rivalry with **Kosala, Anga, Kashi** and the **Vajjis**.
[25:0:2] - **Puranas**: first dynasty of Magadha = **Brihadratha**; **Jarasandha** ruled from **Girivraj** (Sabha Parva, Mahabharata).
[25:0:3] - First capital: **Girivraj / Rajgriha** (modern **Rajgir**), **not Vaishali**.
## SUB 1 | Dynasties in Order
[25:1:0] TABLE head: Order | Dynasty | Founder | Capital
  [25:1:0.0] 1 | Haryanka | Bimbisara | Rajgriha, then Pataliputra (Udayin)
  [25:1:0.1] 2 | Shishunaga | Shishunaga (chosen by the people) | Vaishali; Pataliputra again (Kalashoka)
  [25:1:0.2] 3 | Nanda | Mahapadma Nanda | Pataliputra; fell c. 321 BCE to Chandragupta
## SUB 2 | Causes of Magadhan Supremacy
[25:2:0] TABLE head: Factor | Effect
  [25:2:0.0] Iron ore nearby | Weapons and tools; more output and taxes
  [25:2:0.1] Alluvial soil, ample rainfall | Agriculture surplus
  [25:2:0.2] Rivers Ganga, Son (navigable) | Transport and trade
  [25:2:0.3] Elephants from the east | Military power
  [25:2:0.4] Safe capitals | Rajgriha (hills), Pataliputra (water-fort)
  [25:2:0.5] Unorthodox society | Little Vedic hold; ambitious rulers, able ministers
## SUB 3 | Haryanka Rulers
[25:3:0] # Bimbisara (544–492 BCE)
[25:3:1] - **Founder of the Haryanka dynasty**; **real (actual) founder** of the Magadhan Empire; first great ruler in Buddhist texts.
[25:3:2] - Policy: **marriage alliances**, friendship and conquest – **conquered Anga** (capital **Champa**, modern Munger–Bhagalpur; Magadha lay to its west).
[25:3:3] - **Contemporary of the Buddha** and **Mahavira**.
[25:3:4] - Royal physician **Jivaka (Jeevaka)**, also a contemporary of the Buddha; sent by Bimbisara to treat **Pradyota (Chanda-pradyota) of Avanti** for **jaundice**.
[25:3:5] # Bimbisara's Queens
[25:3:6] TABLE head: Queen | Kingdom / clan | Note
  [25:3:6.0] Kosaladevi (Mahakoshala) | Kosala | Brought Kashi as dowry (revenue)
  [25:3:6.1] Chellana | Lichchhavi (Vaishali) | Mother of Ajatashatru
  [25:3:6.2] Khema | Madra | –
[25:3:7] - Gains: Kashi revenue and security on the western and northern flanks.
[25:3:8] The Kosala marriage was Bimbisara's, not Ajatashatru's. Chellana (not Kosaladevi) was Ajatashatru's mother.
[25:3:9] # Ajatashatru (492–460 BCE)
[25:3:10] - Other name **Kunika**; son of Bimbisara; **killed his father**.
[25:3:11] - Took **Kashi**; war with **Prasenjit of Kosala**, ended with Ajatashatru marrying Prasenjit's daughter **Vajira**.
[25:3:12] - Fortified **Pataligrama** on the Ganga during the Vajji war; it grew into **Pataliputra**.
[25:3:13] - **Defeated and annexed the Vajji** confederacy (capital Vaishali, ruled by Lichchhavis) after **16 years**; minister **Vassakara** split the Lichchhavi chiefs.
[25:3:14] - War engines: **mahashilakantaka** (stone-throwing engine) and **rathamusala** (chariot with mace/blades).
[25:3:15] - Patron of the **First Buddhist Council** at Rajgriha.
[25:3:16] # Udayin (460–444 BCE)
[25:3:17] - Son of Ajatashatru; **first chose Pataliputra** as capital (shift from Rajgriha).
[25:3:18] - Probably killed at the urging of **Palaka of Avanti**; the people then chose Shishunaga.
[25:3:19] Udayin of Magadha is not **Udayan of Vatsa**.
[25:3:20] # Patricide Chain (Buddhist texts)
[25:3:21] - **Bimbisara → Ajatashatru → Udayin**: each killed by his son.
## SUB 4 | Shishunagas
[25:4:0] - **Shishunaga**: chosen by the people; **destroyed Avanti**; capital **Vaishali**.
[25:4:1] - **Kalashoka**: capital back to **Pataliputra**; **Second Buddhist Council**.
[25:4:2] - **Avanti**: capital **Ujjayini**; **Chanda-pradyota**, son of Pulika, contemporary of the Buddha and Bimbisara (Mahavagga, Puranas).
## SUB 5 | Nandas
[25:5:0] - **First non-Kshatriya dynasty**; among **India's first empire-builders**.
[25:5:1] - **Mahapadma Nanda**: titles **Ekarat** (sole sovereign) and **Sarvakshatrantaka** (destroyer of Kshatriyas).
[25:5:2] - **Dhana Nanda**: Greek names **Agrammes / Xandrames**; ruled at **Alexander's invasion**; overthrown by Chandragupta.
[25:5:3] - Greek accounts: huge army and wealth. Extent: **Kalinga and the Deccan**.
[25:5:4] - **Hathigumpha inscription** of **Kharavela**: a **Nandaraja** built an **irrigation canal in Kalinga** (Mahapadmananda).
## SUB 6 | Capitals and Places
[25:6:0] TABLE head: Capital / place | Who, why
  [25:6:0.0] Girivraja / Rajgriha | Bimbisara; five hills, near-impregnable
  [25:6:0.1] Pataliputra (fort) | Ajatashatru at Pataligrama
  [25:6:0.2] Pataliputra (capital) | Udayin
  [25:6:0.3] Vaishali | Shishunaga
  [25:6:0.4] Pataliputra again | Kalashoka
[25:6:1] - **Pataliputra** (modern **Patna**): confluence of **Ganga and Son** (Gandak nearby); Ganga to the north, Son to the west – a **water-fort**.
[25:6:2] - Remains of Pataliputra: **Kumrahar**, **Bulandibagh**.
[25:6:3] - **Kaushambi** was **not** a Magadha capital (Vatsa).
[25:6:4] - **Vaishali**: called the **first republic** of the world (6th c. BC), set up by the **Lichchhavis**; named after King **Vishal**; **birthplace of Mahavira**; Buddhist pilgrimage site.
[25:6:5] - Rajgriha: also **Mahavira's first sermon**.
[25:6:6] Pataliputra fort = Ajatashatru; capital shift = Udayin.

=== NOTES 1:26 | Jainism – Tirthankaras, Mahavira, Triratna, Panchamahavratas, Anekantavada and Syadvada
## SUB 0 | Tirthankaras

## SUB 4 | Jain Concepts – Jiva, Karma, Liberation
[26:4:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [26:4:0.0] Jiva / Ajiva | Conscious soul / non-conscious matter
  [26:4:0.1] Karma | Material bondage of the soul causing rebirth
  [26:4:0.2] Samvara | Stopping karmic inflow
  [26:4:0.3] Nirjara | Shedding existing karma by penance
  [26:4:0.4] Kevala jnana / Kaivalya | Omniscience
  [26:4:0.5] Siddha | Fully liberated soul
  [26:4:0.6] Tattva | Origination, decay, permanence (utpada-vyaya-dhrauvya)
[26:4:1] - **Souls even in rocks and running water** – all objects possess a soul that feels pain → extreme ahimsa.
[26:4:2] - Universe = living souls + **five non-living categories** (e.g. akasa); **eternal**, **no final annihilation**; **no creator god** (non-theistic).
[26:4:3] - **Four forms of existence**: **Deva, Manushya, Naraki** (hell-beings), **Tiryancha** (animals/plants) – **not Yaksha**.
[26:4:4] - Path: Karma → Samvara → Nirjara → Kevala jnana → Siddha.
