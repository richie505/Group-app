## SUB 0 | Jain Councils and the Canon
[0:0] # The two councils
[0:1] TABLE head: Council | Place, date | President | Outcome
  [0:1.0] First | Pataliputra, c. 300 BCE (Chandragupta Maurya's reign) | Sthulabhadra | Teachings arranged into 12 Angas; boycotted by Bhadrabahu's returning monks → first split
  [0:1.1] Second | Vallabhi (Valabhi), Gujarat, 512 CE (Maitraka patronage) | Devardhi Kshamashramana | Canon finally compiled and written down in Ardhamagadhi; Upangas added; 12th Anga lost
[0:2] - **First council:** **Pataliputra** (UKPSC 2021; UPPCS 2004) – not Vaishali, Mathura, Ujjain, Vallabhi, Abu or Pava.
[0:3] - **Svetambara Agama finally edited:** **Valabhi (Balabhi)** (UPPCS 2008).
[0:4] - **CGPSC 2020:** "Svetambara canon includes 12 Angas" ✓; "Angas compiled at Vallabhi" ✗ (compiled at Pataliputra; Vallabhi only recompiled them) → **I true, II false**.
[0:5] - **Andhra:** a **Jain conference at Venkatathatipuri** (Satavahana era), presided over by **Arhabali** – not one of the two councils.
[0:6] # The canon
[0:7] TABLE head: Term | Fact
  [0:7.0] Agamas / Siddhanta | Sacred books of the Jains (APPSC G2 2011), compiled by the Ganadharas
  [0:7.1] 14 Purvas | Earliest scriptures, preached by all Tirthankaras; held in the 12th Anga (Drishtivada); now lost
  [0:7.2] 12 Angas | Main body of the canon – Acharanga, Sutrakritanga, Bhagavati …
  [0:7.3] Ang-bahya | 12 Upangas, 10 Prakirnas, 6 Chhedasutras, 4 Mulasutras
  [0:7.4] Language | Ardhamagadhi Prakrit (UPPSC 2006) – not Pali (Buddhist) or Sanskrit
[0:8] - **Earliest holy book:** the **Fourteen Purvas** (State PSC) – not the 12 Angas or Upangas.
[0:9] - **Acharanga Sutra:** code of conduct for monks; **Bhagavati Sutra:** doctrines.
[0:10] - **Digambaras** hold that the Purvas and original Angas were **all lost** and reject the Svetambara canon; their chief texts are the Satkhandagama and Kasaya-pahuda.
[0:11] - **Not Jain:** **Therigatha** – verses of Buddhist nuns in the Pali Khuddaka Nikaya (UPSC 1996); Acharanga, Sutrakritanga and Brihatkalpa are early Jain texts.

## SUB 2 | Spread of Jainism and Royal Patrons
[2:0] # Spread
[2:1] - **South:** during a **12-year famine** in Magadha (Chandragupta Maurya's reign) **Bhadrabahu** led monks with Chandragupta to **Shravanabelagola** – he is credited with taking Jainism south (CDS 2008), not Sthulabhadra, Sudharma or Indrabhuti.
[2:2] - **Kalinga:** under **Kharavela** (c. 1st century BCE); his **Hathigumpha** inscription records Jain patronage.
[2:3] - **Early Christian era centres:** **Mathura and Ujjain**.
[2:4]   - **APPSC G1 2004:** the centre that grew under **Vajramuni** (1st–2nd c. CE) is keyed **Kausali** (official key; not confirmed in the notes).
[2:5] - **Later:** Karnataka (Gangas, Kadambas, Rashtrakutas), Tamil country, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Malwa; it survives in **Gujarat, Rajasthan, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu**, mainly among **traders**.
[2:6] - **UPSC 2004:** Kharavela's patronage (1st c. BCE) ✓; "spread south under Sthulabhadra" ✗; "Bhadrabahu's followers = Svetambaras" ✗; "early Jains worshipped images, unlike Buddhists" ✗ (initially neither worshipped images).

## SUB 3 | Jainism in Andhra and Telangana
[3:0] # Royal patronage and centres
[3:1] - **Early Kakatiyas** patronised **Jainism** (APPSC; Junior Asst) before turning to Shaivism; Andhra Jainism was mainly **Digambara** → "Jainism of the Digambara sect". Source: **Siddeshwara Charitra**.
[3:2] - **Potla Cheruvu (Patancheru)** was a popular **Jain** centre in the **Eastern Chalukya** period (APPSC G2 2017 ×2).
[3:3] - **Jain kshetras of Telangana:** Bodhan, Vemulawada, Potlacheruvu, Anmakonda (Hanamkonda), Kolanupaka.
[3:4] TABLE head: Centre | Note
  [3:4.0] Nedumba Vasati, Bejawada | Built by Ayyana Mahadevi, wife of Eastern Chalukya Vishnuvardhana
  [3:4.1] Kathikabharana Jinalaya, Dharmavaram | Durgaraja
  [3:4.2] Ramatirtham (Vizianagaram) | Hill centre; Ugradityacharya wrote Kalyanakaraka
  [3:4.3] Kadalalaya Basadi | Mailama, wife of Kakatiya minister Betana (1117 CE)
  [3:4.4] Aryavatam, Kolanupaka, Munugodu | Other Jain centres
[3:5] - Under the Eastern Chalukyas, **Shudras** took up Jainism for social advancement; a Jain monk fled **Rajaraja Narendra's** persecution to Hanamkonda (**Orugallu Kaifiyat**).
[3:6] - **Vijayanagara:** **Devaraya II** built a Jinalaya in the Pan Supari Bazar.
[3:7] - Jainism held rocky **Rayalaseema**; **Buddhism** held the Krishna–Godavari delta.
[3:8] # Decline
[3:9] - **Serious blow:** the **Virashaiva (Lingayat)** movement (APPSC G1) – Kalamukhas and Vira Shaivas persecuted Jains (Brahmaya at Govur and Potlacheru; **Ganapatideva** at Anmakonda).
[3:10] - **Karnataka:** **Basavanna's** 12th-century Lingayat movement; **Tamil country:** the **Alvars and Nayanars**.

[4:1] - **UPSC 2022 ×2:** Jain texts = **Parishishtaparvan and Trishashtilakshana Mahapurana** (2 and 4 only); **Nettipakarana and Avadanashataka are Buddhist**.
[4:2] # Language
[4:3] - **Early Jains discarded Sanskrit** and preached in **Prakrit**; the Agamas are in **Ardhamagadhi**.
[4:4] - **Apabhramsha:** the Jains composed its **earliest important works** and its **first grammar** – so "they never used Apabhramsa for the earliest important works" is the **incorrect** statement (APPSC G1).
[4:5] - Jain writers enriched **Prakrit, Apabhramsa, Kannada and Telugu**.
[4:6] # Social base and decline
[4:7] - **Traders and merchants** formed the social base (war and agriculture were barred); stressed **action / conduct** over birth.
[4:8] - **Decline:** lack of **missionary zeal**, loss of royal patronage, sectarian split, severe austerity, Bhakti revival.

## SUB 5 | Digambara–Svetambara Split and Sub-sects
[5:0] # The split
[5:1] - **Story:** after the 12-year famine, **Bhadrabahu's** monks returned from the south, still naked and strict; those who stayed under **Sthulabhadra** wore **white** → **Digambaras** (sky-clad) and **Svetambaras** (white-clad), c. 300 BCE.
[5:2] - **First founder of the Svetambara creed:** **Sthulabhadra** (RAS 1999) – Bhadrabahu led the Digambaras.
[5:3] TABLE head: Aspect | Digambara | Svetambara
  [5:3.0] Leader | Bhadrabahu | Sthulabhadra
  [5:3.1] Dress | Sky-clad monks | White robes
  [5:3.2] Women | Must be reborn as men for moksha | Can attain liberation
  [5:3.3] Mahavira | Never married | Married Yashoda
  [5:3.4] Monk's items | Broom and water pot | 14 items incl. clothes
  [5:3.5] Canon | Original Angas lost | Keep the 12 Angas
[5:4] # Sub-sects
[5:5] TABLE head: Sub-sect | Branch | Feature
  [5:5.0] Sthanakvasi | Svetambara | Non-idolatrous, prayer halls (sthanakas); Lonka Shah – a Jain sect (UPSC 2018)
  [5:5.1] Terapanthi | Svetambara (from the Sthanakvasis) | Acharya Bhikshu, 18th c.
  [5:5.2] Murtipujaka | Svetambara | Idol-worshipping original Svetambaras
  [5:5.3] Yapaniya | Grew from the Digambaras | Bridge sect; some Svetambara views; nude images; active in Karnataka and Andhra – a Jain sect (UPPCS 2010)
  [5:5.4] Bisapantha, Terapantha, Taranapantha | Digambara | Taranapanthis worship books, not idols
