=== India, Census 2011 – Workers by Category and Sector
| Category | Share of workers 2011 | 2001 |
|---|---|---|
| **Other workers** | **41.6%** (largest) | – |
| **Agricultural labourers** | **30.0%** | 26.5% |
| **Cultivators** | **24.6%** | 31.7% |
| Household industry | 3.8% | – |
- 2001 → 2011: **cultivators decreased, agricultural labourers increased** – labourers **outnumbered cultivators for the first time** (fragmentation, indebtedness, distress).
- Workers **4,818.89 lakh**: main **75.2%**, marginal **24.8%**; about **60% non-workers**.
- **WPR 39.8%**: male **53.3%**, female **25.5%**.
- Agriculture's share: **58.2% (2001) → 54.6% (2011)**; secondary and tertiary rose; there are marked differences in the occupational structure of different categories (rural/urban, men/women).
- Sector shares **2011-12** (NSSO, NCERT): primary about **48.9%**, secondary about 24%, services about **27%** – so "services employed **36.7%** in 2011-12" is **NOT true** (APPSC G-2 2017).
- Main workers in the service sector, 2011 Census: **26.70%** (APPSC G-2 Mains 2019 official key).
- Rural SCs: mostly **marginal farmers and landless labourers**.
> Exam angle: largest category = other workers; 2011 = labourers > cultivators; 27% services, not 36.7%.

=== Female Work Participation and Gender
- Female LFPR (15+): **23.3% (2017-18)** → 30% (2019-20) → **37% (2022-23)** → about **40% (2025)**.
- **Claudia Goldin U-curve:** India on the falling half till **2018-19**.
- Rise: subsistence farming, unpaid family work, hardship, return migration, **wider survey coverage**.
- Low levels: **mechanisation**, more schooling of girls, no flexible hours, distance, safety, family duties.
- Women (2011): **55%** of agricultural labourers, 24% of cultivators, only **12.8%** of operational holdings; men's **out-migration** → **feminisation of farm work**.
# Gender and work (sociology)
- All societies divide household tasks by gender; for women **production and reproduction** are interlinked; much of women's productive work is **unpaid**; men have played a minor role in domestic work (all four statements correct – TSPSC 2023).
- **Engels / Marxist view:** women form part of the **reserve army of labour** – drawn into work in booms and pushed out in slumps (UPSC 2000).

=== Census Worker Categories and Definitions
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Main worker** | Worked **183+ days** (6 months) in the reference year |
| **Marginal worker** | Worked **less than 183 days** |
| Cultivator | Works on own or **leased** land |
| Agricultural labourer | Works on **another's land for wages** |
| Household industry | Production **within the household** (e.g. handloom) |
| Other workers | Factory, trade, transport, services, government |
- Non-workers: students, household duties, dependants, pensioners, rentiers, beggars, others – **no "permanent industrial employers"**.
- **Census Town:** 5,000+ population; **75%+** male main workers non-agricultural; density **400+/sq km**.

=== State Patterns and Andhra Pradesh
| Pattern | States |
|---|---|
| High cultivators | **Himachal Pradesh, Nagaland** |
| High agricultural labourers | Bihar, **AP**, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Jharkhand, West Bengal, MP |
| High other workers | **Delhi, Chandigarh, Puducherry** |
- WPR higher in less-developed, hill and tribal states – subsistence work needs more manual labour.
# Andhra Pradesh (Census 2011)
- Workers **230.81 lakh**; WPR about **46.6%** (÷ 495.77 lakh) – **above** India.
- Shares: **agricultural labourers 47.9%** (largest), other workers 34.8%, cultivators **14.5%**, household industry 2.9%.
- Main agricultural labourers **86.12 lakh** of 193.17 lakh main workers (about **45%**) – nearly **1 in 10** of India's.
- Districts: **Chittoor** highest cultivators; **West Godavari** highest agricultural labourers.
- PLFS 2023-24: AP LFPR **47.5%**, WPR **45.6%** (India 45.1, 43.7); female LFPR **35.8%** (India 31.7%); urban young women WPR 15.9%; rural female WPR higher (**MGNREGS**).

=== PLFS Indicators and Data
| Indicator | Rule |
|---|---|
| LFPR | **(Employed + unemployed) / population** x 100 |
| **WPR** | **Employed / total population** x 100 |
| UR | Unemployed / **labour force** |
| UPSS | Employed if worked **30+ days** in 365 days |
| CWS | Employed if worked **1 hour** on any day of the week |
- PLFS is conducted by the **National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI**.
- **PLFS 2025 (annual):** LFPR **59%**, WPR **57%**, UR about **3%**.
- **Jan-Mar 2026 (CWS, 15+):** LFPR **55.5%**, WPR **52.8%**, UR **5.0%**.
- Q2 FY26 (Jul-Sep 2025): **agriculture 42.4%** of employment; self-employment **55.8%**; casual labour 18.9%.
- A falling UR may show **distress entry** into low-productivity work; the 1-hour CWS rule can hide **underemployment**.

=== Employment Programmes
- **MGNREGA:** Act 2005; launched **2 Feb 2006 at Bandlapalli, Anantapur (AP)**; up to 100 days.
- **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025** replaced MGNREGA from **1 Jul 2026**: **125 days** of guaranteed work.
- **e-Shram** (**26 Aug 2021**): database of unorganised workers, **12-digit UAN**; **31.89 crore** registered (18 Aug 2026).
- **PMEGP (2008):** merged PMRY + REGP; nodal agency **KVIC** (not NABARD).
