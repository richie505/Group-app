=== SC Population – Size, Share and States
- **2011:** SCs **20.14 crore** = **16.6%** of India, about 20% more than 2001; **1,241** notified SC groups in **31** States/UTs.
- **2001:** 16.66 crore = **16.2%** – not 17.5%.
| Measure (2011) | State |
|---|---|
| **Highest number** | **Uttar Pradesh** (about 4.1 crore, ~20% of India's SCs); then West Bengal, Bihar, Tamil Nadu |
| **Highest share** | **Punjab 31.9%**; then Himachal Pradesh 25.2%, West Bengal 23.5% |
| Lowest share (with SCs) | **Mizoram ~0.1%**, Meghalaya ~0.6% |
| UTs | Highest share **Chandigarh**; lowest **Dadra and Nagar Haveli** |
# Rural–urban
- About **76% rural**, 24% urban; SCs = **18.5% of rural** vs **12.6% of urban** population.
- SCs are more rural than the general population; **STs even more rural (89.97%)**.
# Andhra Pradesh and Telangana
- AP (undivided) SCs **84.69 lakh** = **17.08%** of AP = **4.21%** of India's SCs; 32.73 lakh (1971) → 84.69 lakh (2011).
- Telangana (old 10 districts): highest SC population keyed **Ranga Reddy** (about 6.5 lakh, 12.3%) – unverified against the district table.
# Census 2027
- Expected to be the **first enumeration of all castes** in independent India, not only SCs/STs.
> Largest number = UP; largest share = Punjab; 2001 share 16.2%, 2011 16.6%.

=== No Notified SC Population and Concentration
- **No notified SCs (2011):** **Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep**.
- Reason: overwhelmingly **tribal**; SC status comes from untouchability in the Hindu caste order, ST status from tribal traits.
- Small but non-zero: **Mizoram (~0.1%)**, Meghalaya (~0.6%); **Manipur, Sikkim and Tripura have notified SCs** – so any list with Mizoram is wrong.
- Highest SC-share district: **Koch Bihar (West Bengal)**, about 50%; lowest: Lawngtlai (Mizoram).
- Dense in **fertile alluvial plains and coastal deltas** (Punjab, Ganga plain, West Bengal); sparse in hill, forest and north-eastern tribal districts.

=== Constitution and Socio-economic Profile
| Provision | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Article 341** | President notifies SCs for each State/UT |
| Article 46 | DPSP: promote educational and economic interests of weaker sections, esp. SCs/STs |
| **Article 330 / 332** | Reserved seats in **Lok Sabha / State Assemblies** |
| Article 334 | Time limit on reservation; **79th Amendment (1999)** extended it to **2010** (95th to 2020, 104th to 2030) |
| Lok Sabha SC seats | **84** (from 79, 2008 delimitation) |
| Religion rule | Hindus; **Sikhs 1956**; **Buddhists 1990**; Jains not included |
| 83rd Amendment (2000) | No SC panchayat reservation in **Arunachal Pradesh** |
