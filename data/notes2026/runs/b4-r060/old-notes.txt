=== NOTES 4:60 | India - Sex Ratio and Child Sex Ratio: Trends, Regional Pattern, Causes
## SUB 0 | Concepts and Definitions
[60:0:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [60:0:0.0] Sex ratio (India) | Females per 1,000 males (APPSC 2018); an indicator of gender equality
  [60:0:0.1] Sex ratio (UN / international) | Males per 100 females
  [60:0:0.2] Child sex ratio (CSR) | Girls per 1,000 boys aged 0-6
  [60:0:0.3] Sex ratio at birth (SRB) | Girls born per 1,000 boys born
  [60:0:0.4] Biological norm | About 105 boys per 100 girls = roughly 950 girls per 1,000 boys
  [60:0:0.5] Missing women | Shortfall of women, esp. in Asia; coined by Amartya Sen (1990)
[60:0:1] - More boys are born, but **male child mortality is naturally higher** (UPSC 1999: assertion false, reason true).

## SUB 6 | Causes
[60:6:0] - Accepted causes (UPPSC 2005, all four): **high maternal mortality, high female child mortality, female foeticide, more male births**.
[60:6:1] - Roots: **son preference, patriarchy**, neglect of girls' health, intra-household discrimination, male migration.
[60:6:2] - MMR: **254 (2004-06) → 88 (2021-23)**.
[60:6:3] - After curbs on prenatal sex selection, firstborn girls show **25% higher child mortality** than firstborn boys - bias shifts from birth to survival.
## SUB 7 | Laws, Schemes and Cases
[60:7:0] TABLE head: Measure | Year | Aim
  [60:7:0.0] Cradle Baby Scheme (Tamil Nadu) | 1992 | Against female infanticide
  [60:7:0.1] PCPNDT Act | 1994 | Bans sex selection and prenatal sex determination; tests only for genetic/chromosomal abnormalities
  [60:7:0.2] Beti Bachao Beti Padhao | 22 Jan 2015, Panipat (Haryana) | Reverse falling CSR; survival, protection, education of girls; multi-sectoral action in gender-critical districts
[60:7:1] - BBBP does **not** allow prenatal sex determination.
[60:7:2] - **Supreme Court (Jun 2026)**, Justices Sanjay Karol and P.K. Mishra: CSR shows "clear signs of improvement" but bias is **deep-seated** (son preference, patriarchy).
[60:7:3] - Related data: NFHS-5 women's literacy 71.5%. British India: literacy below 16%, female literacy about 7%, IMR about 218, life expectancy about 32 years (APPSC 2024).
[60:7:4] - **Infant Mortality Rate (IMR):** deaths of infants before their first birthday per 1,000 live births; deaths before the fifth birthday give the under-5 mortality rate.

## SUB 0 | Census Definition of Literacy
[61:0:0] - **Literate** = a person aged **7 and above** who can **both read and write with understanding** in any language.
[61:0:1] - One who can only read is **not literate**.
[61:0:2] - Censuses **1951-1971** counted age **5+**; age **7+** from **1981**.
[61:0:3] - **PLFS** uses the same 7+ definition but is a **sample survey** with different samples: not directly comparable with Census.
[61:0:4] - Full literacy (ULLAS) = **95%+** literacy among persons aged **15+**.
[61:0:5] - **Census 2027** adds a **digital literacy** question.
[61:0:6] Reading only = not literate; age limit is 7, not 5 or 6.
