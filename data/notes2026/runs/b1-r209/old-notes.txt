[209:2:0] - HRA looted the **government treasury** on a train at **Kakori** near **Lucknow** (Lucknow–Saharanpur section) to fund revolutionary work.
[209:2:1] - **43 arrested**; government advocate (public prosecutor): **Jagat Narain Mulla**.
[209:2:2] - **Azad** = the **sole** Kakori revolutionary who **escaped arrest**.

## SUB 5 | Lahore Conspiracy Case (1929–1931)
[209:5:0] - Origin: **Saunders killing (1928)** + bomb-making; trial **1929–30** before a **special tribunal** set up by the **Governor-General**.
[209:5:1] - This was the "**second**" Lahore Conspiracy Case; the **first (1915)** was the **Ghadar** case.
[209:5:2] - **Death sentence: October 1930**.
