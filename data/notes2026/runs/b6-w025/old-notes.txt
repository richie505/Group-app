[25:4:1] - Grounds of arrest must be given **in writing** and in a **language the accused understands** - under **Article 22(1)**, for **all offences incl. PMLA and UAPA**.
[25:4:2] - Written grounds at least **2 hours before production for remand**, else arrest illegal.
