## SUB 0 | Supreme Court Strength Raised to 38
[0:0] # Ordinance and Bill
[0:1] - **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026** (**May 2026**, under **Article 123**): **puisne judges 33 → 37**; total **34 → 38 including the CJI**.
[0:2] - **Five judges sworn in**: 4 former HC Chief Justices + 1 from the Bar; 3 against existing vacancies, 2 on new posts.
[0:3] - Concern: if the Ordinance lapses, strength **reverts to 34** - security of tenure, judicial independence.
[0:4] - **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026** replaced the ordinance; passed by **Lok Sabha on 3 Aug 2026**; passed as a **Money Bill** - the Rajya Sabha only returned it (Aug 2026); amends the **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956**.
[0:5] - **First increase since 2019** (31 to 34).
[0:6] - **No constitutional amendment needed** - **Article 124(1)** lets **Parliament fix the number by law**.
[0:7] - **Puisne judge** = an SC judge other than the CJI.
[0:8] > Puisne judges raised from 33 to **37** (not 36); total 38 includes the CJI.

[0:13] - All courts backlog: **over 5.5 crore** cases; **5,600+ vacancies**.
[0:14] - **India Justice Report 2025**: about **15.9 judges per million**; **Law Commission** recommended **50 per million**.
[0:15] - Constitution Bench: at least **5 judges**.
