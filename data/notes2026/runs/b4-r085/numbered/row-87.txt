## SUB 1 | Locational Factors of Steel Plants
[1:0] - Raw materials are **weight-losing**, so plants sit on the **Chota Nagpur** coal-ore crescent (Jharkhand, N. Odisha, Chhattisgarh, W. Bengal).
[1:1] - **Near coalfields:** Bokaro, Durgapur, Kulti-Burnpur; **near iron ore:** Bhadravati, Bhilai, Rourkela.
[1:2] - **Far from coalfields:** **Bhadravati** (Kemmangundi ore; charcoal, then Sharavati/Jog hydel power).
[1:3] - **Midway** between ore (Noamundi, Gorumahisani) and coal (Jharia): **Jamshedpur**.
[1:4] - **Not on a riverside:** **Bhilai** (Tandula dam water); Bokaro (Damodar), Jamshedpur (Subarnarekha–Kharkai), Bhadravati (Bhadra) are.
[1:5] - **West-coast plants** (Ratnagiri, Mangalore, Dolvi, Hazira): exam reason = **high-grade iron ore of Goa** plus ports for imported coking coal and export.
[1:6] - **Coking coal** is the input India must **import** (UPSC 2015) – domestic coking coal is **high-ash**; not saltpetre or rock phosphate.
[1:7] - Plants put away from raw materials for regional balance: **Salem, Visakhapatnam, Vijayanagar**.
[1:8] - **Bokaro–Rourkela combine**: Bokaro gets iron ore from Rourkela and sends back coal.
[1:9] ~~ Sources: PMF Human & Economic Geography 05 p.11, 16-22, 25; app notes 4:87 sub 2
## SUB 2 | Output, Rank and Steel Policy
[2:0] - India = **2nd largest crude steel producer** after **China** (China over 50% of world steel).
[2:1] TABLE head: FY | Crude steel (MT)
  [2:1.0] 2023-24 | 144.3
  [2:1.1] 2024-25 | 152.2
  [2:1.2] 2025-26 | 168.4 (+10.7%; about 9% CAGR since 2021-22)
[2:2] - **APPSC G1 2023 statements:** India 2nd ✓; India 1st ✗; output in **2020 fell** below 2019 (COVID) ✗ → answer **I and II**.
[2:3] - 168.4 MT = 2025-26 output; **NSP 2017**: 300 MT capacity and 255 MT production by 2030-31 (437 MT ore + 161 MT coking coal).
[2:4] - **Mission Purvodaya** (2020): develop **Eastern India as an integrated steel hub**.
[2:5] - **PLI for Specialty Steel**: approved 2021. **Green Hydrogen Mission**: ₹455 crore for steel pilots till 2029-30.
[2:6] - Do not confuse: ₹25,938 crore = PLI-Auto; ₹33,660 crore = BHAVYA; ₹3,000 crore = NICDIT (BE 2026-27).
[2:7] - Emissions: Indian steel **2.54 t CO2/t** vs world **1.9**; 10–12% of national CO2; EU CBAM exposure.
[2:8] - Draft notification brings iron and steel under the **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme**: cuts for 255 plants from FY 2026-27 (19 Jul 2026).

## SUB 4 | Vizag Steel – AP Angle
[4:0] - **Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (RINL)**: built with **USSR** technical help; India's first **shore-based** integrated plant.
[4:1] - 1965-66 agitation slogan: "**Visakha Steel – Andhra's Right**".
[4:2] - Chosen over Bailadila, Goa, Neyveli, Salem and Hospet (not Durgapur).
[4:3] - Foundation stone **20 January 1971**; first blast furnace named **Godavari** (foundation 1982).
[4:4] - Explosion killed 8 (8 Jun 2026); 3-member probe panel (10 Jun 2026).
[4:5] - Hindustan Zinc runs a **zinc smelter** at Visakhapatnam.
