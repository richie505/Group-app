## SUB 0 | Factors and Orientation of Indian Industry
[0:0] - Factors: raw material, power, market, labour, transport, **water**, government policy, clustering, historical (colonial ports).
[0:1] TABLE head: Orientation | Examples
  [0:1.0] Raw-material (weight-losing) | Iron and steel (~4 t input per t), sugar (cane perishable), cement (limestone)
  [0:1.1] Power | Aluminium (bauxite ore; 14,000–15,000 kWh/t)
  [0:1.2] Market (weight-gaining, fragile) | Bakery, soft drinks; refineries (Koyali, Mathura, Barauni)
  [0:1.3] Footloose | Software, diamond polishing, electronics assembly
[0:2] - **Raw material NOT the primary factor:** **electronics** (UPPSC 2015) – unlike iron and steel, sugar and cement.
[0:3] - **Footloose example:** **software** (not oil refinery, sugar or aluminium). Clusters: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Noida.
[0:4] - **Principal raw materials:** limestone → **cement**; bauxite → **aluminium**; manganese → ferro-manganese.
[0:5] - **Weber (1909)**: MI above 1 (iron and steel, sugar) → near raw material; ubiquitous inputs → market.
[0:6] - **Cotton** = pure material (no weight loss), so cotton textiles spread all over India.
[0:7] - Railways let industry move inland; colonial nodes Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai.

## SUB 4 | SEZs, NIMZs and Manufacturing Policy
[4:0] - **National Manufacturing Policy 2011**: manufacturing **25% of GDP** and **100 million additional jobs by 2022** (APPSC G2 2017).
[4:1] - **NIMZ**: instrument of the NMP 2011 – large integrated townships, bigger than SEZs, different governance, exit and tax rules.
[4:2] - **India's first NIMZ** was proposed in **Andhra Pradesh** (Prakasam district) (UPSC 2016).
[4:3] - **SEZ Act 2005**, in force **10 Feb 2006**; 368 notified, **276 operational** (31 Mar 2025). Examples: Vallarpadam (Kerala), Nanguneri (TN).
[4:4] - **SEZs by State:** **Tamil Nadu** has the most – 58 notified, 49 operational (Mar 2025).
[4:5] - **BHAVYA**: 100 plug-and-play parks; ₹33,660 crore, 2026-27 to 2031-32; challenge mode; run by NICDC.
[4:6] - **APIIC**: nodal, about **571** parks in AP. Manufacturing ~16–17% of GDP; target 25% by 2047.
[4:7] ~~ Sources: app notes 4:86 sub 4; UPSC CSE 2016 key (printed)
## SUB 5 | Regional Imbalance
[5:0] - **IPR 1956**: balanced regional growth; PSUs in backward regions. **Fourth Plan (1969-74)**: attract industry to backward areas.
[5:1] - Bhilai and Rourkela were set up to develop backward tribal areas.
[5:2] - Steel plants at **Salem, Visakhapatnam, Vijayanagar** placed away from raw materials.
[5:3] - South and West lead; East, North-East lag. Economic Survey 2025-26: clusters too small, regulatory rigidities.
