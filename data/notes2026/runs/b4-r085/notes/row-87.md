- **First steel industry of India** (exam key): **Jamshedpur (TISCO, 1907)**; earlier: Porto Novo (TN) 1830 iron unit, Kulti 1874.
- **Post-independence group** (Second Plan 1956-61): **Bhilai, Durgapur, Rourkela** – Jamshedpur, Bhadravati, Kulti-Burnpur are pre-1947.
- **Pittsburgh of India**: **Jamshedpur**.
- **SAIL**: incorporated **24 Jan 1973** (books/keys often give **1974**); runs Bhilai, Rourkela, Durgapur, Bokaro, Burnpur + special steel plants Salem, Durgapur (alloy), Bhadravati; HQ **New Delhi**.
- **Prime Minister's Trophy** for best integrated steel plant (from 1992-93): **SAIL Bhilai** – record **11 wins**.

=== Locational Factors of Steel Plants; PMF Human  Economic Geography 05 p.20 (Bhilai: Dalli-Rajhara ore); en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visvesvaraya_Iron_and_Steel_Plant (SAIL 1989, merged 1998)
- Raw materials are **weight-losing**, so plants sit on the **Chota Nagpur** coal-ore crescent (Jharkhand, N. Odisha, Chhattisgarh, W. Bengal).
- **Near coalfields:** Bokaro, Durgapur, Kulti-Burnpur; **near iron ore:** Bhadravati, Bhilai, Rourkela.
- **Far from coalfields:** **Bhadravati** (Kemmangundi ore; charcoal, then Sharavati/Jog hydel power).
- **Midway** between ore (Noamundi, Gorumahisani) and coal (Jharia): **Jamshedpur**.
- **Not on a riverside:** **Bhilai** (Tandula dam water); Bokaro (Damodar), Jamshedpur (Subarnarekha–Kharkai), Bhadravati (Bhadra) are.
- **West-coast plants** (Ratnagiri, Mangalore, Dolvi, Hazira): exam reason = **high-grade iron ore of Goa** plus ports for imported coking coal and export.
- **Coking coal** is the input India must **import** (UPSC 2015) – domestic coking coal is **high-ash**; not saltpetre or rock phosphate.
- Plants put away from raw materials for regional balance: **Salem, Visakhapatnam, Vijayanagar**.
- **Bokaro–Rourkela combine**: Bokaro gets iron ore from Rourkela and sends back coal.

=== Output, Rank and Steel Policy
- India = **2nd largest crude steel producer** after **China** (China over 50% of world steel).
| FY | Crude steel (MT) |
|---|---|
| 2023-24 | 144.3 |
| 2024-25 | 152.2 |
| 2025-26 | 168.4 (+10.7%; about 9% CAGR since 2021-22) |
- **APPSC G1 2023 statements:** India 2nd ✓; India 1st ✗; output in **2020 fell** below 2019 (COVID) ✗ → answer **I and II**.
- 168.4 MT = 2025-26 output; **NSP 2017**: 300 MT capacity and 255 MT production by 2030-31 (437 MT ore + 161 MT coking coal).
- **Mission Purvodaya** (2020): develop **Eastern India as an integrated steel hub**.
- **PLI for Specialty Steel**: approved 2021. **Green Hydrogen Mission**: ₹455 crore for steel pilots till 2029-30.
- Do not confuse: ₹25,938 crore = PLI-Auto; ₹33,660 crore = BHAVYA; ₹3,000 crore = NICDIT (BE 2026-27).
- Emissions: Indian steel **2.54 t CO2/t** vs world **1.9**; 10–12% of national CO2; EU CBAM exposure.
- Draft notification brings iron and steel under the **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme**: cuts for 255 plants from FY 2026-27 (19 Jul 2026).

=== Iron and Steel – Types, Raw Materials and Process
| Form | Carbon | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pig iron | ~4% | Blast-furnace product, brittle |
| Cast iron | ~3% | Hard, brittle |
| **Wrought iron** | **< 0.1%** | **Purest** commercial iron |
| Mild steel | 0.1–0.4% | — |
| **Hard (high-carbon) steel** | **0.5–1.5%** | — |
- Blast furnace: coke (fuel, reducer) + iron ore + limestone (flux).
- Process mix (8 Sep 2026): **43% BF-BOF, 22% EAF, 35% induction furnace**; BF-BOF projected at 56% by 2030.
- Mini/secondary units melt scrap or **sponge iron** in EAF and induction furnaces.
- India = **leading producer of sponge iron (DRI)**; **Odisha** 1st, **Chhattisgarh** 2nd, then West Bengal and Jharkhand.
- 2024-25: iron ore output 289.398 MT (imports 6.4 MT); coking coal output 66.49 MT but imports **57.07 MT**.
- Old framing (IAS 1993): India self-sufficient in **coking coal** for three decades – now outdated (heavy imports).

=== Vizag Steel – AP Angle
- **Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (RINL)**: built with **USSR** technical help; India's first **shore-based** integrated plant.
- 1965-66 agitation slogan: "**Visakha Steel – Andhra's Right**".
- Chosen over Bailadila, Goa, Neyveli, Salem and Hospet (not Durgapur).
- Foundation stone **20 January 1971**; first blast furnace named **Godavari** (foundation 1982).
- Explosion killed 8 (8 Jun 2026); 3-member probe panel (10 Jun 2026).
- Hindustan Zinc runs a **zinc smelter** at Visakhapatnam.

=== Aluminium – Companies and Plants
- **2nd most important** metallurgical industry after steel; India **2nd** in primary aluminium; **7th** in bauxite reserves.
- **Bauxite** is the principal raw material; **Odisha** = largest reserves and production (Koraput, Kalahandi).
- **Power-oriented**: about **14,000–15,000 kWh per tonne** → smelters near hydel or captive thermal power.
| Company | Location | Note |
