=== Evidence of Climate Change – Past Climates and Recent Warming
# Past climates (asked 6 times)
- **Tree rings (dendroclimatology)**: ring width is a fairly accurate index of a year's **precipitation** (water available in the growing season); old tree rings are the best record of a place's **past local climate over centuries** (weather logs cover only recent decades).
- Other evidence: glacial landforms (advances and retreats), sediments of glacial lakes, archaeological finds (Rajasthan wet and cool about 8,000 BC).
- **Great Ice Age = Pleistocene** (cooling began in the **Pliocene**) – APPSC G1 2002 key: "Pleistocene and Pliocene". Last glacial peak about 18,000 years ago; present interglacial began about 10,000 years ago.
- **Glaciation of peninsular India** = the **Permo-Carboniferous (Carboniferous) ice age** of Gondwana, recorded in the **Talchir tillite** at the base of the Gondwana rocks; Pleistocene glaciers reached only the Himalaya.
- **Little Ice Age**: about **1550–1850** (NCERT); much colder winters in Europe and N America; among exam options **1650–1870** is the period given. 950–1250 AD = Medieval Warm Period.
# Recent warming
| Indicator | Record |
|---|---|
| 2024 | Hottest year on record, about **1.55°C** above pre-industrial |
| 2025 | 2nd/3rd warmest (~1.43°C); **warmest La Nina year** |
| 2015–2025 | The **11 warmest years** (WMO State of the Global Climate 2025) |
| India | Warmed about **0.7°C** since 1900 (less than global land, ~1.59°C); IMD: **2024** warmest year since 1901 |
| Gangotri Glacier | Retreated about **1,700 m** since 1935 |
| Sea level | Up about **11 cm** since 1993 |
- **Global warming** = rise in global surface temperature, caused by rising greenhouse-gas concentrations (CDS 2017 A-R: both true, II explains I).
- **Sea-level rise** = thermal expansion + melting ice; higher snow albedo is not a cause.

=== Causes – Natural and Anthropogenic
# Natural causes (asked 6 times)
| Cause | Meaning / effect |
|---|---|
| **Eccentricity** | Shape of Earth's orbit |
| **Obliquity** | Axial tilt |
| **Precession** (of the equinoxes) | Wobble of the rotational axis |
| Solar output (sunspots) | Satellites since 1978 show **no net increase**: the Sun does not explain recent warming |
| Volcanoes | **SO2** + ash form aerosols that reflect sunlight: **short-term cooling** |
- **Milankovitch** (Serbian, **1920s**): astronomical theory – orbital forcing by eccentricity, tilt (shifting of the axis) and precession. **Solar irradiance is NOT a Milankovitch factor** (NCERT lists sunspots separately as a solar cause).
# Anthropogenic causes
- **Chief contributor** to warming in the last and present century = **anthropogenic impact** (IPCC AR6), not plate tectonics, volcanoes or Earth's rotation.
- Fossil fuels (CO2), **land-use change** (deforestation removes sinks), **agriculture** (methane, N2O). CO2 causes about **three-quarters** of warming.
- Rising GHGs → global warming, **CO2 fertilisation** of plants, stratospheric cooling linked to **ozone depletion**; there is **no "oxygen fertilisation effect"** (CDS 2007).
- **Water vapour** = most abundant greenhouse gas (feedback).
- **China** = largest CO2 emitter, and largest in the developing world; India 3rd (after China and USA).
| Gas | GWP (100 yr, AR6) | Main sources |
|---|---|---|
| CO2 | 1 | Fossil fuels, deforestation, cement |
| Methane | ~27–30 | Rice paddies, livestock (**enteric fermentation**), landfills |
| Nitrous oxide | **~273** | **Fertilisers** (largest farm source), manure |
| CFCs / HFCs | Very high | Refrigeration, air conditioning (synthetic) |
| **SF6** | **~25,200 (highest)** | Electrical switchgear (**entirely synthetic**) |
# Short-lived climate pollutants (SLCPs)
- Stay in the air **days to a few decades** – far **shorter** than CO2.
- **Black carbon** = **soot**; **tropospheric ozone** is not emitted directly (sunlight on methane, CO, VOCs, NOx); methane; HFCs.
- **Climate and Clean Air Coalition**: UNEP + **six countries**, 16 Feb 2012.

=== Impacts of Global Warming
- IPCC: warming raises the frequency and severity of **cyclones, storms and hurricanes – all of these**, plus floods, droughts, glacier melt (UPPCS 2018).
- **Melting polar ice and sea-level rise** = the most cited effect; Uttarakhand 2002 PYQ keyed statements 1, 3 and 4 (coral islands submerged, Fiji by 2044) – these were alarmist forecasts, not facts (as of 2026 Fiji is not submerged).
| Area | Effect |
|---|---|
| Sea level | Uninhabited Kiribati islets **Tebua Tarawa, Abanuea** submerged 1999; Tuvalu at risk |
| Coral reefs | **1.2°C** warm-water reef tipping point already crossed (UN brief, Jun 2026) |
| Amazon | Beyond ~2°C parts may turn **savanna and a carbon source** |
| Oceans, species | Acidity up **30%** since the Industrial Revolution; up to **25%** of mountain species face extinction (AR6) |
| Disease | Warmer, wetter conditions spread **vector-borne** malaria, dengue |
- **AP**: heatwaves in Palnadu, Kurnool, Anantapur; **coastal erosion at Uppada (Kakinada)**.
- **COP31**: **Türkiye (Antalya), November 2026**.
~~ Sources: IPCC AR6; Shankar Environment ch.17, 20; WMO; app notes 4:21 sub 6

=== Geoengineering – Reducing Global Warming
| Proposal | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Stratospheric sulphate aerosol injection** | SO2 forms reflective aerosols; copies **volcanic** cooling | **Termination shock** if stopped suddenly |
| **Cirrus cloud thinning** | Seeding high cirrus so they thin and **more outgoing heat escapes** | – |
| Marine cloud brightening | Sea-salt spray as **cloud condensation nuclei** | – |
- UPSC 2019: cirrus thinning and sulphate injection are proposed for **reducing global warming** – not artificial rain, cyclones or solar wind.
