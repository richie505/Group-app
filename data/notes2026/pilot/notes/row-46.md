=== Founder, Firsts and Independence from the Mauryas
# The firsts
- **First historical ruling dynasty of the Deccan:** the **Satavahanas** (c. 200 BC – 200 AD), the oldest Deccan dynasty (Sainik Welfare 2012; UP 2001).
  - Distractors: Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Cholas, Pallavas – all centuries later.
- **First dynasty to rule Andhra:** the **Satavahanas** (G1 2010 re-exam; G1 PYQ); not the Cholas, Eastern Chalukyas or Rashtrakutas.
- **Puranic name:** the Puranas call them **Andhras** or **Andhrabhrityas** ("servants of the Andhras", or Andhras who had been feudatories); the dynasty identified with the Puranic Andhras = Satavahanas.
- **Caste:** **Brahmins**; the Nasik inscription calls Gautamiputra **"Ekabrahmana"** (APPSC PYQ).
- **⚠ Trap:** they claimed **Brahmana** status, not Buddhist descent – a statement "Satavahanas claimed Buddhist descent" is false.
# Founder – Simuka
- **Founder:** **Simuka** = Simukha = Srimukha = Shimukha (Puranic "Sinthaka"); asked 13 times (G2 2000, 2008; Geologist 2007; SI 2011; UPPSC 2008).
- **Founder of the administration too:** Simuka (G2 2008); he united several principalities into one kingdom.
- **Rise:** killed the **Kanva** king **Susarma** and ended the Kanvas; ruled from **Pratishthana**; said to have ruled 23 years.
- **Progenitor:** per the **Puranas = Srimukha**; per inscriptions, coins and Kathasaritsagara = **Satavahana** (Srimukha's father) – exams answer Simuka.
- **Successors:** brother **Kanha (Krishna)**, 2nd king; then son **Satakarni I**, 3rd king. Purnotsanga (Vedasri) and Kanha are distractors for "founder".
# Independence and span
- **Freedom from the Mauryas:** the Satavahanas began as Mauryan feudatories; the Andhras won freedom from Mauryan rule under them (APPSC PYQ).
- **Rule of ~450 years:** after Mauryan decline they ruled the Deccan for **456 years** (Matsya Purana) – "about 450 years" (Social & Cultural History of AP).
- **Ashoka's 13th Rock Edict:** lists the Andhras among his vassal peoples.
- **Sequence in Magadha:** Mauryas → Sungas → Kanvas → Satavahanas.
- **Successor states:** Ikshvakus (Andhra), Abhiras (Maharashtra), Chutus (Karnataka), Pallavas (Tamil Nadu), Vakatakas (Vidarbha).
> Statement angle: "Founded by Simuka" ✓; "claimed Buddhist descent" ✗; "long conflict with the Sakas over the premier (western) ports" ✓ (Periplus) → answer "1 and 3".

=== Nasik Prasasti and Gautamiputra's Conquests
# Nasik Prasasti – the record
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Issuer | **Gautami Balasri**, mother of Gautamiputra Satakarni |
| Date | **19th regnal year** of her grandson **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** (G2 2008) |
| Place | Nasik, Pandavleni Cave 3 (Maharashtra) |
| Language, script | Prakrit prose, Brahmi |
| Content | Gautamiputra's **military victories**, empire and titles |
- **Most-asked fact:** the inscription describing Gautamiputra's achievements = **Nasik** (G1 1987; G2 2008; Welfare Officers; APPSC PYQ ×3).
- **⚠ Trap:** issued by **Gautami Balasri in Pulumavi's reign**, not by Gautamiputra and not in his reign.
- **⚠ Trap:** Naneghat = Naganika (Satakarni I); Kodavali = Chanda Satakarni; Hathigumpha = Kharavela; Junnar, Pitalkhora = distractors.
- **Gift recorded:** a village and **100 nivartanas** of land to monks on **Trirashmi** hill.
# Conquests
- **Nahapana:** Gautamiputra defeated the **Kshaharata Saka** king **Nahapana** (G1 1990-91); not Pulumayi, Yajna or Rudra Satakarni.
- **Lost lands regained:** Malwa, Saurashtra, Konkan, Nasik regained from Nahapana in Gautamiputra's reign (G2 2000).
- **Enemy faced:** the **Sakas** (not Hunas, Greeks or Parthians); praised as "destroyer of **Sakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas**".
- **Jogalthambi hoard (Nasik district):** **13,270** silver coins of Nahapana **restruck** by Gautamiputra – proof of the victory.
# Regions named in the Prasasti
| Region | Area |
|---|---|
| **Anupa** | Narmada valley, capital **Mahishmati** (G2 2017 ×2; APPSC PYQ) |
| Akara / Avanti | East Malwa (Vidisha) / West Malwa (Ujjain) |
| Asika, Asaka (Asmaka), Mulaka | Godavari valley, Paithan area |
| Suratha, Kukura | Saurashtra; western Malwa–Rajasthan |
| Aparanta / Vidarbha | North Konkan / Berar |
- **⚠ Trap:** Vidisha and Ujjain are Akara and Avanti, not Anupa; Podana is a distractor.
# Social claims
- **Protector of the varna system:** **Varnasankarya-nirodhaka** – stopped the mixing of the four varnas (Chhattisgarh PSC 2013); not Pushyamitra, Kharavela or Vasudeva.
- **Brahmanical image:** Ekabrahmana, "crusher of Kshatriya pride" (Kshatriya-darpa-mana-mardana), Agama-nilaya.
- **⚠ Sources differ:** NextGen says he "started the Salivahana era (AD 78)".
  - Settled: 78 CE begins the **Shaka era**, tied to Chashtana's accession; the "Salivahana era" is a later legend (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaka_era).
- **Dates:** traditionally c. 106-130 CE (23rd king, ruled 34 years per NextGen); some scholars now prefer c. 60-85 CE.

=== Queens and Metronymics
# Queens
| Queen | Role |
|---|---|
| **Devi Naganika** | Wife of **Satakarni I**; daughter of **Maharathi Tranakayiro** (Amgiya family); **regent** for her minor son; issued **Naneghat** |
| **Gautami Balasri** | Mother of Gautamiputra; issued the **Nasik Prasasti**; gave a Nasik cave vihara to Bhadrayaniya monks |
| Lilavati | Sri Lankan princess, queen of **Hala** – distractor |
- **Regent queen:** **Devi Naganika** ruled as guardian during her son's minority (G2 2019; APPSC PYQ ×2).
- **Whose queen:** Naganika = queen of **Satakarni I** (APSLPRB 2023; APPSC PYQ) – not Srimukha, Satakarni II or Gautamiputra.
- **Her father:** Maharathi **Tranakayiro** – the marriage allied the Satavahanas with the Maharathis (APPSC PYQ ×2).
- **⚠ Trap:** regent = Naganika, not Gautami Balasri; Devi Mathari, Rudradamanika, Chashtana's or Nahapana's daughter are distractors.
# Metronymics
- **What:** kings put their **mother's name** before their own – Gautamiputra (son of Gautami), Vasishthiputra.
- **Began with:** **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (G2 2008 key; asked 6 times) – not Simuka, Hala or Vasishthiputra Pulumavi.
- **Precisely:** the prefix is the **mother's gotra** name (Gotami < Gautama, Vasithi < Vasishtha) (JA Panchayat Raj 2012).
- **Dynasty known by metronymics:** the **Satavahanas** (CDS 2020) – not the Mallas, Videhas or Yaudheyas.
- **Meaning:** usually explained by **polygamy**; shows respect for mothers, but succession stayed **patriarchal**.
> Statement angle: "Gautamiputra took pride in calling himself son of Gautami" ✓ (Sachivalayam).

=== Key Kings in the Matsya List
| No. | King | Exam fact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Simuka** | Founder; ended the Kanvas |
| 2 | **Kanha (Krishna)** | First Satavahana inscription (Nasik); expanded to Nasik |
| 3 | **Satakarni I** | Greatest early king; 2 Ashvamedhas; Dakshinapathapati; Kharavela's contemporary |
| 6 | **Satakarni II** | Longest reign, **56 years**; southern torana of Sanchi |
| 13 | **Kuntala Satakarni** | Made **Sanskrit** the royal language |
| 17 | **Hala** | Gathasaptasati; Kavivatsala; ruled 5 years |
| 23 | **Gautamiputra Satakarni** | **Greatest of all**; last of the early Satavahanas |
| 24 | **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi (Pulomavi II)** | Nasik Prasasti issued; capital to Amaravati; **Navanagaraswami** |
| – | **Vasishthiputra (Sivasri) Satakarni** | Married **Rudradaman's daughter** |
| 27 | **Yajnasri Satakarni** | Greatest of the later kings; Nagarjuna's patron; ship coins |
| – | **Chanda Sri (Chandasri)** | Kodavali inscription |
| 30 | **Pulomavi III** | **Last king**; Myakadoni inscription |
- **Greatest Satavahana:** **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (APPSC PYQ); **⚠ Trap:** Yajnasri = greatest of the **later** kings.
- **Successor of Gautamiputra:** **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** – "succeeded by Yajnasri" is false (Sachivalayam).
- **Navanagara (new city):** built by **Pulomavi II**, title Navanagaraswami (G2 2000 backlog).
- **Rudradaman's son-in-law:** **(Vasishthiputra/Sivasri) Satakarni**; Rudradaman twice defeated him but spared him as a near relative (Junagadh) (APPSC PYQ).
- **Nagarjuna at whose court:** **Yajnasri Satakarni** (Sachivalayam) – not Pulomavi III, Satakarni I or Srimukha.
- **Last king (Puranas):** **Pulomavi III**, the 30th (G2 2011 ×2); Sivasri, Vijayasri, Chandrasri are distractors.
- **⚠ Trap:** the APPSC 2011 key printed **Chandasri**, but the Matsya list ends with Pulomavi; our key is Pulomavi III. Rocket numbers the Myakadoni king "Pulumavi IV".
# End of the dynasty
- **Fall:** the Ikshvaku **Santamula (Srisantamula)** overthrew Pulomavi III (c. AD 225-230) and took Dharanikota and Nagarjunakonda; Pulomavi III spent his last days at **Myakadoni** (Bellary).

=== Origin Debate – Sukthankar and Others
- **V.S. Sukthankar:** "**Andhras and Satavahanas are not the same**" (G2 2012; APPSC PYQ ×2).
- **Sukthankar again:** Satavahanas were **not Andhras but Andhrabhrityas** (G2 2008 ×2; printed "V.N. Sukthankar").
- **Bellary theory:** Sukthankar placed their home in **Bellary (Ballari)** on the basis of the **Myakadoni** inscription (G2 2017 – "Macedonian" is a misprint for Myakadoni; G2 PYQ).
- **⚠ Trap:** **R.G. Bhandarkar** held the opposite – Andhras and Satavahanas were the same people.
- **Distractors:** V.V. Mirashi, S.A. Joglekar, P.T. Srinivasa Iyengar, V.A. Smith, Rapson, Gopalachari.
| Scholar | View |
|---|---|
| V.V. Mirashi | Home in **Vidarbha** (Berar) |
| E.J. Rapson, R.G. Bhandarkar | Origin in eastern Deccan (Andhra), from Puranic "Andhra" names |
| Gopalachari | Rejected Bellary; favoured **Maharashtra** |
| D.C. Sircar | Andhras, not Andhrabhrityas |
| K.A. Nilakanta Sastri | Andhra = tribal, Satavahana = dynastic, Satakarni = family name |
| B.S.L. Hanumantha Rao | Belonged to the original Andhra janapada |
| R.S. Sharma | Dravidians |
~~ Sources: app notes 1:46 (sub 1); Rocket AP History p.6

=== Capitals
- **Principal capital / main town:** **Pratishthana (Paithan)** on the **Godavari**, Aurangabad district, Maharashtra (Welfare Officers; UP Lower 2008; AMVI 2009; APPSC PYQ).
- **Andhra capital:** **Dhanyakataka** (Dharanikota, near Amaravati, on the Krishna) (APPSC PYQ); distractors Vengi (E. Chalukyas), Warangal (Kakatiyas), Kondapur.
- **Shift east:** after Rudradaman's attacks **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** moved the capital to **Amaravati (Dhanyakataka)**.
- **Current link:** **Amaravati**, the designated capital of Andhra Pradesh today, was the historical Satavahana capital (APPSC AEE).
- **Only-option rule:** if Paithan is absent, mark **Amaravati** (UPPCS Mains 2005; distractors Nanded, Naldurg, Durg).
- **First capital – debated:** Srikakulam, Krishna dt (Barnett, V.A. Smith, Burgess); Pratishthana (Mirashi, Raychaudhuri); Dhanyakataka (Bhandarkar).
- **⚠ Trap:** Taxila, Kanchi, Pataliputra, Arikamedu, Nagarjunakonda (Ikshvaku capital Vijayapuri) are not Satavahana capitals.
- **Other centres:** Kotilingala (Karimnagar) – claimed early centre; Mahishmati – capital of Anupa.

=== Sources – Puranas, Literature, Foreign Accounts
# Puranas
- **Main Purana:** the **Matsya Purana** – genealogy and reign of each king; Satavahana political history rests on it (Welfare Officers).
- **Matsya figures:** **30 kings, 456 years** (271 BC – 174 AD); kings 1-23 = early, 24-30 = later Satavahanas.
- **Compiled under:** **Yajnasri Satakarni** (APPSC PYQ).
- **Vayu / Vishnu:** 17 kings, 272 years – not treated as standard.
- **⚠ Trap:** the **Vishnudharmottara Purana** (Chitrasutra) is the "sourcebook of Indian painting", not a Satavahana source (Animal Husbandry GS).
# Literature
- **Early Satavahana history:** told in **Kathasaritsagara, Pratishthanapura-kalpa, Dvatrimsatputtalika**.
- **⚠ Trap:** the **Gathasaptasati** does **not** narrate early Satavahana history (G2 2017; APPSC PYQ).
# Kharavela
- **Contemporary:** Kharavela of Kalinga was the contemporary of **Satakarni I** (G2-B 1989); the Hathigumpha inscription and the Chulakalinga Jataka record the contact.
- **⚠ Sources differ:** NextGen says Kharavela "attacked and defeated" Satakarni I.
  - Settled: Hathigumpha line 4 says he sent an army west "disregarding Satakarni", to the Kanhabenna; no defeat is recorded (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hathigumpha_inscription).
# Foreign accounts
| Writer | Work / fact |
|---|---|
| Megasthenes | Indica; first foreign source on the Andhras |
| Pliny | Natural History (Latin); Roman gold drained to India |
| Periplus (anonymous Greek) | Saka–Satavahana conflict; Koddura the only east-coast port; Arikamedu |
