## SUB 0 | Founder, Firsts and Independence from the Mauryas
[0:0] # The firsts
[0:1] - **First historical ruling dynasty of the Deccan:** the **Satavahanas** (c. 200 BC – 200 AD), the oldest Deccan dynasty (Sainik Welfare 2012; UP 2001).
[0:2]   - Distractors: Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Cholas, Pallavas – all centuries later.
[0:3] - **First dynasty to rule Andhra:** the **Satavahanas** (G1 2010 re-exam; G1 PYQ); not the Cholas, Eastern Chalukyas or Rashtrakutas.
[0:4] - **Puranic name:** the Puranas call them **Andhras** or **Andhrabhrityas** ("servants of the Andhras", or Andhras who had been feudatories); the dynasty identified with the Puranic Andhras = Satavahanas.
[0:5] - **Caste:** **Brahmins**; the Nasik inscription calls Gautamiputra **"Ekabrahmana"** (APPSC PYQ).
[0:6] - **⚠ Trap:** they claimed **Brahmana** status, not Buddhist descent – a statement "Satavahanas claimed Buddhist descent" is false.
[0:7] # Founder – Simuka
[0:8] - **Founder:** **Simuka** = Simukha = Srimukha = Shimukha (Puranic "Sinthaka"); asked 13 times (G2 2000, 2008; Geologist 2007; SI 2011; UPPSC 2008).
[0:9] - **Founder of the administration too:** Simuka (G2 2008); he united several principalities into one kingdom.
[0:10] - **Rise:** killed the **Kanva** king **Susarma** and ended the Kanvas; ruled from **Pratishthana**; said to have ruled 23 years.
[0:11] - **Progenitor:** per the **Puranas = Srimukha**; per inscriptions, coins and Kathasaritsagara = **Satavahana** (Srimukha's father) – exams answer Simuka.
[0:12] - **Successors:** brother **Kanha (Krishna)**, 2nd king; then son **Satakarni I**, 3rd king. Purnotsanga (Vedasri) and Kanha are distractors for "founder".
[0:13] # Independence and span
[0:14] - **Freedom from the Mauryas:** the Satavahanas began as Mauryan feudatories; the Andhras won freedom from Mauryan rule under them (APPSC PYQ).
[0:15] - **Rule of ~450 years:** after Mauryan decline they ruled the Deccan for **456 years** (Matsya Purana) – "about 450 years" (Social & Cultural History of AP).
[0:16] - **Ashoka's 13th Rock Edict:** lists the Andhras among his vassal peoples.
[0:17] - **Sequence in Magadha:** Mauryas → Sungas → Kanvas → Satavahanas.
[0:18] - **Successor states:** Ikshvakus (Andhra), Abhiras (Maharashtra), Chutus (Karnataka), Pallavas (Tamil Nadu), Vakatakas (Vidarbha).
[0:19] > Statement angle: "Founded by Simuka" ✓; "claimed Buddhist descent" ✗; "long conflict with the Sakas over the premier (western) ports" ✓ (Periplus) → answer "1 and 3".
[0:20] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.5-7, p.12; Rocket AP History p.3, p.22, p.46, p.56; app notes 1:46 (sub 0, 3)
## SUB 1 | Nasik Prasasti and Gautamiputra's Conquests
[1:0] # Nasik Prasasti – the record
[1:1] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [1:1.0] Issuer | Gautami Balasri, mother of Gautamiputra Satakarni
  [1:1.1] Date | 19th regnal year of her grandson Vasishthiputra Pulumavi (G2 2008)
  [1:1.2] Place | Nasik, Pandavleni Cave 3 (Maharashtra)
  [1:1.3] Language, script | Prakrit prose, Brahmi
  [1:1.4] Content | Gautamiputra's military victories, empire and titles
[1:2] - **Most-asked fact:** the inscription describing Gautamiputra's achievements = **Nasik** (G1 1987; G2 2008; Welfare Officers; APPSC PYQ ×3).
[1:3] - **⚠ Trap:** issued by **Gautami Balasri in Pulumavi's reign**, not by Gautamiputra and not in his reign.
[1:4] - **⚠ Trap:** Naneghat = Naganika (Satakarni I); Kodavali = Chanda Satakarni; Hathigumpha = Kharavela; Junnar, Pitalkhora = distractors.
[1:5] - **Gift recorded:** a village and **100 nivartanas** of land to monks on **Trirashmi** hill.
[1:6] # Conquests
[1:7] - **Nahapana:** Gautamiputra defeated the **Kshaharata Saka** king **Nahapana** (G1 1990-91); not Pulumayi, Yajna or Rudra Satakarni.
[1:8] - **Lost lands regained:** Malwa, Saurashtra, Konkan, Nasik regained from Nahapana in Gautamiputra's reign (G2 2000).
[1:9] - **Enemy faced:** the **Sakas** (not Hunas, Greeks or Parthians); praised as "destroyer of **Sakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas**".
[1:10] - **Jogalthambi hoard (Nasik district):** **13,270** silver coins of Nahapana **restruck** by Gautamiputra – proof of the victory.
[1:11] # Regions named in the Prasasti
[1:12] TABLE head: Region | Area
  [1:12.0] Anupa | Narmada valley, capital Mahishmati (G2 2017 ×2; APPSC PYQ)
  [1:12.1] Akara / Avanti | East Malwa (Vidisha) / West Malwa (Ujjain)
  [1:12.2] Asika, Asaka (Asmaka), Mulaka | Godavari valley, Paithan area
  [1:12.3] Suratha, Kukura | Saurashtra; western Malwa–Rajasthan
  [1:12.4] Aparanta / Vidarbha | North Konkan / Berar
[1:13] - **⚠ Trap:** Vidisha and Ujjain are Akara and Avanti, not Anupa; Podana is a distractor.
[1:14] # Social claims
[1:15] - **Protector of the varna system:** **Varnasankarya-nirodhaka** – stopped the mixing of the four varnas (Chhattisgarh PSC 2013); not Pushyamitra, Kharavela or Vasudeva.
[1:16] - **Brahmanical image:** Ekabrahmana, "crusher of Kshatriya pride" (Kshatriya-darpa-mana-mardana), Agama-nilaya.
[1:17] - **⚠ Sources differ:** NextGen says he "started the Salivahana era (AD 78)".
[1:18]   - Settled: 78 CE begins the **Shaka era**, tied to Chashtana's accession; the "Salivahana era" is a later legend (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaka_era).
[1:19] - **Dates:** traditionally c. 106-130 CE (23rd king, ruled 34 years per NextGen); some scholars now prefer c. 60-85 CE.
[1:20] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.10, p.25; Rocket AP History p.6, p.8; app notes 1:46 (sub 4, 5); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautamiputra_Satakarni; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaka_era
## SUB 2 | Queens and Metronymics
[2:0] # Queens
[2:1] TABLE head: Queen | Role
  [2:1.0] Devi Naganika | Wife of Satakarni I; daughter of Maharathi Tranakayiro (Amgiya family); regent for her minor son; issued Naneghat
  [2:1.1] Gautami Balasri | Mother of Gautamiputra; issued the Nasik Prasasti; gave a Nasik cave vihara to Bhadrayaniya monks
  [2:1.2] Lilavati | Sri Lankan princess, queen of Hala – distractor
[2:2] - **Regent queen:** **Devi Naganika** ruled as guardian during her son's minority (G2 2019; APPSC PYQ ×2).
[2:3] - **Whose queen:** Naganika = queen of **Satakarni I** (APSLPRB 2023; APPSC PYQ) – not Srimukha, Satakarni II or Gautamiputra.
[2:4] - **Her father:** Maharathi **Tranakayiro** – the marriage allied the Satavahanas with the Maharathis (APPSC PYQ ×2).
[2:5] - **⚠ Trap:** regent = Naganika, not Gautami Balasri; Devi Mathari, Rudradamanika, Chashtana's or Nahapana's daughter are distractors.
[2:6] # Metronymics
[2:7] - **What:** kings put their **mother's name** before their own – Gautamiputra (son of Gautami), Vasishthiputra.
[2:8] - **Began with:** **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (G2 2008 key; asked 6 times) – not Simuka, Hala or Vasishthiputra Pulumavi.
[2:9] - **Precisely:** the prefix is the **mother's gotra** name (Gotami < Gautama, Vasithi < Vasishtha) (JA Panchayat Raj 2012).
[2:10] - **Dynasty known by metronymics:** the **Satavahanas** (CDS 2020) – not the Mallas, Videhas or Yaudheyas.
[2:11] - **Meaning:** usually explained by **polygamy**; shows respect for mothers, but succession stayed **patriarchal**.
[2:12] > Statement angle: "Gautamiputra took pride in calling himself son of Gautami" ✓ (Sachivalayam).
[2:13] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.7, p.10, p.15; Rocket AP History p.53; app notes 1:46 (sub 7)

[3:9] - **Fall:** the Ikshvaku **Santamula (Srisantamula)** overthrew Pulomavi III (c. AD 225-230) and took Dharanikota and Nagarjunakonda; Pulomavi III spent his last days at **Myakadoni** (Bellary).

## SUB 5 | Origin Debate – Sukthankar and Others
[5:0] - **V.S. Sukthankar:** "**Andhras and Satavahanas are not the same**" (G2 2012; APPSC PYQ ×2).
[5:1] - **Sukthankar again:** Satavahanas were **not Andhras but Andhrabhrityas** (G2 2008 ×2; printed "V.N. Sukthankar").
[5:2] - **Bellary theory:** Sukthankar placed their home in **Bellary (Ballari)** on the basis of the **Myakadoni** inscription (G2 2017 – "Macedonian" is a misprint for Myakadoni; G2 PYQ).
[5:3] - **⚠ Trap:** **R.G. Bhandarkar** held the opposite – Andhras and Satavahanas were the same people.
[5:4] - **Distractors:** V.V. Mirashi, S.A. Joglekar, P.T. Srinivasa Iyengar, V.A. Smith, Rapson, Gopalachari.
[5:5] TABLE head: Scholar | View
  [5:5.0] V.V. Mirashi | Home in Vidarbha (Berar)
  [5:5.1] E.J. Rapson, R.G. Bhandarkar | Origin in eastern Deccan (Andhra), from Puranic "Andhra" names
  [5:5.2] Gopalachari | Rejected Bellary; favoured Maharashtra
  [5:5.3] D.C. Sircar | Andhras, not Andhrabhrityas
  [5:5.4] K.A. Nilakanta Sastri | Andhra = tribal, Satavahana = dynastic, Satakarni = family name
  [5:5.5] B.S.L. Hanumantha Rao | Belonged to the original Andhra janapada
  [5:5.6] R.S. Sharma | Dravidians

## SUB 6 | Capitals
[6:0] - **Principal capital / main town:** **Pratishthana (Paithan)** on the **Godavari**, Aurangabad district, Maharashtra (Welfare Officers; UP Lower 2008; AMVI 2009; APPSC PYQ).
[6:1] - **Andhra capital:** **Dhanyakataka** (Dharanikota, near Amaravati, on the Krishna) (APPSC PYQ); distractors Vengi (E. Chalukyas), Warangal (Kakatiyas), Kondapur.
[6:2] - **Shift east:** after Rudradaman's attacks **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** moved the capital to **Amaravati (Dhanyakataka)**.
[6:3] - **Current link:** **Amaravati**, the designated capital of Andhra Pradesh today, was the historical Satavahana capital (APPSC AEE).
[6:4] - **Only-option rule:** if Paithan is absent, mark **Amaravati** (UPPCS Mains 2005; distractors Nanded, Naldurg, Durg).
[6:5] - **First capital – debated:** Srikakulam, Krishna dt (Barnett, V.A. Smith, Burgess); Pratishthana (Mirashi, Raychaudhuri); Dhanyakataka (Bhandarkar).
[6:6] - **⚠ Trap:** Taxila, Kanchi, Pataliputra, Arikamedu, Nagarjunakonda (Ikshvaku capital Vijayapuri) are not Satavahana capitals.
[6:7] - **Other centres:** Kotilingala (Karimnagar) – claimed early centre; Mahishmati – capital of Anupa.
[6:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.5, p.10; Rocket AP History p.42, p.54; app notes 1:46 (sub 9)
## SUB 7 | Sources – Puranas, Literature, Foreign Accounts
[7:0] # Puranas
[7:1] - **Main Purana:** the **Matsya Purana** – genealogy and reign of each king; Satavahana political history rests on it (Welfare Officers).
[7:2] - **Matsya figures:** **30 kings, 456 years** (271 BC – 174 AD); kings 1-23 = early, 24-30 = later Satavahanas.
[7:3] - **Compiled under:** **Yajnasri Satakarni** (APPSC PYQ).
[7:4] - **Vayu / Vishnu:** 17 kings, 272 years – not treated as standard.
[7:5] - **⚠ Trap:** the **Vishnudharmottara Purana** (Chitrasutra) is the "sourcebook of Indian painting", not a Satavahana source (Animal Husbandry GS).
[7:6] # Literature
[7:7] - **Early Satavahana history:** told in **Kathasaritsagara, Pratishthanapura-kalpa, Dvatrimsatputtalika**.
[7:8] - **⚠ Trap:** the **Gathasaptasati** does **not** narrate early Satavahana history (G2 2017; APPSC PYQ).
[7:9] # Kharavela
[7:10] - **Contemporary:** Kharavela of Kalinga was the contemporary of **Satakarni I** (G2-B 1989); the Hathigumpha inscription and the Chulakalinga Jataka record the contact.
[7:11] - **⚠ Sources differ:** NextGen says Kharavela "attacked and defeated" Satakarni I.
[7:12]   - Settled: Hathigumpha line 4 says he sent an army west "disregarding Satakarni", to the Kanhabenna; no defeat is recorded (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hathigumpha_inscription).
[7:13] # Foreign accounts
[7:14] TABLE head: Writer | Work / fact
  [7:14.0] Megasthenes | Indica; first foreign source on the Andhras
  [7:14.1] Pliny | Natural History (Latin); Roman gold drained to India
  [7:14.2] Periplus (anonymous Greek) | Saka–Satavahana conflict; Koddura the only east-coast port; Arikamedu
  [7:14.3] Ptolemy | Geography; Pulumavi vs Chashtana; Maisolia a great market
[7:15] - **⚠ Sources differ:** NextGen: Megasthenes gives the Andhras "30 forts and one lakh infantry"; app notes: Pliny's army figures.
[7:16]   - Settled: Pliny quoting Megasthenes – **30 walled towns, 1,00,000 foot, 2,000 horse, 1,000 elephants** (https://www.philipharland.com/Blog/2023/06/indians-taprobanians-and-serians-pliny-the-elder-on-peoples-and-customs-in-india-and-beyond-first-century-ce/)
[7:17] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.3-5, p.7; app notes 1:46 (sub 2); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hathigumpha_inscription
