## SUB 0 | Acharya Nagarjuna
[0:0] - **Who:** a **Buddhist philosopher** (AEE 2008) – not a Greek king, Vedic rishi or Jain monk; spread **Mahayana** Buddhism in Andhra.
[0:1] - **Titles:** **"Second Buddha"** (Junior Analyst 2011), Indian Martin Luther, Indian Einstein.
[0:2] - **Philosophy:** founder of the **Madhyamika** school; propounded **Sunyavada** (emptiness) (G1 2012; APPSC PYQ).
[0:3] - **⚠ Trap:** not Advaita, Mahadvaita or Visishtadvaita; **Syadvada** = Kundakundacharya; Asvaghosha and Somadeva Suri are distractors.
[0:4] TABLE head: Work | Fact
  [0:4.0] Suhrullekha ("friendly letter") | Letter to Yajnasri Satakarni; Itsing saw students memorise it
  [0:4.1] Prajnaparamita Shastra | Foundation text of Mahayana
  [0:4.2] Ratnavali | Advice to a Satavahana king; Rajaparikatha chapter = Shreyorajya (welfare state)
  [0:4.3] Yogasara | Credited to Nagarjuna (G2 2017 key)
  [0:4.4] Mulamadhyamakakarika | Verse treatise on Madhyamika
[0:5] - **Suhrullekha and Prajnaparamita:** both by **Acharya Nagarjuna** (Sachivalayam 2019); not Aryadeva, Dharmanandi, Chandramukha.
[0:6] - **Welfare-state doctrine:** set out by Nagarjuna in the **Ratnavali** (Welfare Officers).
[0:7] - **⚠ Trap:** NextGen merges Ratnavali with "Rasaratnavali/Rasaratnakara" (alchemy); exams mean the Ratnavali of advice to the king, and Harsha's play Ratnavali is a different work (https://encyclopediaofbuddhism.org/wiki/Ratnavali).
[0:8] - **Yogasara:** **Nagarjuna** (G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ) – not Dhanapala, Vastupala or Kondakundacharya.
[0:9] - **Life:** contemporary and friend of **Yajnasri Satakarni**; lived at **Sriparvata** (Nagarjunakonda) in the 1,500-room vihara the king built; disciple **Aryadeva**.
[0:10] - **Works in stone:** built the railing (rampart) of the Amaravati Mahachaitya and stone mandapas at Sriparvata.
[0:11] - **Death legend:** Kathasaritsagara says a Satavahana prince had him killed.
[0:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.2, p.24, p.29-31; Rocket AP History p.32, p.52; app notes 1:49 (sub 6); https://encyclopediaofbuddhism.org/wiki/Ratnavali
## SUB 1 | Kings Vedic, Queens Buddhist – Patronage
[1:0] - **Royal religion:** the Satavahana kings followed the **Vedic religion** (APPSC PYQ); their **queens patronised Buddhism**.
[1:1] > Statement angle (G2 2008): "rulers followed Hinduism but some queens patronised Buddhism" ✓; fourfold caste ✓; maritime trade flourished ✓; "women were looked down upon" ✗ (the false one).
[1:2] - **Yajnasri Satakarni's faith:** personally Vedic (the name shows it); with no Vedic option, mark **Buddhism** – he was Nagarjuna's friend and patron (APPSC PYQ).
[1:3] - **Ikshvakus:** Vedic themselves but **great supporters of Buddhism** (Social & Cultural History); Virapurushadatta's reign = golden age of Buddhism.
[1:4] > Statement angle (Welfare Officers): Satavahanas and Ikshvakus gave to Hindu (Brahmanical) cults and to Buddhist monks ✓; both claim Shrauta (Vedic) sacrifices ✓; "Satavahanas gave only to Hindu temples" ✗ → "1 and 2".
[1:5] - **Donors:** queens, merchants and common people; **Gautami Balasri** gave the Nasik cave vihara to the **Bhadrayaniya** sect; Pulomavi II gave a village to Mahasanghika monks at Karle.
[1:6] - **Guild support:** guilds at Nasik, Junnar and Kanheri held money on deposit and paid the interest to monks.

## SUB 2 | Gudimallam and Shaivism
[2:0] - **Oldest Shiva temple:** **Parasurameswara temple, Gudimallam** (Yerpedu mandal; Chittoor, now Tirupati district) (G2 2008; APPSC PYQ).
[2:1] - **⚠ Trap:** Thondawada, Renigunta, Jogulamba (Alampur) are distractors.
[2:2] - **Oldest Shiva linga of AP and India:** **Gudimallam** – Shiva carved standing on the linga as a hunter (G1 1991; APPSC PYQ); not Amaravati, Draksharama, Srisailam, Nagarjunakonda, Hanumakonda.
[2:3] - **⚠ Outdated:** Gudimallam's district "Chittoor" — now **Tirupati district** (2022 AP district reorganisation).
[2:4] - **Other Shaiva evidence:** 14 early Shiva temples at **Veerapuram** (Kurnool); Gathasaptasati opens with a Shiva prayer; king names Sivasri, Shivaskandha.
[2:5] - **Pashupata:** first branch of Shaivism, founder **Lakulisha**.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.23, p.26-27; Rocket AP History p.34; app notes 1:49 (sub 2)
## SUB 3 | Buddhist Sects – Rajagirika, Sri Parvataswamy
[3:0] - **Rajagirika centre:** **Guntupalli** (G2 2012 key) – not Gudivada, Sriparvatam or Jaggayyapeta.
[3:1] - **Sri Parvataswamy = the Buddha:** identification by **N. Venkataramanayya** (G2 2019 key; APPSC PYQ).
[3:2] - **⚠ Trap:** **Kielhorn** identified Sri Parvataswamy with Mallikarjuna of Srisailam; another key named Kielhorn – follow G2 2019.
[3:3] - **First Buddhist acharya in Andhra:** **Mahadeva Bhikshu**, founder of **Chaityakavada** (worship of chaityas).
[3:4] - **Mahasanghika:** main centre **Dhanyakataka**; followers called Mahasanghis or Chaityakas. **Bhadrayaniya** sect: Nasik.
[3:5] - **Arrival:** Buddhism reached Andhra in the **6th century BC**; "Andhra Buddhism" held the **Pratitya Samutpada** doctrine; Tibetan tradition – the Buddha preached at Amaravati at king Subendran's request.
[3:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.28-29; Rocket AP History p.8; app notes 1:49 (sub 3)
## SUB 4 | Jainism – Kundakundacharya
[4:0] - **Samayasara:** by **Kundakundacharya (Kondakundacharya)**, a Digambara monk (G2 2019; APPSC PYQ); not Somadeva Suri, Mahaviracharya or Pujyapada.
[4:1] - **Philosophy:** **Syadvada** and Anekantavada; other works Pravachanasara, Panchastikayasara, Niyamasara.
[4:2] - **Ashram:** **Konakondla** (Anantapur district), where he practised yoga in a cave.
[4:3] - **Jain tradition on the Satavahanas:** Simuka was first a Jain (guru Kalakasuri); Ashoka's grandson **Samprati** spread Jainism in Andhra; Guntupalli was first a Jain site.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.27-28; Rocket AP History p.46, p.52; app notes 1:49 (sub 7)
## SUB 5 | Vedic Sacrifices and Bhagavata Religion
[5:0] - **Naneghat (Devi Naganika):** Satakarni I performed **2 Ashvamedhas** and a **Rajasuya**; gave cows, land and gold to Brahmanas.
[5:1] - **Deities invoked at Naneghat:** Indra, Vasudeva, Sankarshana, Surya, Chandra, Yama, Varuna (NextGen p.24 also lists Agni).
[5:2] - **Yajna-performing kings:** Satakarni I, Satakarni II, Gautamiputra, Yajnasri; the names **Vedasri** and **Yajnasri** show Vedic faith.
[5:3] > A-R angle (Welfare Officers): "Satavahanas performed the Ashvamedha to legitimise power" ✓; "they patronised both Brahmanas and Buddhists" ✓ – but R does not explain A.
[5:4] - **Bhagavata religion:** founded on Krishna-Vasudeva (a Vrishni of Mathura; disciple of **Ghor Angirasa** in the Chhandogya Upanishad); entered Andhra under **Kanha (Krishna)**, the 2nd king; called the "Vasudeva religion" by Hala's time.
[5:5] - **Symbols:** Bhagavata symbols on Gautamiputra's inscriptions; Nasik calls him **Agama-nilaya**.
[5:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.7-8, p.24-25; app notes 1:49 (sub 1, 2)
