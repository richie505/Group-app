Patterns:
- **Exact repeats:** G2 2008 reused the same stems twice (Kavivatsala ×2, Rishabhadatta interest ×2, Sukthankar ×2, Nasik victories); G2 2017 asked Kodavali three times across papers; G1 2023 set the same match twice.
- **Group 2's favourites:** guilds and interest (10 of the 15 guild PYQs are G2), Sukthankar, Kodavali, Anupa, Gathasaptasati.
- **Group 1's favourites:** authors of texts (Katantra, Samayasara, Prajnaparamita, Lilavati, Harshacharita wrong pair) and one-off concept questions (Kataka/Skandhavara 2024).
- **Non-APPSC papers** (UPSC, UPPSC, NDA, CDS) supply nearly all the stupa/chaitya/vihara definitions, Karle and Guntupalli rock-cut statements and the metronymic dynasty question.
- **Traps the setters reuse:** Naneghat vs Nasik; Gautami Balasri vs Naganika; Dakshinapathapati vs Dakshinapatheshwara; Gramika vs Gulmika; Brihatkatha "in Sanskrit"; Motupalli as a Satavahana port; iron-copper coins (Ikshvaku); Guntupalli vs Gudivada.
