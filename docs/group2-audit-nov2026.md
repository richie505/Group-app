## 3. The PYQs that were removed: really out of the syllabus?

4,797 MCQs had been removed as outside the 2023 Group 2 syllabus, 4,100 of them PYQs.
- 2,284 are school general science (physics, chemistry, biology). The 2023 Group 2 syllabus has no general science
  line, so they stay out.
- The other 1,816 were checked again, independently:
  - **1,718 confirmed out**: computer basics, school science, psychology and pedagogy, research methods and
    statistics, sociology theory, world history and world agriculture, capital-market trivia, Rajput / Pala /
    Rashtrakuta / Sangam history (not in the Group 2 lines), department rules (AP Endowments Act).
  - **56 fitted a line after all and were restored** (e.g. Haryanka and the republics as background to Buddhism and
    Jainism; AP merchant guilds and temples; Kushans, Alexander and Pallava ghatikas from old Group 2 papers;
    national research institutes). 41 of them came back (the rest were already back by another route).
  - **42 old news items stay out** (Science Congress venues 2001-2019, Miss Universe 2019): old news is not a current
    event.
- 117 of the removed PYQs came from Group 2's own old papers (2008, 2012, Mains 2017/2019). They test what the 2023
  syllabus dropped (research methods, psychology, general science); a few that fit were restored.

## 4. MCQ quality checks

- **Duplicates:** 104 notes MCQs that were exact copies (same question, same options) were dropped. 1,237
  past questions repeat across exams (the same question asked by different commissions); they are kept, as each is
  a real PYQ.
- **Repeated options:** 8 PYQs had an option printed twice in the paper; the copy was replaced with a plausible wrong
  option, the key unchanged.
- **Statement sets** list every statement in their options (only 4 left that could not be rebuilt).
- **No key / cancelled:** 117 PYQs (official keys missing or questions cancelled) stay as answer-only cards.
- Every MCQ has a valid key; every notes MCQ has an explanation.
